Revelations

Black Ops 3 · The end of the Aether story

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4 · 2–2½ hours.
  • The upgraded Apothicon Servant.
  • The Keeper Protector altar + ~5500 points.
  • Lil' Arnies from the box, and ideally a Max Ammo gobblegum.
  • Pack-a-Punch open.

"For The Good Of All" — the last stand of the Aether story: put the Shadow Man away for good.

The full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Shoot the graves in death order. Doable from the very start: four graves by spawn (two in spawn, two near the Origins bumper — shootable through the door). Shoot them in the order the characters died: Richtofen, Dempsey, Takeo, Nikolai — left to right that's 2, 3, 1, 4. Red flames confirm.
  2. The Keeper ritual — audio reel 1. A small pyramidal stone hides on one of the bumper pads (random). Use a pad, and while it recharges stand on it and look for the stone — it's easy to miss. Then summon the Keeper Protector from the OPPOSITE pad's side, bounce over, lure him onto the pad — close enough to the stone and he starts chanting. Every enemy on the map now hunts him — protect him until he finishes and drops the reel. Play it on the reader in Nacht, beside Jugger-Nog.
    You'll need the Keeper altar built and ~5500 points.
  3. Nine Arnies in the Apothicon — audio reel 2. Inside the beast, throw Lil' Arnies into 9 holes without leaving — leave, and every hole resets. Each set of 3 spawns 3 Margwas; kill them before the next set (they usually drop Max Ammos). Solo: throw 3, pop a Max Ammo gobblegum, throw 3, wait for a Margwa Max Ammo for the last 3. Then take the reel and play it in Kino — the reader is on a box by the main desk, opposite the teleporter.
  4. Catch the 6 bones. Each starts as a brick you shoot down with a PaP'd weapon — a flying bone pops out, and you catch it by firing the upgraded Apothicon Servant near it.
  5. Sophia's remains — audio reel 3. The bones pile up in Nacht between the reel reader and Jugger-Nog. Shoot them with the upgraded Servant, then shoot the corpse that appears — reel 3 drops. Play it on the table at the Origins Excavation Site.
  6. Reflect the turret beams. S.O.P.H.I.A. hovers above Nacht. Use all 4 corruption turrets — but you can't hit her directly: aim at the blue crystal left of each portal to bank the beam into her. The turret kicks you out once the beam locks. Then interact with her in Nacht.
  7. Samantha's room — the Kronorium. All players in the Kino teleporter. Grab the Kronorium from her bed, wait for the return trip, and set it on the Kino desk.
    Doing the wisp? Pick it up right before this teleport and place it on the toy chest while you're in the room.
  8. Four eggs, four runes. One egg exists at a time, at semi-random spots (spawn by Quick Revive · Origins by Gen 3 / Wunderfizz / Keeper altar corner / Buried KN-44 · MotD by the turret · Shangri-La broken pillar / fire square · DE Wunderfizz bucket / Primis statues · Kino blackboard / broken floor · Verrückt green tanks / Speed Cola / plants / chair). Put each egg in one of the 4 holes in the Apothicon, kill zombies nearby to hatch it into a worm, then carry the worm to its area (spawn, DE, Verrückt, MotD) and find the invisible rune spot — a beep speeds up as you get close. Place, wait, take the rune. Repeat ×4.
  9. The rune code and the gauntlet. Everyone on the circle in Kino's projection room → teleported to the empty boss room. Interact with the Kronorium on its stand — the pages show your 4 runes in a specific order (remember it by scoreboard positions). Across the room, a purple symbol cycles — interact each time YOUR next rune shows. Then survive 4 arena phases: Margwa packs plus lava, Furies and tornados; in the anti-gravity phase, stand on the light circles or die. Take the Summoning Key.
  10. Throw the Key at 7 relics outside the map:
    Lost the Key? A miss drops it near where you threw. If it glitches in a portal, the carrier downs himself and it reappears — or grab a Death Machine and swap back.
  11. The finale. Gear up — then take Kino's teleporter to the boss room. Key on the pedestal, kill zombies to charge it, throw it at S.O.P.H.I.A. — she strips the Shadow Man's shield. Shoot him until he's driven into the Apothicon statue's mouth, then interact with the Kronorium. Enjoy the XP — and if you've done every quest before this one, the RK5 starts every game with you from now on.

Side Easter Eggs

Seven of them — including the wisp, the best reward in the game.

Free perk for everyone
An anti-gravity parkour

Activate anti-gravity and the corruption generator in Der Eisendrache, look above the portal: a perk bottle floats out there. Wall-run out along the walls to grab it — it gives a random perk to every player. Falling into space just respawns you on the map.

Secret song — "The Gift"
3 teddy bears
The jukebox radio
4 buttons, any zombies song

Press 4 buttons in Nacht quickly (less time with more players) and a radio spawns by the box — shoot it to play any zombies song, knife to stop.

The wisp
Every box weapon comes out PaP'd

Requirements

  • At least one player has completed ALL the other main easter eggs.

A shining wisp sits on the spawn truck. One enemy hit while carrying it loses it for the whole game — so leave it until main quest step 7, grab it right before the Samantha's-room teleport, and place it on her toy chest.

Reward: every Mystery Box weapon comes out already Pack-a-Punched, and Takeo's katana becomes buyable in Shangri-La.

The chalk quest

Unlocks a table in Nacht where players can leave a weapon for each other — and an M1927 wall-buy in DE.

  1. Grab the pink chalk in Der Eisendrache (needs anti-gravity) — on the small upper platform.
  2. Move the wall sentences: take "Wish Too Often..." from DE → place it in Verrückt on the "Ascend from Darkness" floor spot by the power → place "Ascend from Darkness" in Nacht's spiral staircase → place "Salvation Lies Above" on Nacht's empty wall by the back staircase.
  3. The Kino swap: take "Knowledge Itself Is For The Taking" from MotD's infirmary entrance → place it in Kino behind the teleporter (where the lever was) → carry the "A Soul Alone..." you got in exchange back to the MotD spot.
  4. A sound plays; the table in Nacht glimmers. Weapons in, weapons out.
Upgraded Lil' Arnies
150 kills + one good throw

The lobby collectively kills 150 zombies with Arnies, then throw one into the window above the church at spawn. Everyone holding Arnies gets them upgraded, ammo refunded.

Wonder Weapons

The Apothicon Servant, the Thundergun and Lil' Arnies all come from the Mystery Box on this map. The one with a quest is the Servant's upgrade:

Estulla Astoth — upgraded Apothicon Servant
5 flying 115 stones, then PaP

The Servant comes from the Mystery Box. The upgrade Shadows of Evil never let you have: shoot 5 element-115 stones flying in the sky, then Pack-a-Punch it inside the Apothicon. Required for the main quest (catching bones, freeing Sophia).

Pack-a-Punch

An Apothicon ate it. You're going in after it.

  1. Activate the 4 ritual altars — pay 500 and hold the area until the ritual ends (the points come back). Each opens a portal toward Nacht.
  2. Use the console in Nacht upstairs when it flashes — roughly when the Apothicon swings in front — then jump into its mouth through the opened door.
  3. Free the machine: inside the stomach, shoot the three glowing balls on its side.
