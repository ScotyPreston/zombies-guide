Blood of the Dead

Black Ops 4 · Alcatraz, again — the hardest one

Map

The island layout. Tap it to open it full screen, then use − and + to zoom in on an area.

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4. Normal difficulty, no modifiers — casual locks you out.
  • Both power switches on, Pack-a-Punch open.
  • The Hell's Retriever and the Spoon.
  • The Spectral Shield — ideally upgraded to the Attuned shield.

"Most Escape Alive" — the reference calls this the hardest quest in Zombies history. Expect a long game.

The full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Scratch the Warden's wall. Upstairs in the Warden's House, melee the wall with the Spoon — scratch marks appear all over it.
  2. Free Monkey Bombs. Down the stairs from the Cell Block 2nd Floor wolf head, a small monkey statue sits outside the map. Charge your specialist weapon to level 2, bring a big horde to the statue and use it there — red souls fly into the monkey until it flies off. The Monkey Bombs then wait on a table in Richtofen's Laboratory.
  3. Open the ritual chamber. Shield-blast the Citadel Tunnels code box and enter 666 — a Warden spawns. Lead him upstairs, throw a Monkey Bomb by the scratched wall, and his slam opens a hole. Inside, hold use on the electric chair: the room tears itself apart, the crew talks, then grab the glowing red orb from the table before you leave.
  4. The orb, the book, the bird. Place the orb on the map in Richtofen's Lab. Turn around: a glowing blue book, the Kronorium, is on the floor. Hold use — white flash — and a ghostly bird steals it.
  5. Hunt the bird — three times. Shield out, hold the spirit vision and scan the map: birds chirping = you're close. When you see it, shield-blast it. There are 40 possible spots (the reference points to Glitch's YouTube video for all of them), and only one bird per round.
  6. The fourth bird — Zombie Blood. The last bird sits at one of the video's "Zombie Blood" spots and is invisible: you know you're facing it when you see a glint of light and hear a man crying. Blast the code box and enter 872 for a Zombie Blood power-up, grab it, run to the spot, confirm in spirit vision (don't stare or it flies off) and throw the Hell's Retriever at its feet. The Kronorium drops nearby. Fail = next round.
  7. Start a trial. Place the Kronorium on the corpse in the electric chair; hold use to stop the pages, then read the three-digit number through the shield's spirit vision. Blast the code box and enter it: the lighthouse beam points at a portal — find it in spirit vision and blast the portal. Five trials, random order, one per round; fail one and the book gives a new code next round.
    No beam? Michigan Avenue's trial shows no beam — just look for the portal there.

    The five trials (each ends with a red orb):

    Docks trial
    Morse code + a ghost escort
    1. Blast the Docks portal. Solo: enter 872 for a Zombie Blood first (Temporal Gift elixir before grabbing it); co-op: one player holds a zombie.
    2. In the Warden's Office, behind the locked gate (the Acid Gat part cage), is a morse machine. Hold use briefly = dot, hold 2–3 s = dash, a second between entries. The Warden laughs when an entry is wrong — so try dashes until he laughs, write the code down as you go; your character confirms when it's done.
    3. Go to the Infirmary room with the skeleton on the operating table, kill a zombie there, and find the ghost in spirit vision. Blast it, hold use on it, then feed it souls until it follows you.
    4. Walk it to the gondola, ride down with it, and lead it to the Docks portal — it leaves the orb.
    Showers trial
    The banjo — the frustrating one
    1. Blast the Showers portal. A ghost offers a banjo — hold use to take it.
    2. Stand in the blue circle (outside it you take damage) and get kills — the zombies don't have to be in it. The circle moves after a while; keep following it until the banjo stops taking souls.
    3. Co-op: every player has to take the banjo and do this. A banjo strum = done; hand it back to the ghost for the orb.
    Power House trial
    Simon says, symbols, a helpless ghost
    1. Blast the Power House portal, then run to Building 64 at the Docks: a smoking, stopped machine. Hold use to start Simon says — a power box lights, you confirm it, then two, up to five.
    2. At the end three panels keep flashing — photograph the symbols on the paper under them. Before leaving, grab the punch card on the shelf between the two archways to the Citadel.
    3. In Model Industries, hold use on the machine: screens light up. Find your three symbols on the screens, hold use on each — you get a new symbol each time. Photograph those too.
    4. In the Power House, a ghost wanders trying switches and failing. Find the three switches with your new symbols under them, and blast the ghost as he heads for each one so he can pull it. Three pulls = orb.
    Michigan Avenue trial
    Protect the ghost across the map
    Stock up first: ammo, a specialist charge, Monkey Bombs, the Magmagat, Undead Man Walking if anyone has it.
    1. Blast the Michigan Avenue portal, then go to the Cafeteria (down the stairs from the Infirmary), kill a zombie and find the ghost in spirit vision. Blast it so everyone can see it.
    2. Escort him to the portal. Magmagat fire under his feet, Monkey Bombs to pull the crowd, specialist for the hordes — but specialist weapons hurt him, so aim away. He drops the orb at the portal.
    New Industries trial
    Drain and blast the killer ghost ×5
    Go in with max shield charges — four with the Attuned shield.
    1. Blast the New Industries portal, then kill a zombie in D-Block: a hostile ghost appears in spirit vision, heading off to murder a worker ghost in New Industries. Blast him visible.
    2. Drain him with the key attack until he vanishes, find him again in spirit vision, blast him — five times in all. He turns redder each time (solo: keep zombies alive to refill charges).
    3. When he's almost fully red and changes posture, run ahead to New Industries and wait by the doorway. As he comes through, trigger the spinning chain trap. Collect the orb once it stops.
  8. Five orbs on the map, then the key. Place all five orbs on the lab map. Go up with PaP'd Magmagat / M1927 / Hades / MOG 12, all perks and a full shield. Hold use on the Kronorium in the chamber: the Summoning Key goes into the corpse, the Warden knocks you out, and you wake in a cell.
  9. Out of the cells. After the Warden's speech the bird opens your cell. Take your gear from the bag and refill the shield at the bench if you built it there. Follow the bird through the prison and the Recreation Yard back to Richtofen's Lab — dogs and Wardens the whole way. Outside Model Industries a giant Warden takes no damage until the ghosts and the bird lift him away; grab the last orb, place it on the map, and a door on the right opens.
  10. The boss. Up the stairs to the garage door: everyone stands on an unlit light and holds use.
    • Waves of dogs, zombies and Wardens. When the giant Warden appears in a red circle with three orbs over his head, get in the circle and shoot the orbs.
    • Circle gone → turn around and shield-blast the orb on top of the central machine, twice. Do the whole thing twice.
    • Richtofen curses the machine — whoever is Richtofen holds use to enter it (solo, a bot does it). Everyone else runs from an invincible Warden.
    • Richtofen returns in knight's armour with the Fire Staff crystal; he holds use at the arena to strip the giant Warden's immunity. Kill him. Cutscene — "escape".

Side Easter Eggs & achievements

The reference is an achievement walkthrough, so this tab is its achievement list. The songs and jump scares aren't covered — send a reference and I'll add them.

Round 20 on the West side
Never open the Catwalk

Reach round 20 without opening the Catwalk. You keep the whole West side and one perk. Counts on casual with three bots. Hit the box for a Blundergat plus a wall rifle (VAPR), open every door, and run the loop: courtyard outside Richtofen's Lab → Model Industries → tunnel to the Power House → stairs back up. Keep moving so nothing spawns in front of you.

Shield revive
Revive a player with a blast

Bank one blast (key-drain three zombies), then hold spirit vision on a downed player and fire the blast at them.

Gondola ride with the Warden
Best done solo

Solo gives you two free self-revives. Flip the Docks power switch — a Warden spawns — call the gondola down and survive until its green lights and bell. With 750 points, board with him and start the ride. He'll probably down you; it still counts when the gondola reaches the cell block.

Retriever + Spoon + Blundergat + Tommy Gun
All four at once

Normal difficulty (the Spoon doesn't exist on casual) and no attachments or death effects on the Tommy Gun or it won't pop. The Tommy Gun is a 1,500 wall-buy at the Docks, in the corner under the "Dock" sign. Blundergat from the box or the free quest.

Feed the wolves from afar
Gondola, Citadel and Model Industries — buggy

Feed a wolf head from each of three places in one game. Do it co-op with a sniper/tactical rifle (ABR, Paladin, Koshka, Essex) — a friend trains zombies by the head, you kill one from the spot. Claymores by the head, triggered by a led zombie, also work. Buggy: may need a second try.

  1. Model Industries (the room above Richtofen's Lab): shoot into the New Industries wolf-head room from here.
  2. Citadel: under the Cell Block 2nd Floor head, look up through the grated floor and shoot through it.
  3. Gondola: ride from the Docks toward the prison and shoot into the Eagle's Plaza alley before it stops.
Warden with all three gats
Blundergat, Magmagat, Acidgat in one game

Kill a Warden with the plain Blundergat, upgrade to the Magmagat and kill the next, then apply the Acid Gat kit and kill a third.

Max Redeemer kills
Buggy — keep throwing

With the Hell's Redeemer, charge it (three blue rings pulse under you) and throw it into a big horde; an Insta-Kill helps. Reports say aim at their feet, or pause before it returns. Repeat until it pops.

Wonder Weapons

Mob of the Dead's arsenal, rebuilt: the Hell's Retriever and its upgrade, the Blundergat and its two elements, and the Spoon. The Retriever comes first — most of the rest needs it.

The Sweeper
Pack-a-Punched Blundergat, no element

The plain PaP upgrade — the reference's pick alongside a PaP'd Magmagat for the quest.

Acidgat
Blundergat + the Acid Gat Kit

Build the Acid Gat Kit (three parts, two behind Warden's-key locks) and apply it to a Blundergat or Magmagat.

Magmagat
The fireplace, 15–20 souls, and a run
  1. Hold use with the Blundergat (or Acidgat) out at the Warden's House fireplace to place it. You're locked in.
  2. Kill zombies and pick up the blue orbs they leave — 15–20 souls — and place them at the fireplace. Take back the Tempered Blundergat.
  3. Run it to New Industries before its blue flame dies, passing the fire barrels along the way to re-light it. Best at round's end with one zombie left — you have to cross the Catwalk.
  4. Hold use at the big machine in New Industries; a moment later, take back your Magmagat.
The Spoon
Normal difficulty only — needs the Retriever + Warden's key
  1. With the Warden's key, unlock the code box behind the gate in the Citadel Tunnels by the Docks.
  2. In the Warden's Office, stand here and hold spirit vision: three numbers on the pillars. They change every game.
  3. Back at the box, shield-blast it, then tap each digit to enter the code. A cage drops into the water with the skeleton holding the Spoon.
  4. At the Docks, blast the power box by the water, look up: a crane swings a fishing net with the skeleton in it. Throw the Retriever at the skeleton when it's right over the box on the dock — the Spoon drops for everyone.

The Golden Spork / Scalpel upgrades aren't in the reference — send one and I'll add them.

Buildables

Spectral Shield
Key + Essence + Grate — the map's Swiss-army knife
  1. Key — dropped by the first Warden you kill (one can spawn on the Catwalk run; one is guaranteed once both power switches are on).
  2. Spectral Essence — on one of the three red Afterlife boxes:
  3. Grate — in or near the Citadel:
  4. Build it on the Cell Block 3rd Floor table — the reference's pick, and you can refill it there during the quest's escape.
How it works: shield out, hold fire = key attack (4–5 s per zombie; 3 drains = 1 blast). Hold aim = spirit vision; aim + fire = spirit blast. Blasts power boxes, start trials, revive teammates. It's needed for Pack-a-Punch, the Redeemer, the Spoon and the whole quest.
Attuned Spectral Shield
Upgrade: drain the Mystery Box lock

Requirements

  • The shield and the Hell's Retriever.
  1. Everyone by the box. Spin it, and while it's moving (a lock shows instead of a weapon) key-drain the lock.
  2. When the keyhole turns blue, throw the Retriever at the lock — everyone at once so each player gets one. The shield becomes the Attuned Spectral Shield: four blast charges and more durability.
Acid Gat Kit
3 parts — two need the Warden's key
  1. Case — on the ground in the Transverse Tunnel (New Industries to the Power House).
  2. Motor — Warden's Office, in the locked cage with the orange lock; open it with the Warden's key.
  3. Third part — Infirmary, on the way to the roof, in the locked cabinet behind the Soda perk's wall; Warden's key again.
  4. Build at any free table — the reference uses the one by the Transverse Tunnel. Then apply it to a Blundergat.

Pack-a-Punch

Two power switches, a sprint across the Catwalk, and the shield does the rest. The machine moves between three spots.

  1. Power, West side — the switch inside the Power House.
  2. Open the Catwalk. With that switch on, hold use at the door under the "Catwalk" sign. It's an endurance run: zombies, hellhounds, maybe a Warden — use your specialist and keep pushing forward until you come out in the old Mob of the Dead spawn room.
  3. Power, Docks — the second switch inside Building 64. Both on = a Warden spawns; kill him for the key if you don't have it yet.
  4. Blast the roof box. Build the shield, then go through the Infirmary to the Roof. Key-drain three zombies for a blast, hold spirit vision, and fire it at the power box beside Pack-a-Punch.
The machine rotates between the Power House near spawn, the Docks power room and the Roof.

Gondola

Between the prison and the Docks, once both switches are on. Boards at the Docks Bridge and the Cell Block 3rd Floor.

Perk machines

Four machines, fixed spots. They're labelled by slot — Brew, Cola, Soda, Tonic — and each sells whatever perk you put in that loadout slot before the game.

The reference's loadout for the quest: Quick Revive (Brew), Stamin-Up (Cola), Dying Wish (Soda), Deadshot Dealer (Tonic). Elixirs: Undead Man Walking, Nowhere But There in co-op, Temporal Gift solo.
