The Trials
3 per player — required for the quest

The four pillars by the main altar each show 3 glowing symbols — three challenges per player. Yellow symbols are yours, blue are your teammates'. Look at one to read the challenge and track progress; they change every game, but the bottom one is always easy, the middle medium, the top hard. Rewards, bottom to top: Max Ammo, an SVG sniper or Brecci shotgun, and an extra perk slot — interact with a finished challenge to claim.

Easy pool: breathe from a spore underwater · destroy 5 spores · cut 5 spider webs · water 5 plants · water one plant three times.

Medium pool: 20 underwater kills · 15 kills on zombies lured by a carnivorous plant · 10 headshots on spore-infected zombies · get a power-up from a plant · 20 kills with the fan trap · kill a Thrasher before it enrages.

Hard pool: kill 8 spiders with spore gas · headshot 5 Thrashers · eat a fruit from a plant · 30 kills with the plane trap · kill 3 zombies mid-mutation (burst them before the roots drag them under).

"Seeds of Doubt" — the quest: 3 gears, 1 elevator

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Grow a shell and load the AA gun. Start early: plant seeds, water them three times with blue water, and shoot the seed with the KT-4/Masamune after each watering to fertilise (green particles = it took). You want a bonus plant holding an artillery shell — the more seeds you run, the sooner it comes. Take the shell to the AA gun on the bunker roof (first door right of the entrance) and load it — listen for the clunk, and don't spam the button. When the massive plane flies into the gun's sights, fire. It drops a smoking gear near the green water, in front of the bunker, or by Lab A's zipline — depends where you hit it.
  2. The zipline drop — gear 2. Electrify your shield (all trials done, stand by the altar), then head to Lab A's zipline. The gear sits on a deck you can only reach by falling off the zipline mid-ride.
    Solo: melee the zipline console with the electrified shield, ride, and press melee over the deck — you drop dead-vertical, no drift.
    Co-op: player A rides, player B melees the console with the electrified shield while A is over the deck — hit it early and A lands in the water, out of the map, game over for him. Don't pre-shoot it.
    Grow cloning plants before attempting this (3 colours + KT-4 fertilise) — a saved state takes the sting out of a missed drop.
  3. "Anywhere But Here!" — gear 3. Chew the gum: it teleports you into an unreachable room above Lab A where the third gear is just lying on the floor by some large eggs. Works any time.
  4. Fix the elevator. Take all three gears to the elevator left of the bunker entrance (the path your skull opened) and place them.
  5. The boss — swampy Takeo.
    No way back once you ride down — go in with Pack-a-Punched guns, perks, Masamune, the gas mask and your gums.
    Everyone in the elevator, press the button. At the bottom, shoot the roots with the Masamune to clear the way, pass the final door, and there he is: a giant rooted Takeo with 4 roots, each carrying spores. Shoot each spore once with the Masamune, survive while they hide, repeat when they reopen — zombies, Thrashers and spiders pour in the whole time, but Max Ammos drop constantly, so lean on the Masamune. Cut all four roots and enjoy the scream, the cutscene, and all perks.

Side Easter Eggs

Eight of them, from ten-second songs to becoming an actual spider.

Secret song — "Dead Flowers"
3 puppets
Secret song — Samantha's Lullaby
Water pockets: 1-3-5-6-7-5

In the wall near the purple water source are several pockets you fill with bucket water. From the left, pour 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 5 thirds of a bucket, then interact with the 7th pocket to start the song. Overfilled one? Knife it to drain.

The distant monster
Round 50+

Hit round 50 and a feral roar sounds in the distance. Near Lab B, look far outside the map — a giant shadow moves between the trees.

Doppelganger jumpscare
Scope the tank

Get a sniper rifle (box, or the medium trial reward), go to the green-tank room in the bunker, and scope the test subject inside a tank for 20–30 seconds. If it takes, one of the four characters appears in the room with no name over his head. Walk up to him. Regret it.

A friendly Thrasher
Blue eyes, your side

Needs the Masamune, the skull, and the hypnotised walls from the Masamune build. Shoot the roots on the green-tank wall with the Masamune to reveal a spore, then pop the spore while a zombie stands in it — he mutates with friendly blue eyes and fights for you until his battery runs out.

The golden bucket
Never runs dry

Needs one of every plant type grown and the purple-water poster hypnotised. Under Lab B, use the skull on the roots blocking a growing spot, then place your bucket on the spot. Three growing spots appear — seed them; carnivorous plants hatch on their own. Feed them zombies until a plant grows where your bucket sits: inside is the golden bucket, which never empties.

Hallucinations
Too much spore gas

Breathe spore gas long enough and certain spots start showing you things: the green tanks, both lab crafting tables, and the catwalk above Lab B.

Become the spider
The strangest one

Requirements

  • The skull, the KT-4, an electrified shield and all trials done.
  1. During a spider round, use the skull's hypnosis — one spider glows red. Keep her alive, kill the rest.
  2. Walk her to all three water sources and let her drink.
  3. Lower the Lab A cage, bring her in, raise it. Hit the panel with the electrified shield, lower it again.
  4. Finish the round. A special spider round starts — these ones only die to the KT-4, electrified shield or skull. Survive it, raise the cage, take the venom.

From now on, once per round (or per Max Ammo), you can transform into an undying spider for a minute.

It's buggy: get cornered by too many enemies and you can clip through the floor and die under the map.

Wonder Weapons

The KT-4 and its upgrade are built, not boxed — and the skull is its own beast. All three matter for the main quest.

KT-4
3 parts — free at its own table

A plant-goo sprayer that melts groups — charge the shot for more. Its other job: fertilising seeds.

  1. Spider venom. Power on, then use Lab A's control panel to lower the cage. Lead a spider to it — it walks in on its own — raise the cage, wait for the bang, take the venom.
    Set this up before the first spider round (~round 6, fog rolls in, spiders only, ends in a Max Ammo).
  2. The green flask. With power on, play around Lab B until a zombie spawns glowing green — kill it, take the flask.
  3. The flower. In the bunker: door straight ahead, all the way down, open the door facing Mule Kick, and swim the caverns until you find the blue tunnel going deeper. The flower is at the bottom.
    Take Jugger-Nog or you'll drown. Shooting the spore at the bottom of the tunnel refills your breath.
  4. Craft it free at the special table in the flooded room near one of the ritual altars.
Masamune
The upgraded KT-4 — required for the quest

Requirements

  • The KT-4, the Skull of Nan Sapwe and the shield.
  • All players' trials done (for the electrified shield step).
  1. The spider's tooth — a mini-boss. Behind the blue water source is a big web: shoot it with the KT-4 and drop in. You're locked in an arena with a giant spider — dodge, and shoot her mouth while it glows yellow. She spawns spiderlings throughout. Kill her, and take a tooth before you leave.
    On the way out there's a purple pool — wade in for free Widow's Wine.
  2. The rainbow flower. Take an empty bucket into the sewer pipe next to the KT-4 table (500 points). Spam interact while flying past the 115 meteorite to scoop rainbow water. Then use the skull's hypnosis (right-click) on the poster near the purple water, the wall left of the bunker entrance, and the wall by the green tanks. Finally, hypnotise the wall facing the spore in the underwater flower tunnel — it reveals a growing spot. Plant a seed there and water it three times with rainbow water. Yes, you're watering a plant underwater. It's magic. Grab what grows.
    The skull won't stretch to all three items on one charge — swap weapons after each use to save its ammo and recharge faster.
  3. The 115 flask. With your shield electrified (stand by the altar after all trials), hit Lab B's console with the shield and get in the cage. Solo it moves itself; in co-op someone works the console. At the bottom, press interact in the dark until your character confirms the grab.
  4. Upgrade it at the second table in the KT-4 room.
Skull of Nan Sapwe
4 rituals — the map's special weapon

Left-click obliterates zombies; right-click hypnotises them still. Several quest steps need the hypnosis. It recharges over time when stowed.

  1. Find the ritual court. Right from spawn, two doors: a main altar holding 4 bloodied skulls, ringed by 4 pillars. Each skull has 2, 3, 5 or 6 arrows marked on it.
  2. Clean each skull on its matching altar. The symbols move between games, but the four altars are fixed: spawn · the crashed gunship · the bunker underground by the KT-4/sewer room · outside the bunker past the power, near the zipline. Place the right skull and green zombies swarm it — they ignore you; keep them off the skull. Succeed and carry the white skull back to the main altar. Fail, and it's back on the main altar next round.
    Ritual 2 adds spiders; ritual 3 adds a Thrasher. It escalates — do the early ones early.
  3. The cave. All four cleaned skulls back on the main altar opens a cave below. Interact with the skull inside: you're locked in with waves of Keepers — kill enough and the Skull of Nan Sapwe is yours.

Buildables

Both required for the main quest. The bucket lives on its own tab.

Gas mask
3 parts — required for the quest

No more coughing in spore clouds, and longer underwater time. It wears out and rebuilds — the bar shows durability.

  1. The cable — at the crash site: on a rock beside the box spot, or right outside the plane on either side.
  2. The mask — green water area: upstairs by the perk machine, beside the water on a box, or between the growing spot and Lab B.
  3. The filter — the outdoor room left of the power: near the zipline or on the rocks by the Mystery Box.
  4. Craft at Lab A or B.
Shield
3 parts — and it can be electrified
  1. Part 1 — under Lab A, on the pillars: beside the Kuda or the neighbouring windows.
  2. Part 2 — on a tree in the Lab B area: near a spore on the upward path by a growing spot, or near the land mines.
  3. Part 3 — the AA-gun balcony (first door right inside the bunker): broken stones, near the window, or the box spot.
  4. Craft at Lab A or B.
Electrify it: after every player finishes their trials, hold the shield near the main altar and wait for the lightning. An electrified shield kills zombies that touch it, knocks perk prices down when you hit the machines, and drives several quest steps.

Buckets, water & plants

This map's whole ecosystem. Zombies drop seeds; you plant them at growing spots, water them with a bucket, and what grows depends entirely on which water you used.

The bucket

Four buckets on the map — spawn room, Lab A, Lab B and the main bunker (one per player). The three spawn-area spots:

A full bucket holds three waterings. A seed left unwatered for a whole round hatches on its own as something ordinary.

Power and Pack-a-Punch

Turning on the power

  1. Fill both generators with water. One at Lab A, one at Lab B — any colour works. Once you fill the first, a timer starts: fill the second before it runs dry.
  2. Into the bunker. With both generators wet, the main bunker door opens free. Infected zombies wait behind it, and this guarantees a Thrasher.
  3. Fix the vent underwater. Left door inside, go as far as you can — the lever shows one vent dead. Dive, clean the web on your left; the door shuts and you survive underwater until it reopens.
  4. Flip the switch — it's right in front of you when you surface.

Freeing Pack-a-Punch

  1. Part 1 — drops underwater when you clean the fan web during the power steps. You can grab it before power's even on.
  2. Part 2 — knife the cocoons in the purple-water underground. They spit zombies, and eventually the part.
  3. Part 3 — a valve in the submerged caverns where the KT-4 flower lives. Never in the blue tunnel itself.
  4. Drain it. Back at Pack-a-Punch, work the three panels around the fence — the water drains and the machine is yours.
