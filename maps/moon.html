Black Ops 1 · Griffin Station

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4 — soloable (solo you play as Richtofen, which matters for two of the steps).
  • Power on, the Hacker device, a Wave Gun, and Gersch Devices + QEDs from the box.
  • Steps 20 and 22 must be done by whoever is playing Richtofen.

"Big Bang Theory" — full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Turn on the power by the pyramid.
  2. Play Samantha Says outside spawn — a 6-step colour sequence on the four monitors (red, green, blue, yellow). Use the in-app tracker —
  3. Get the Hacker device. It's in the Lab — 6 possible spots: 3 floors, 2 per floor. Everything the Hacker can do — see Hacker tab
  4. Hack the lab panel — costs 500 points. It's on the 2nd floor of the lab: hack the 4th panel.
  5. Hack 4 access panels — timed. In the lab there are 8 access panels; hack 4 of them, and only the green ones.
  6. Quickly activate the 4 panel switches. In the lab, hold X (or Square) and get all four switched in quick succession.
  7. Let an excavator breach Tunnel 6.
  8. Hack the excavator control in spawn.
  9. Move the round stone artifact. In Tunnel 6, knife or shoot the artifact ball along until it reaches the spawn satellite.
  10. Get the Wave Gun and shoot the ball. Connect the Wave Gun end to end, then shoot the ball off the satellite.
  11. Move the ball from Tunnel 11 to the pyramid.
  12. Kill 25 zombies to fill the tube on the pyramid.
  13. Flip the power switch next to the tube.
  14. Samantha hands you a Death Machine for 90 seconds.
  15. Go to Area 51 and get the tiles. Throw a grenade to knock the tiles down, throw a Gersch Device to collect them, then teleport back to the Moon.
  16. Throw a QED at the tiles near Quick Revive.
  17. Find the S-shaped hose around the lab — it has three possible spots.
  18. Place the tube on the machine in spawn.
  19. Place the golden rod between the tiles.
  20. Richtofen: use the panel until it turns green.
  21. Get 100 kills around the pyramid.
  22. Richtofen: place the rod in the pyramid. Richtofen gets every perk for doing it.
  23. Play Samantha Says again — three more games, and they get harder each time.
  24. QED the front of the pyramid.
  25. Throw a Gersch at the ball in spawn.
  26. The rockets destroy the Earth. Every player walks away with all perks, permanently.

The whole quest on one sheet

Samantha Says tracker

The monitors outside spawn flash a colour sequence you have to repeat. Tap each monitor as it flashes and the colour drops into its column, so you can read the whole sequence back without memorising it.

Step 2 is a 6-colour sequence. Step 23 makes you play three more, each harder than the last — hit RESET between games.

Everything the Hacker can do

The Hacker is in the Lab — 6 possible spots across 3 floors, 2 per floor. Beyond the quest, it's the most useful tool on the map.

  1. Hack the 3 excavator consoles. From spawn, stop or redirect the excavators breaching Tunnel 6, Tunnel 11 and the Dome — 1,000 points per hack.
  2. Hack any door open. Drops the price to 200 points. Takes 32 seconds, or 22 with Speed Cola.
  3. Hack windows to instantly rebuild. Each window can be rebuilt four times for 100 points each; after that it costs you 300.
  4. Hack another player to give them points. 500 points per hack. During Double Points you give 1,000 but only lose 500.
  5. Hack a perk machine you've already bought from — it takes the perk back and refunds your money.
  6. Hack power-ups. For 5,000 points turn any power-up into a Max Ammo — or turn a Max Ammo into a Fire Sale for 5,000.
  7. Hack Gobblegum power-ups — they follow the same rules as ones dropped by zombies.
    You can't hack Extra Credit power-ups, Widow's Wine spider power-ups, or Gobblegum machines.
  8. Hack the Mystery Box three ways: Hack an empty box location to bring the box there and get one hit off it — 1,200 points; that spot then won't hold a box during Fire Sales for four rounds After buying from the box, hack the weapon it offers to swap it for another — 600 points Hack that second weapon again and it glows blue for anyone to pick up — you get 950 points back
  9. Hack wall guns for 3,000 points — the gun flips upside down and the ammo prices reverse: Pack-a-Punched ammo becomes cheap, un-upgraded ammo costs 4,500.
  10. Hack the Pack-a-Punch machine for free — the gates close for 30 seconds and keep zombies out.

Wave GunSplits into the dual Zap Guns · needed for the quest ›

Moon's headline wonder weapon, and the one the main quest needs.

  1. Only from the Mystery Box — there's no build quest on this map.
  2. Two weapons in one. Fired whole it liquefies a crowd; split it apart and you get the dual Zap Guns, which are better for picking off single targets.
  3. Needed for the quest — you connect it end to end and shoot the artifact ball off the satellite.
Gersch DeviceThrowable black hole · used twice in the quest ›

A throwable black hole. It sucks zombies in and deletes them.

  1. Only from the Mystery Box.
  2. Used twice in the quest — to collect the tiles at Area 51, and to throw at the ball in spawn near the end.
Q.E.D.Random-effect grenade · used twice in the quest ›

The Quantum Entanglement Device — a grenade that does something random every time. It can help enormously or hurt you, so treat it as a gamble.

  1. Only from the Mystery Box.
  2. Used twice in the quest — on the tiles near Quick Revive, and on the front of the pyramid.

Reaching Pack-a-Punch

Pack-a-Punch isn't on the Moon at all — it's back at Area 51, so you have to teleport there and back.

  1. Find the teleporter. It's outside, right next to the biodome.
  2. Teleport back to Area 51 and use the Pack-a-Punch machine there.
  3. Same trip as the quest. Area 51 is also where you knock down the tiles in step 15.
  4. Handy: the Hacker can shut the Pack-a-Punch gates for 30 seconds to keep zombies off you while you upgrade.

Perk machines

Eight perks on Moon — but only six machines are actually on the Moon itself. Jugger-Nog and Speed Cola are both back at Area 51.

The big Moon quirk: Area 51 only ever has one of Jugger-Nog or Speed Cola, and it alternates every single time you teleport in. If you want the other one, you have to make another trip.

Worth knowing

  1. Finish the main quest and you keep every perk, permanently — for all players.
  2. The Hacker refunds perks. Hack a machine you've already bought from and it takes the perk back and returns your points.
  3. Mule Kick warning: go down and you lose whatever gun was in that third slot.
  4. On the Black Ops 3 version (Zombies Chronicles), Double Tap becomes Double Tap II and PhD Flopper is replaced by Widow's Wine in the Bio-Dome.
