TranZit

Black Ops 2 · Green Run — ride the bus, fear the fog

“Tower of Babble” has two versions — you either help Richtofen or Maxis, and you can only do one per game. Your choice carries into Die Rise and Buried, so pick a side and stick with it. Tabs below:

Requirements

  • Players: 2+ recommended. On PC (Plutonium) the BO2-Full-Solo-Mods mod makes it solo-friendly — only 2 lamp posts instead of 4.
  • Power on — step 1 below.
  • Jet Gun built —
  • EMP Grenades for ALL players (Mystery Box only) + an explosive weapon (Ray Gun is ideal).
  • Tip: play as Stuhlinger — he’s the one who hears Richtofen talk.

Richtofen’s steps

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Turn on the power. Ride the bus all the way round: Bus Depot → Diner → Farm → Power Station. Open the outhouse door and drop down the hole into the secret lab. In the big white room, grab the three parts lying around — the Hand, the Switch and the Generator — build the Power Switch at the panel, then flip it.
  2. Build the Nav Card Table at the pylon.

    The table has four parts, and you can only carry one at a time. Every part gets walked to the big electric pylon in the cornfield — it’s in the fog between the Farm and the Power Station, off the left side of the road. The build spot is at the base of the pylon.

    • 115 Meteor — Bus Depot. Leave through the main doors and look into the zombie barrier just behind the Mystery Box spawn.
    • Wooden Table — spot 1: in the Tunnel (Bus Depot → Diner), by the zombie barrier on the right in the same walkway as the M16 wall-buy. Spot 2: Power Station, on the edge of the metal walkway below Tombstone Soda.
    • Electrical Box — spot 1: the Farm, just left of the fridge where you can store a weapon. Spot 2: Town, by the dumpster where the Mystery Box spawns.
    • Radio — spot 1: Diner, on top of a locker in the mechanic’s garage. Spot 2: on a table inside Nacht der Untoten (the broken brick building in the cornfield — stick to the right path after the Farm heading to the Power Station).
  3. Build the Jet Gun. Four parts, then assemble it at the workbench in the Town bar (the Stamin-Up building). Full part locations: .
  4. Get EMP Grenades + an explosive weapon. Both come from the Mystery Box — look for the blue light in the sky to find it. Every player needs EMP Grenades; at least one of you wants a Ray Gun (or grenades work) for step 6.
  5. Break the Jet Gun at the Nav Card Reader. Stand at the reader (next to your new table at the pylon) and hold the Jet Gun’s trigger until it overheats and explodes on purpose. Richtofen speaks to Stuhlinger. You can pick the scattered parts back up and rebuild it afterwards if you want it again.
  6. Kill 25 zombies under the pylon with explosives. Stay under the pylon and rack up about 25 explosive kills (Ray Gun / grenades). Richtofen will confirm when it’s done.
  7. EMP the four lamp posts at the same time. Each player stands at a different green-lit lamp post out in the fog and you all throw an EMP at them simultaneously. Done right, a blue orb flies from each lamp to the pylon and the quest completes — check the lobby screen: the TranZit icon now glows blue.
Solo on Plutonium? With the BO2-Full-Solo-Mods mod you only need to EMP 2 lamp posts — throw one EMP, then use a denizen portal to warp to the second lamp and throw the other.

Requirements

  • Players: 2+ on console/vanilla. Solo is only possible on PC (Plutonium) with the BO2-Full-Solo-Mods mod — then 1 Turbine at the table is enough.
  • A Turbine per player — built at the Bus Depot (step 1).
  • EMP Grenades from the Mystery Box.
  • No Jet Gun needed — that’s the nice part of Maxis’s side.
  • Get perks + Pack-a-Punch sorted first — you’ll be playing with the power OFF.

Maxis’s steps

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Build the Turbine. All three parts are inside the Bus Depot spawn room. Build it at the workbench before you leave. In co-op, every player should build and carry their own.
  2. Build the Nav Card Table at the pylon.

    The table has four parts, and you can only carry one at a time. Every part gets walked to the big electric pylon in the cornfield — it’s in the fog between the Farm and the Power Station, off the left side of the road. The build spot is at the base of the pylon.

    • 115 Meteor — Bus Depot. Leave through the main doors and look into the zombie barrier just behind the Mystery Box spawn.
    • Wooden Table — spot 1: in the Tunnel (Bus Depot → Diner), by the zombie barrier on the right in the same walkway as the M16 wall-buy. Spot 2: Power Station, on the edge of the metal walkway below Tombstone Soda.
    • Electrical Box — spot 1: the Farm, just left of the fridge where you can store a weapon. Spot 2: Town, by the dumpster where the Mystery Box spawns.
    • Radio — spot 1: Diner, on top of a locker in the mechanic’s garage. Spot 2: on a table inside Nacht der Untoten (the broken brick building in the cornfield — stick to the right path after the Farm heading to the Power Station).
  3. Turn the power ON, then straight back OFF. At the Power Station, drop down through the outhouse, build the Power Switch from the three parts in the white lab room (Hand, Switch, Generator), and flip it. The moment the Avogadro (the electric man) rises out — turn the power back off. The rest of this quest is done with the power off.
  4. Get EMP Grenades. Mystery Box only — follow the blue light in the sky and spin until they show up.
  5. Summon the Avogadro at the pylon. Go to the pylon and be patient: wait until the storm builds and there is lightning in the sky all around you — every direction you look, not just one. Then kill the last zombie to end the round, and the Avogadro spawns in.
  6. EMP the Avogadro at the Nav Card Table. Place a Turbine beside the Nav Card Table by the back leg of the pylon (co-op: 2 Turbines, one on each side of the table). Lure the Avogadro over and throw an EMP at him when he’s on the opposite side from the Turbines so the blast doesn’t kill them. He dies instantly and shoots back up into the sky — Maxis speaks. That’s your confirmation.
  7. Power a lamp post with a Turbine. Grab a Turbine (solo/2-player: just take one from the table; 4-player: the two table Turbines must stay running — the other two players carry theirs) and place it at any green lamp post in the fog — there’s one right outside the Cornfield, and one at every stop. The lamp glows with an orange orb and fires power at the pylon. When the pylon lights up orange, you’re done — the lobby TranZit icon glows orange.

Tap a card to open it.

Galvaknuckles6,000 points — the fog stops being scary

Electric knuckles that one-hit the denizens (the little face-grabbers in the fog) and one-hit normal zombies up to round 14. Best quality-of-life buy on the map — but the machine is hidden on the Diner roof, and you need the Hatch to get up there.

1 — Find the Hatch

The Hatch is a buildable part that spawns in one of four locked rooms. Each room opens by placing a Turbine in front of its door:

  • Bus Depot — the shutter room on the right at spawn; Hatch in the right corner.
  • Diner — the locked building next to the crashed truck.
  • Farm — the locked door by the barn; Hatch next to the tractor.
  • Town — the locked door at the back, right of the Mystery Box spawn.

2 — Use it at the Diner & buy

Carry the Hatch to the back of the Diner and look up — there’s a hatch frame in the ceiling. Place it, then double-tap jump to pull yourself onto the roof. The Galvaknuckles chalk is up there for 6,000 points.

The Hatch can also be used to get on the roof of the bus instead — fun, but a waste. Spend it on the Galvaknuckles.
Nav CardGrab it early — it matters later

Just outside the Bus Depot spawn, to the right in the dirt, there’s a small Nav Card you can pick up. It doesn’t fit TranZit’s own Nav Card Table — the navcards link the three Victis maps (TranZit / Die Rise / Buried) together. Grab it every game anyway; it’ll come into play when those guides are built.

One buildable wonder weapon — no box luck involved.

Jet Gun (Thrustodyne Aeronautics Model 23)Free — built from 4 parts

A jet engine on a handle: it sucks zombies in and shreds them, no ammo needed — but hold the trigger too long and the gauge maxes out and the gun explodes, scattering its parts where you stand. Honestly weak as a weapon; you build it because Richtofen’s easter egg requires it .

You can carry only one part at a time. Build it at the workbench in the Town bar — the left building, where Stamin-Up is.

Jet Engine — in the Tunnel (Bus Depot → Diner)

  • On the right, by the M16 wall-buy.
  • On the left, in the corner by the locked garage door.
  • In the middle, by the blue car.

Handle — inside Nacht der Untoten (cornfield, right path after the Farm)

  • Back right wall, by the destroyed shelf.
  • On the stairs.
  • By the lantern and cabinet.

Wires — Power Station (power must be ON first)

  • On the broken electrical box — drop over the railing to reach it.
  • On the right, on the ledge near the lava pit.
  • On a barrel past the catwalk, to the right.

Pressure Gauge — Hunter’s Cabin (fog on the right, Power Station → Town)

  • Directly in front of the fireplace.
  • On the bench near the wooden barrel.
  • On the metal bed frame.
Overheat = boom. Feather the trigger and watch the gauge. If it blows, the parts drop right there — pick them up and rebuild at the bench.

TranZit started the buildables system — parts lying around the map, carried one at a time to a workbench.

TurbinePortable power — the map’s master key

A fan on a mannequin stand that generates local power. It opens the Turbine-locked doors at every stop, powers lamp posts, holds the Pack-a-Punch door open, and both sides of the easter egg lean on it.

All three parts are in the Bus Depot spawn room — grab them one at a time and build at the workbench before you leave. It can be destroyed by zombies (and by your own EMPs), so treat it as disposable and rebuild when needed.

Zombie ShieldNo more hits from behind

Blocks all damage from behind while equipped, melee-kills in one hit until round 20, and can be planted on the ground as a distraction. It breaks after enough hits — build a fresh one when it does.

Both parts are around the Diner, and the build bench is in the garage, behind the Mystery Box spawn:

  • Hand Trolley — inside the Diner: behind the counter, or by the far booth from Speed Cola.
  • Car Door — in the bathroom right of the Mystery Box, on the garage table by the zombie barrier, or by the car on the garage’s maintenance platform.

Opening Pack-a-Punch

5,000 points per upgrade. The machine is behind the Town bank vault — and the door only opens while a Turbine is running back at the Power Station. Fiddly the first time; easy once you know the loop.

  1. Build the Turbine at spawn (three parts in the Bus Depot room), then ride the bus: Diner → Farm → Power Station.
  2. Turn on the power. Buy the bunker door, drop down, collect the Hand, Switch and Generator box in the white room, build the Power Switch and flip it. Then go back to where you dropped in and find the rusted door with the lightning bolt — place your Turbine in front of it. Do it with one zombie left alive, or they’ll smash the Turbine.
  3. Open the vault in Town. Take the bus (or run the fog road — knife the denizens fast). Enter the bank — first building on the right — cook a grenade and throw it at the vault door. Inside, grenade the second vault door too. If your Turbine is still spinning at the Power Station, the wall inside opens to the Pack-a-Punch room. Grab the three parts in there and build the machine at the end of the corridor on the right.
Room sealed itself again? That’s TranZit. Build a fresh Turbine at the Bus Depot and park it at the rusted door again — the vault room reopens.
