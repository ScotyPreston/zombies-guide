"The Beginning of the End" — the Chaos crew fights their way out of the arena.

The full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Grind the skull. A skull with a symbol on its forehead hides in one of 9 spots in the Temple — hit it with your specialist weapon to free it. Take it to the grinder in the Flooded Crypt and hit it with 3 fully charged Death of Orion shots; collect the dust.
  2. Anger the crowd, collect the... gift. Earn bad crowd affinity — grenades into the crowd, standing in fire, taking hits, rockets at the stands — until they throw dung at you. Pick it up.
  3. The burnt stick. Let a gladiator throw an axe into one of the two spawn bonfires — a chunk of wood drops. Place the stick in the bonfire below Odin Tower, wait 2–3 rounds, and take it back charred. (Dung, skull dust and stick can be done in any order.)
  4. Brew the fertilizer. Stick, dung and skull dust go on the plate by the blood bath at the bottom of Zeus Temple. Wait 2 rounds until it glows green, then carry it to the bottom of Danu Temple, place it in the centre, and wait 3–4 rounds for the green smoke.
  5. Crack the floor, shoot the roots. Equip the fire bomb and get a fire-bomb kill on a zombie standing right on the fertilizer — the floor cracks open blue. All players stand in the centre 15 seconds to teleport down. Below: shoot the red glowing ball on the wall, climb a floor, repeat twice more — a Max Ammo after each.
  6. The four bulls. Four circular bull symbols around the map get shot with your shield; each one summons a gladiator to kill. Nine possible spots:
  7. The sacrifice order. In the Ra Altar Room, interact with the blue symbol: 4 symbols appear bottom-to-top. Each is a special enemy — kill them in that order, twice through. Wrong kill = restart next round.
    Symbol translation: the chart below maps each symbol to its enemy. Pause and write your order down, or pop an Undead-Man Walking.
  8. The Arena stone and the poles. Everyone to the middle of the Arena, interact with the stone, and you're teleported underground. Shoot the 4 poles in the windows (bullets or shield fire) until each rises fully into the ceiling.
  9. Charge the circles, survive the storm. Back in the Arena, 4 electric circles need charging: kill Kill-O-Watt-stunned zombies inside them, or Brawlers/Gladiators with the Kill-O-Watt anywhere. All four charged → 4 orbs appear centre-Arena — interact, get an unlimited specialist weapon, and survive until the screen flashes.
  10. The Orion bank-shots. Underground, 3 sets of blue symbols hide through walls: shoot one with the Death of Orion so the shot chains through the other two. Stand where the shots show and fire until the symbol stays lit — one needs a jump shot.
  11. The Pit survival — the key. The audio cue sends everyone to The Pit: stand on the plate, survive the lockdown (2 Max Ammos drop during it), and the key floats up through the grate.
  12. The Champions. The key opens the Arena gate — a Helion Salvo is strongly recommended. Tigers and specials pour in; then an armoured elephant: keep your distance, break the armour weak spots on its sides, then shoot the exposed hearts (sides and front). Kill it — and its friend, same way. Cutscene. Done.

Death of Orion
The free method — no box luck needed

Requirements

  • Pack-a-Punch open and a PaP'd weapon.
  • The acid trap built.
  1. Shoot the fire bowl. Between the Danu and Ra bottom entrances there's a broken wall — hit the bowl of fire behind it with a Pack-a-Punched weapon.
  2. Follow the fire arrow. On the Ra–Danu bridge, a burning arrow points at where the statue head spawned (1 of 4 spots):
  3. Melt the head. Place it on the metal drain under the acid trap and run the trap — a gold scorpion is left behind. Take it to the bottom of Danu Temple.
  4. Charm the crowd. Build max crowd affinity — kill cleanly, never get hit, stay out of fire — until the priest announces "A Gift from Serket" and the crowd throws a golden vase into the spawn arena.
  5. Milk the scorpion. At the Danu tree: knife the small hole, set the vase under the scorpion spout, end the round, and collect the green venom.
  6. Poison the box. Pour it on the left side of the Mystery Box — your next pull is the Death of Orion.

Pack-a-Punch

No power to flip on this map — the towers run on blood.

  1. Ring all four gongs. One in each god's temple (Ra, Danu, Odin, Zeus). Each gong summons a special enemy — kill it and take its head.
  2. Four heads on the spikes. Down in The Temple at the bottom of the map, place all four heads on the spikes in the centre.

Perks — the Black Ops 4 way

BO4 changed the system: there are no fixed perk machines. You pick four perks in your loadout before the game, and they map to the four altar slots you buy in order at 2500 / 3000 / 3500 / 4000.
