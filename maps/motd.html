Mob of the Dead

Black Ops 2 · Alcatraz prison

Requirements

  • Players: 2–4 — NOT possible solo (the final step is Weasel versus the mobsters).
  • Hell's Retriever obtained —
  • The plane built —

"Pop Goes the Weasel" — full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Get the Warden's Key. It hangs above either the Warden's Office entrance or the Cafeteria entrance. Enter Afterlife at the station across from that entrance, hold jump to float up into the ghost room, and zap the electrical panel to drop the key. Revive yourself and pick it up.
  2. Obtain the Hell's Retriever. Feed the three Cerberus dog heads (6 kills each), then grab the tomahawk in the Citadel Tunnels. Full walkthrough with all 3 dog-head locations — see Wonder Weapons tab
  3. Collect the five blue skulls for the free Blundergat. Throw the Hell's Retriever at each of the five skull spots, then claim the Blundergat from the middle table in the Warden's Office. All 5 skull locations with screenshots — see Wonder Weapons tab, Blundergat card
    This exact Blundergat is required — one from the Mystery Box does NOT count for the quest.
  4. Build the plane and fly to the Golden Gate Bridge. Collect all five parts and assemble the Icarus on the roof runway, then board it with your crew. On the bridge you get Pack-a-Punch; after 30 seconds the electric chairs spawn — sit to return to Alcatraz. All 5 parts with screenshots — see Buildables tab, Icarus card
  5. Collect the Spoon. Reveal it behind the movie poster near the Warden's Office, zap it in Afterlife, then Retriever it off the Cafeteria shelf. This is the start of the Golden Spork side quest. Full walkthrough with screenshots — see Side Easter Eggs tab, Golden Spork card
  6. Refuel and fly to the bridge two more times. A gas can now spawns in each area where a plane part was found. Collect all five, refuel the plane, and fly again — repeat until you have made three total trips to the bridge.
  7. Enter the mobsters' prison numbers. After the third bridge trip, go to the Citadel Tunnels number pad. Enter Afterlife from the top of the stairs and input the four numbers from the characters' prison suits, in order: 101  →  872  →  481  →  386
    The pad blinks and changes digits after each correct number. Afterlife kills you if you don't revive in time — split this across TWO Afterlife uses instead of dying.
  8. Follow the headphones. After reviving, Brutus speaks and the screen goes black-and-white with Stanley Ferguson's narration. Headphones spawn one after another — pick each up and listen until the narration ends, then find the next. Five total: 1. The Citadel Tunnels staircase · 2. Close to Double Tap · 3. The walkway between the Warden's Office and the Cafeteria · 4. The staircase from the Cafeteria to the Infirmary · 5. The staircase to the rooftop
  9. Enter Afterlife and board the plane. With all five headphones heard, get set for the finale. Enter Afterlife in the Infirmary and interact with the plane — no refuel needed. You'll board it in Afterlife mode.
  10. Break or continue the cycle. At the bridge, revive at the electric chair — then it's a PvP showdown: Weasel versus the mobsters. Weasel dies → the cycle CONTINUES: the loop resets and all four wake up back in Alcatraz. · Weasel wins → the cycle is BROKEN: the loop ends (canon leading into Blood of the Dead).

Golden SporkOne-hit-kill melee until round 33 ›

Requirements

  • The plane built and flown —
  • Hell's Retriever —
  • The Acid Gat (Blundergat + Acid Gat Kit) — you must get the 50 kills with this exact weapon
  1. Reveal and grab the Spoon. Face the Warden's Office and look right for the cell with the movie poster. Throw the Hell's Retriever at the poster to reveal an Afterlife door, then use the Afterlife station just left of it. In Afterlife, look at the ground, find the Spoon and zap it — an audio cue plays. Revive yourself, head to the Cafeteria, and throw the Retriever at the Spoon on the metal shelf in the back to collect it.
  2. Sink the Spoon in the bathtub. Head to the Infirmary (the room with the Mystery Box) and find the bloodied bathtub in the back-right. Interact with it — the Spoon sinks into the tub.
  3. Kill exactly 50 zombies with the Acid Gat — in the Showers. Both parts matter: it has to be the Acid Gat, and the kills have to happen in the Showers. After the 50th kill you'll hear an ominous laugh.
    The laugh is your confirmation — without it, the bathtub won't give you anything. If nothing happens, you're short on kills or killed some outside the Showers.
  4. Claim the Golden Spork. After the laugh, return to the bathtub and interact with it to take the Golden Spork.
  5. How it works: a melee weapon that one-hit-kills zombies all the way up to round 33.

Hell's RetrieverReturning tomahawk · free · needed for the main quest ›
  1. Find the three Cerberus dog-head drawings on the walls and feed each one 6 zombie kills right in front of it. You'll see the zombies flip through the air as the head devours them — when it's fed, the head disappears.
  2. With all three heads fed, head to the Citadel Tunnels and turn right — the tunnel is now open. Pick up the Hell's Retriever inside.
  3. How it works: it's on your tactical button (L1/LB). Hold the button to throw harder for more damage and to pull in power-ups from further away. It always flies back to you, so you can't lose it.
Hell's RedeemerUpgraded Retriever · charged throws insta-kill · round 10+ ›

Requirements

  • Hell's Retriever already obtained (card above)
  • Round 10 or later, and the plane built so you can reach the bridge —
  • Discipline: during the bridge round you may kill with nothing but the Retriever
  1. Get kills with the Retriever. Kill roughly 10–15 zombies with the Hell's Retriever. You'll hear an Afterlife-style audio cue confirming this part is done.
    Listen for the cue — it's the only confirmation the game gives you. There's no on-screen message.
  2. Fly to the Golden Gate Bridge on round 10 or later, and for that entire round kill only with the Hell's Retriever — no guns, no melee. A second audio cue confirms it.
    If you kill anything with another weapon during this round, the step fails and you'll have to retry it on a later bridge trip.
  3. Throw the Retriever into the lava. Back at Alcatraz, go to the first dog head in the Broadway cell block and throw the Hell's Retriever down into the lava pit below. It's consumed and vanishes from your loadout — that's supposed to happen.
  4. Finish the round, then on the new round head back down and enter Afterlife. Collect the Hell's Redeemer from the same spot in the Citadel Tunnels where you originally picked up the Retriever.
  5. How it works: hold L1/LB to charge before throwing. Charged at least once, it insta-kills any zombie — and Brutus too, once his helmet is off. You can charge up to three times; the pulses while holding tell you the charge level.
Blundergat5 blue skulls → free from the Warden's Office ›

Requirements

  • Hell's Retriever obtained (card above) — you throw it at each skull
  • Alternative: the Blundergat can also drop from the Mystery Box, but see the warning below
For the main Easter egg you must use THIS free Blundergat — a Mystery Box one does not count for the quest.

The five skulls are technically only visible in Afterlife, but if you throw the Hell's Retriever at the exact spots below you never have to enter Afterlife at all.

  1. Skull 1 — the prison cell. Leave spawn through C-D Street and look in the first cell on the right. Throw the Hell's Retriever at the toilet, between the shelf and the bed.
  2. Skull 2 — the Warden's Office. Inside the office, hug the wall to the right of Speed Cola and look out at the two electricity poles by the Warden's House. Throw the Retriever at the top of the second pole.
  3. Skull 3 — the Roof. Up where the plane is built, go to the back-left corner, look above the lamp post and throw the Retriever at the roof edge.
  4. Skull 4 — Jugger-Nog, upstairs Docks. Stand opposite Jugger-Nog, look up into the back corner, and throw the Retriever at the lamp while jumping.
  5. Skull 5 — the Docks, by the Mystery Box. In the back of the Docks, look into the closed-off section and throw the Retriever at the second wooden pillar from the left — the middle one past PhD Flopper.
  6. Claim it. With all five skulls collected, go to the Warden's Office and interact with the middle table — the Blundergat burns into existence for you to pick up.
  7. Upgrade path: the Blundergat converts into the Acid Gat with the Acid Gat Kit —

Icarus (the plane)5 parts · your ride to Pack-a-Punch on the bridge ›

Most parts need the Warden's Key (main quest step 1) and Afterlife work. Build everything on the roof runway.

  1. The Engine. Enter Afterlife above the Warden's Office and follow the path right to zap an electric panel. Then enter Afterlife at the Docks in Building 64 and zap the three activated generators. Finally use the Warden's Key on the right-hand cell as you enter the Warden's Office and take the Engine.
  2. The Sheets. Enter Afterlife opposite the Showers to find the ghost door. Look up at the pipes and zap the electrical panel, then head into the Showers and zap the panel opposite the laundry machine. Interact with the laundry machine to start a 30-second lockdown — survive it and take the Sheets.
  3. The Rigging. Unlock the number pad at the bottom of the Citadel Tunnels stairs with the Warden's Key. Enter Afterlife at the top of the stairs and look left on the way down — three glowing numbers. Enter them on the pad, revive yourself, then take the Rigging from the elevator below.
  4. The Gas Tanks. In the Docks, stand at the Afterlife panel spot and activate the power switch to grab the Gas Tanks — then enter Afterlife and zap the same switch to free yourself.
  5. The Valves. In the Infirmary, open the locked cabinet with the Warden's Key and take the Valves.
  6. Open the runway and build. Enter Afterlife in the Infirmary with your back to the Roof; head right into the secret room, climb through, and zap the rooftop power switch. Revive, then build all five parts into the plane on the runway.
  7. Flying it is the main quest —
Acid Gat Kit3 parts · converts the Blundergat into the Acid Gat ›

Requirements

  • You need a Blundergat first — either the free quest one or a Mystery Box drop works for this.
  • The conversion is reversible: you can swap back to the Blundergat later and it keeps its Pack-a-Punch status.

The Acid Gat fires rounds that behave like a Monkey Bomb — zombies are drawn to the impact and take heavy splash damage. Each part has 3 possible spawns.

  1. Part 1 — the Motor. Always in the Warden's Office. Check all three: 1. Near the Uzi wall-buy, next to the locked door that holds a plane part 2. In the left corner of the Mystery Box spawn spot 3. By the boarded-up fireplace, left of Speed Cola
  2. Part 2 — the Case. Always in the Cell Block. Check all three: 1. On a trolley to the right, just outside the Library at spawn 2. Opposite the Afterlife box in Michigan Avenue, across from the stairs to the Showers 3. Beneath the stairs coming down from the Warden's Office toward the Cell Block center, near the B23R wall-buy
  3. Part 3 — the Acid Bottle. Always in the Infirmary. Check all three: 1. On a metal tray in the left area of the Infirmary 2. In the corner with the bloodied bathtubs 3. By a bloodied bathtub, opposite the Mystery Box spawn
  4. Build it and convert. Take all three parts to any workbench, then trade your Blundergat in at the finished kit to get the Acid Gat. Workbenches: by Electric Cherry in the upstairs Cell Block (safest) · back-right corner of the Cafeteria mess hall · underneath the Docks, left of the generator room
Zombie Shield3 parts · protects your back · no prerequisites ›

Worth grabbing early — it guards your back while you train zombies. Nothing needs unlocking first: you can collect all three parts in one trip. Each part has 3 possible spawns.

  1. Part 1 — the Cell Door. All three spots are around the Citadel Tunnels: 1. The spiral stairs on the left, running from the Citadel Tunnels down to the Docks 2. In the elevator hallway at the bottom of the Citadel Tunnels 3. In the far-right corner as you leave the Citadel Tunnels, before Building 64 (the generator room)
  2. Part 2 — the Clamp. All three spots are inside Building 64 / the generator room at the Docks: 1. On a shelf against the brick wall behind the entrance to Building 64 2. On the floor, tucked by a generator 3. On a shelf in the back-right corner
  3. Part 3 — the Hand Trolley. All three spots are out in the Docks: 1. In front of the large crane, just left of the locked door needed for a plane part 2. By the cargo on the left, on the way to the Mystery Box and the Sniper Tower trap 3. Underneath the Sniper Tower on the left, directly opposite the Mystery Box
  4. Build it. Take all three parts to any workbench and assemble the shield. Workbenches: the Cafeteria (most open space) · the upstairs cell block in Broadway · under the Docks

Reaching Pack-a-Punch

Pack-a-Punch sits on the Golden Gate Bridge — you fly there.

  1. Build the Icarus — all 5 parts.
  2. Board the plane on the roof runway with your team and fly to the bridge. The Pack-a-Punch machine is right there (5,000 points).
  3. After 30 seconds on the bridge, the electric chairs spawn — sit in one to return to Alcatraz.
  4. Later trips: a gas can spawns in each area where a plane part was found — collect all five and refuel the plane to fly again.

Perk machines

Five perks are available in a normal game. Locations are fixed — nothing moves between matches. Note there is no Quick Revive on this map: going down puts you into Afterlife, where you revive yourself.

Power each machine on its own. Unlike other maps, there's no single switch that turns the perks on. For every machine: go into Afterlife, shoot its voltmeter with electricity, then revive yourself. You can usually do this while you're already in Afterlife for something else.
