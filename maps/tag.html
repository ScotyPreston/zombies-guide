Tag der Toten

Black Ops 4 · Call of the Dead, reborn — the Aether finale

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4. Normal difficulty, no modifiers.
  • All three power switches, Pack-a-Punch, the zipline handle and Golden Pack-a-Punch.
  • Two totems' full challenge sets completed.
  • Samantha's Music Boxes, a stick of dynamite, and the shield.
  • The Wunderwaffe.

"Salvation Lies Above" — the last Aether quest. Easier than Blood of the Dead or Voyage, but long: the Seal of Duality goes round the map three times.

The full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Wake the Hermit, finish two totems. Flip the Docks switch at spawn and climb to the top of the Lighthouse so the Hermit notices you — that activates the challenge totems. Complete every challenge on two totems (the reference recommends Lighthouse Station and Forecastle). Set up Pack-a-Punch, power, the Music Boxes, shield and Wunderwaffe while you're at it.
  2. The dials. Back at the top of the Lighthouse the Hermit winches down a set of dials. Take them to Artifact Storage — the room with the Cola perk — and hold use on the four wall slots. Rotate each dial until it dings.
  3. Offerings three. Subtitles on. The Apothicon Blood starts talking; when the subtitle says "Find us offerings three", listen: each clue is "[Apothicon word], where ______". Ignore the word, note the phrase — each maps to one of 20 spots holding a bone or organ (the reference points to GregFPS's video for the phrase-to-spot list). Three offerings.
  4. The Seal of Duality. A fourth clue leads to a board on a wall: melee it down to reveal a safe (four possible spots, video in the reference). Dynamite the safe and take the Seal.
  5. The big orb. Place the Seal on the barrel under the red orb in Artifact Storage. The orb bounces round the room — shoot it until your screen flashes white three times and it settles back over the barrel.
  6. Three small orbs. Three orange orbs are out on the map. Don't walk up to one — it relocates. From a distance, throw a snowball at it, then shoot it once it turns blue: it flies home to Artifact Storage. All three back, repeat the snowball-and-shoot inside the room and they merge into the big orb. Take the Seal off the barrel.
  7. The campfire. In the Sunken Path, place the Seal on the campfire and throw a Samantha's Music Box at it: the flame turns blue, zombies vanish, dialogue plays. When it's orange again, take the Seal back.
  8. Soapstones. Take two soapstones from the Hermit. Behind the Lighthouse toward the Boathouse, place them in the two square dents in the snow, then fire the Lighthouse trap to heat them (stay clear of the laser). Grab them and get to the Facility without touching water — stairs to the zipline. In Human Infusion slot one stone into the panel; carry the other to the scorch mark in the Decontamination hallway, freeze it with that trap, and slot it in too. A fuse pops out: take it to the big metal door downstairs in Lighthouse Station and fit it in the panel on the right.
  9. Power the fuse. With the Wunderwaffe, shoot the two batteries outside the Facility (one shot each, they spark). Then kill an electric zombie beside each of three generators until it sparks. Hold use on the fuse: the door opens — take the crystal. Put the Seal on the barrel again.
  10. Orbs and campfire, round two. Repeat steps 5–7 exactly: shoot the big orb to three flashes, snowball-and-shoot the three small orbs home and into the big one, Seal to the campfire, Music Box, blue flame, take it back.
  11. Lockdown and the four Pack-a-Punches.
    Upgrade every gun you care about BEFORE this step — Pack-a-Punch becomes unusable afterwards.
    All players at the top of the Lighthouse: put the Seal on the Hermit's plank and survive a ~2-minute lockdown inside (split the horde across floors in co-op). He winches the Seal back. Take it to the Pack-a-Punch the lighthouse beam points at, upgrade it, and charge the machine with nearby kills until it returns the Seal; the beam moves on — all four locations. Then the orb dance a third time (the small orbs stay in the room this time) and the campfire a third time.
  12. The Seal's journey — the "boss". All perks, full shield. Flinger on the Sun Deck to the island, upgrade the Seal at Golden Pack-a-Punch, charge it. It throws up a bubble shield and the map transforms — all water becomes lava (hop the rocks). Stay inside the bubble as the Seal walks the map; outside it you burn. When it stops, kill zombies beside it to charge it (Max Ammo + Carpenter each time). It climbs the Lighthouse and heads for the Facility — take the zipline, it catches up. In Human Infusion the bubble shrinks: keep charging it from inside until the screen flashes and every zombie despawns. Take the Agarthan Device to the Hermit, then watch the Lighthouse from the Sun Deck as he leaves — he's Pablo Marinus. The device drops on the Forecastle: hold use, and in the final cinematic push the stick forward to walk the children into the light.

George Romero's glasses — free 500
Lighthouse Station

On a table in Lighthouse Station, a pair of big glasses. Every player can hold use on them once for 500 points. A tribute to George A. Romero.

"The Secret" — the flinger to Mars
One player per game; inconsistent
  1. Dynamite the barricade on the Facility platform; behind it is a flinger with a broken fuse box.
  2. Take the fuse box to the Hermit; he hands it back repaired. Put it back on the flinger.
  3. Getting the flinger to send you to the secret is hit-and-miss (solo and co-op methods both exist — the reference links a solo video). You land in water: keep meleeing when you freeze, and swim through the cave until you reach a beach looking at Mars.
Four bells in 12 seconds
The easiest one in the game

Throw four snowballs in a row at the four bells at spawn.

Five Hellhounds with snowballs / freeze 10 at once
Snowballs, or the Tundragun into a horde

Snowball five dogs in one game. The freeze-10 usually pops alongside it in co-op; otherwise fire the Tundragun into a packed horde.

All nine ziplines, no kills, no downs
Only counts between the first and last ride

From your first zipline until you've ridden all nine, don't kill anything and don't go down. Kills before the first ride are fine. The reference has a video of the route.

Open every path without power
23 doors, 3 dynamites, the water pipe — no switches

Never touch a power switch (it can't be turned off). Buy all 23 doors (3 from spawn, 5 out of the Lighthouse, 7 on the freighter, 4 in the Facility, 1 in Lighthouse Cove, 1 out of the Cove toward the ship, 1 in the blue-rock cave, 1 Beach-to-ship), dynamite all three red-X barricades, and shoot the blue pipe in Artifact Storage to put out the Sunken Path fire. No perks, no power.

Break the jar on the Lighthouse with the Strife
Start with the Strife

From the Loading Platform at the Facility, shoot the near-invisible jar on top of the Lighthouse with the Strife. Line the spot up with a sniper first.

Pack at all five locations
Four normal spots + Golden

Use Pack-a-Punch at all four rotating spots and at Golden Pack-a-Punch in one game — normal PaP one gun, Golden a different one.

Wonder Weapons

Three in the box. The Tundragun and the Wunderwaffe each have a free route; the Thundergun is box-only.

Tundragun
Free: five snowball targets, in order

Throw a snowball at each target in this order; the Tundragun then appears in the box left of the last target.

Wunderwaffe DG-Scharfschütze
Free: icicle, pot, Vril Device, reward chest

Requirements

  • The zipline handle.
  1. Shoot down an icicle (one of three) and pick it up.
  2. Melt it in the cooking pot at the end of the Forecastle; after about a minute, take the key from the pot.
  3. Zipline to the Facility, Specimen Storage: open the safe on the desk — the Vril Device. Kill zombies beside it until it glows and stops taking souls, then take it.
  4. Give the Vril Device to the Hermit on his plank; he sends down a Reward Key. The reward chest is on the floor just right of the plank — the Wunderwaffe.
Needed in the main quest to shoot the two Facility batteries.

Snowballs & Dynamite
Infinite snowballs; dynamite from flame zombies

Snowballs freeze and shatter zombies, never run out (piles everywhere — hold use), and stack on top of your grenade. Totem challenges, the Tundragun, the orbs and the soup all use them.

  1. Dynamite parts — with snowballs equipped, snowball the flame zombies that spawn in normal rounds; each kill drops one of three parts.
  2. Build it — the reference uses the Gangway on the freighter, a short walk from Soda.
  3. Use it on a barricade (hold use, back off) — three on the map, plus the Seal's safe and the flinger barricade in the quests.

Pack-a-Punch

Three switches, a blue rock for the Hermit, and a machine that follows the lighthouse beam between four spots — plus the Golden Pack-a-Punch on the island, which maxes a gun for 5,000.

Power

Pack-a-Punch

  1. Docks and Bridge switches on.
  2. The blue rock. In the cave from the Lighthouse down to the Beach (Lighthouse Approach) a big blue rock sits on one of the walls — melee it and pick it up.
  3. Give it to the Hermit at the top of the Lighthouse: he winches a board down, put the rock on it. Pack-a-Punch now rotates between four spots — the lighthouse beam points at the current one.

Ziplines & Golden Pack-a-Punch

  1. Zipline 1 — Sun Deck. Find the crank wheel (three spots in the Stern), fit it to the crank and hold use until it stops asking.
  2. Zipline 2 — Main Deck. Crank wheel in one of three Forecastle spots.
  3. The zipline handle. Back at the top of the Lighthouse the Hermit sends it down. Ride the zipline on the Lighthouse's outer ring to the Facility.
  4. Facility power. Inside, down to the big vault and the lower level: flip the Human Infusion switch — Nikolai confirms there's power for Golden Pack-a-Punch.
  5. Golden Pack-a-Punch. When the lighthouse points out to sea (Nikolai usually says so), all players stand on the Sun Deck jump pad outside the Bridge power room. 5,000 fully upgrades a gun (instead of 15,000). The island floods with zombies while you're there — only go to upgrade.

Perk machines

Four machines, fixed spots. They're labelled by slot — Brew, Cola, Soda, Tonic — and each sells whatever perk you put in that loadout slot before the game.

The reference's quest loadout: Quick Revive (Brew), Stamin-Up (Cola), Dying Wish (Soda), Deadshot Dealer (Tonic); Ragnarok specialist for everyone.
