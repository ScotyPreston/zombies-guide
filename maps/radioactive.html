Attack of the Radioactive Thing

Infinite Warfare · 1950s atomic beach town

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4.
  • The crowbar (maintenance closet by the pool) and the Cleaver (dead shark in the market) get used constantly.
  • Pack-a-Punch open.
  • The Chem Solver tab handles the map's infamous math.

The full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Collect the five zombie body parts. All fixed spawns, any order:
    1. Arm 1 — the beach arm (where the power switch was). Build the Seismic Wave Generator and place it on the arm to force it up.
    2. Arm 2 — in a fire pit at the RV Camp.
    3. Leg 1 — in the tree outside the TV Station toward Racin' Stripes: bounce a grenade off the left branch and it drops.
    4. Leg 2 — round 5+: melee a green mist zombie with the Cleaver (from the dead shark in the Beachside Market).
    5. Torso — repair the freezer trap in the Beachside Market (part on the boxes outside the gas station garage), activate it, then melee the frozen meat.
    6. Head — during the Double-PaP fuse run, it's on a plate right in front of you in the RV.
  2. Gather the other parts.
    1. Chem station (3): the couch at the market's back entrance · an RV Camp picnic table · the table between the RV Camp and beach.
    2. Bomb (3): under the wooden bridge below the big bridge · under the red car's front passenger wheel · behind the chain-link fence by the leg tree.
    3. Mirrors (3): knock one off the car outside the market (crowbar) · smash the RV Camp bathroom mirror (crowbar) · give Elvira a filled green vial and take the mirror behind her couch.
  3. The bomb code safe. Read the 4-digit code under the desk near the market's safe (changes every game). With the crowbar, set the 4 green valves: each meter sweeps 0–9 — hit it on one of your digits (any order, all four digits used). The safe opens; write the bomb code down — you can't check Tab during the finale.
  4. Rebuild the zombie — get the garage key. Body parts on the spawn bed, the three mirrors around the room, then the punchcard from the spellbook room into the machine across from the bed. Guess the machine's 5-digit code: the sweeping dot marks positions 0–9, and the code always uses 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 — only the order changes. Right code → laser reassembles the zombie. Now enter the same code backwards and fire again: the body burns and drops the garage key. The garage (opposite the market) holds the chem board, the bomb, and the chem station — place your parts.
  5. The chemical step. The whole thing — O number, colours, boards, radio and the mixing math — lives on its own tab with a solver that does the arithmetic for you. When your final compound is mixed, add it to the bomb; if it's the one the radio named, the teleporter sequence unlocks.
  6. Crogzilla.

    Before you teleport

    • The bomb code memorised — no Tab menu in there.
    • Full setup: PaP'd guns, perks, gums.
    1. Escort the bomb down the beach — stay close to move it, dodge the constant fireballs.
    2. Deathrays: charge a beach deathray at the glowing chest spot — it only glows during his attacks. Aim slightly above it as the animation starts. 3–4 hits.
    3. Poisoned beach: start the teleport sequence at the bomb holder, then survive as poison floods the beach in waves, each spitting Crog pods. Fall back as it spreads.
    4. Lasers and the code: a timed laser field spawns — everyone navigates back to the bomb and interacts. Inside the Thing, every player enters the bomb code on a faster, blurrier version of the punchcard machine. Anyone fails, you redo the poison phase.

Power, Pack-a-Punch and Double Pack

Power

One switch — but it starts detached. Grab the switch box on the beach (facing the Radioactive Thing), carry it to the power area by Blue Bolts, and it flips itself.

Pack-a-Punch — do Elvira a favour

  1. The spellbook. Power on, then take the book from the now-open safe in the convenience store (the Skull Hop room).
  2. Give it to Elvira on her couch in the TV Station — she hands you a green vial.
  3. Fill the vial with Cleaver kills. The Cleaver is on the dead shark in the Beachside Market; kill until the vial reads full in the Tab menu.
  4. Walk her to the portal. Hand over the full vial and she follows you — lead her to the portal outside the TV Station near Bang Bangs, and she opens the way to Pack-a-Punch.

Double Pack-a-Punch

  1. Refill the green vial the same way — but don't give it to Elvira this time.
  2. The timed fuse grab. Crowbar in hand (pool maintenance closet), enter the PaP portal, hit the stoplight button on the left of the projector room, and leave — you spawn in a tiny RV. Immediately turn around, look up-right: crowbar the slightly-open cabinet and take the Alien Fuses. Fast — the timer is short.
  3. Fix the electric trap. The repair part is behind the bus across from the power entrance — slot it into the fuse box.
  4. Charge the fuses. With a full vial + fuses, stand mid-transformers until your screen shakes — place them there, run the electric trap, wait it out, and collect.
