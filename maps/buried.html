Buried

Black Ops 2 · an underground western town — and a very big friend

“Mined Games” has two versions — you help Richtofen or Maxis, one per game. Your side should match what you did on TranZit and Die Rise: finishing all three on ONE side unlocks the Victis endgame — . Either side rewards all perks for the rest of the game.

Requirements

  • 4 players, game on Original mode.
  • Paralyzer + Time Bomb from the Mystery Box — . Tip: feed Leroy candy at the box to lock it in place for the whole game — .
  • Galvaknuckles or Bowie Knife for the sign step.
  • Vulture Aid (church, 3000) — you literally cannot see the wisp without it.
  • Open all doors and clear the debris early — you’ll be crossing the map constantly. A full bank helps.

Richtofen’s steps

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Build the Guillotine. The bench is between the Saloon and the gunsmith building (the Mule Kick house). Careful: some parts fit BOTH the guillotine and Maxis’s gallows — build at the right bench.
    • Satellite Dish — upstairs in the Saloon, on the side nearest the guillotine bench, by the fence.
    • Spool of Wire — the gunsmith room downstairs (the chalk room), in the corner near the catacombs entrance.
    • Crystal — in the catacombs: go up the railing from the Saloon, turn left, take the next left, jump over the hole — it’s in the debris directly ahead.
    • Antenna — the barn downstairs, heading toward the catacombs, behind a hanging lantern.
  2. Charge the four red orbs with the Paralyzer. Shoot each dark red orb with the Paralyzer until it turns white (it keeps the red glow). They’re at:
    • The alleyway between the Saloon and the candy shop.
    • Left of the church entrance, behind a rock.
    • In the tunnel system above the town.
    • In the foliage by the mansion entrance, just off the maze.
  3. Knock down the lantern with a grenade. A purple lantern floats out of the courthouse window (toward the church or the jail). Cook a grenade 2–3 seconds, then throw it at the lantern — the blast drops it so you can pick it up. It fades if you leave it on the ground too long.
  4. Charge the lantern on the witches. Only the player carrying the lantern can charge it: kill about 10 witches in the haunted mansion. Every kill plays a rising pitch — a new, different sound means it’s full. Then place the lantern on the glowing symbol on the gunsmith roof (outside the Mule Kick room).
  5. Decode the wall puzzle. A three-line code appears on the wall right by the roof symbol. It’s a tic-tac-toe (pigpen) cipher spelling three of the five tunnel sign names. Match your wall lines against the chart and write the three names down in order.
  6. Melee the three signs, in order. The five named signs are posted around the tunnels. Hit YOUR three — in the order the wall gave them — with the Galvaknuckles or Bowie Knife.

    Each name written in the cipher, for matching against your wall:

  7. Walk the wisp home. Hitting the last sign correctly spawns a wisp next to it — visible only with Vulture Aid. Run into it and it jumps ahead; keep chasing it along its route: last sign → barn second level → jail second floor (past the wall-buy) → general store second floor → candy store → the guillotine. If nobody touches a wisp it fades and you redo the signs.
  8. Kill 5 charged zombies at the guillotine. With the wisp sitting at the guillotine, nearby zombies absorb it and glow blue. Kill five of them — each death sends an orb into the guillotine’s crystal. Richtofen then asks for a Time Bomb.
  9. Round ∞ — find the switch in the future. Place a Time Bomb ON the guillotine bench, gather all four players crouched around it, and detonate. You’re thrown into Round Infinity: black-and-white, ~90 seconds, and the zombies are unkillable — don’t waste ammo, just dodge. Search the crew’s dead bodies for the switch — it’s on a random one. Bodies can lie: in front of the guillotine · by the Saloon stairs · left of the gunsmith entrance · the crevice behind the barn/gunsmith · the jail entrance · between the general store and the box · right of the candy store · between the big rock at the gallows and the candy store awning · behind the church rock · past the church entrance by the workbench · the alley between the bank and the barn.
    Empty your hands first. Carrying a buildable part when you grab the switch can glitch it so it won’t place. And if time runs out without finding it: get another Time Bomb (Max Ammo re-arms it, or trade the box for Monkeys and back) and retry.

    Found it? Back in the present, place the switch on the guillotine.

  10. Pull the maze levers in the right order. In the hedge maze (behind the mansion) four gates carry colored switches: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow. Find the correct order by trial and error: pull all four, and the ones pulled in a correct position spark after the fourth. To reset for another try, everyone returns to town and runs back through the mansion together.
    Hints: the four jars behind the candy shop counter show a color order — a decent first guess, not guaranteed. If the yellow switch is missing (a known glitch), reset the maze the same way; it can also hide on a hedge in the maze center or near the fountain. Track which orders you’ve tried!
  11. Make a wish and shoot the targets. Have Leroy break the fountain in front of the church (send him at it with booze — ), then press the button on it to “make a wish”. Metal targets start spawning at four stations, one per player — each player must shoot every target at their station before it vanishes (~2 seconds; a lightning flash marks where the next appears — shoot the flash if you can’t see the target). Maxis’s steps

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Build the Gallows. The bench is next to the courthouse entrance. Careful: some parts fit BOTH the gallows and Richtofen’s guillotine — build at the right bench.
    • Battery — behind the altar in the church.
    • Spool of Wire — the room below Mule Kick (the gunsmith chalk room), corner near the catacombs entrance.
    • Antenna — the barn downstairs toward the catacombs, in one of the stables on the right.
    • Bulbs — the room above Leroy’s cell, on the boxes near the Mystery Box spawn.
  2. Break the four red orbs with the Subsurface Resonator. Place the Resonator (powered by a Turbine) at each orb and destroy it. Same four spots as Richtofen’s side:
    • The alleyway between the Saloon and the candy shop.
    • Left of the church entrance, behind a rock.
    • In the tunnel system above the town.
    • In the foliage by the mansion entrance, just off the maze.
    Give Leroy candy next to the Resonator and he’ll fetch the Turbine to it himself — .
  3. Knock down the lantern with a grenade. Same as the other side: the purple lantern floats from the courthouse; cook a grenade 2–3 seconds and drop it with the blast, then pick it up before it fades.
  4. Sugar Rush — charge the lantern the Maxis way. No witches this time: the lantern charges on zombies killed by Leroy (after candy), the Trample Steam, the Subsurface Resonator, the Head Chopper, or a Nuke — and the lantern carrier must stand close to the kills. When it’s full, place it on the chalk symbol on the gunsmith roof.
  5. Decode the wall puzzle. The three-line tic-tac-toe cipher appears by the roof symbol — it spells three of the five tunnel sign names. Write them down in order.
  6. Melee the three signs, in order with the Galvaknuckles or Bowie Knife.

    The five signs, each written in the cipher:

  7. Escort the wisp to the gallows — twice. Prep first: place a Time Bomb, open the 1250-point couch between the tunnels and the gunsmith upper level, and have Leroy break the barrier between the general store and the candy shop. Then the sign-hitter follows the wisp its whole route — tunnels → courtroom → past Speed Cola toward the jail → loop up the jail's top floor toward the barn → down through the tunnels → out the gunsmith upper level → Saloon → candy shop → courthouse → the gallows, powering one container. The catch: the wisp only survives while zombies are near it — stage zombie “checkpoints” at the jail, the gunsmith and the Saloon (or start a fresh round as it moves so spawns follow you).
    One run powers one container — now detonate the Time Bomb to travel back and do the whole escort again for the second one.
  8. Ring the bells. Nine bells in three buildings, three each:
    • Courthouse, ground floor — left of the judge’s podium · on the table right of the podium · in the corner immediately left as you enter.
    • Candy shop, second floor (not the power switch room) — corner of the square table by the wall · the table with two pots near the couch blockade · the chair right of the stairway door.
    • Barn, top level — hay bale before the jail drop-down · hay bale right of the wall hole near the gunsmith · hay bale right of the railing gap.

    Behind the secret bookcase in the mansion sits a switchboard with a 3×3 light grid — columns left to right = candy shop / barn / courthouse. One player works the board and calls out which light glows; the other three stand ready at the bells. Ring the called bell in time and the light turns green and the next lights up; too slow and the board resets. Work through all nine.

    The board operator is stuck in the mansion with the witch spawning nonstop — Galvaknuckles drop her in one hit.
  9. Make a wish and shoot the targets. Same finale as Richtofen’s side: Leroy breaks the fountain by the church, wish on it, and each player clears every target at their station — Saloon · Jugger-Nog/box · mansion windows · candy store opposite the church.

    Done = “Mined Games” complete, all perks for the rest of the game — and the lobby’s Buried icon glows orange. The story finale lives in .

Leroy — the friendly Giant

Buried’s signature mechanic: a huge friendly prisoner who smashes debris, fights zombies, and even builds for you — if you keep him in booze and candy.

Freeing him

Leroy is in a jail cell near the first Mystery Box spawn (head up the stairs to the Sheriff’s House, jump across to the Jailhouse, and drop through the floor). Grab the key from the wall by the cell and open it. He ignores you until you hand him booze (there’s a bottle right by his cell) — then he smashes the debris off his cell and starts pacing between his cell and the Saloon.

Don’t shoot him. Wounding him sends him back to his cell, locked in — you’ll need the key again. It respawns on one of four hangers: left of his cell · the judge’s bench in the courthouse · the middle room upstairs in the Saloon · behind the deposit box in the bank. (Melee is fine — a whack just makes him sprint for a bit, handy for steering him.)
The key, booze and candy share the chalk slot — you can’t hold them and a wall-gun chalk at the same time.

Booze

Give him booze and he charges off in the opposite direction, smashing any debris in his path — this is how you open the blocked passages (including the church). The further he runs, the more bonus points you get. He flattens zombies and players on the way. Booze spawns in his cell and in the Saloon once per round; after you’ve used one, buy another for 1000 at the Saloon counter.

Candy

The Endgame — Easter Egg TrackerThe Victis story finale — TranZit + Die Rise + Buried

At the back of the courthouse ground floor is a box with a 3×4 light grid — the Easter Egg Tracker. Each row is a player; the columns are Tower of Babble (TranZit) · High Maintenance (Die Rise) · Mined Games (Buried). Blue = done for Richtofen, orange = done for Maxis.

Requirements

  • All four players have finished all three easter eggs on the same side.
  • All Nav Card tables built with the correct Nav Cards inserted.

With twelve lights in one color: shoot the tracker and press the button it reveals.

  • Richtofen’s ending: Maxis is erased; Richtofen rides along in Stuhlinger’s head. Reward: permanent Fire Sale + a Mule Kick variant that carries four weapons.
  • Maxis’s ending: Maxis takes the Aether and stuffs Richtofen into a random zombie — all eyes turn orange. The one blue-eyed “Richtofen zombie” drops a power-up every time it’s killed.
Warning: pressing it erases everyone’s easter egg progress. With all three done and the cards placed, you can also press “use” near the tracker to play the hidden song Samantha’s Lullaby.

The ghost pianistDartboard bullseye → free perk

Hit a bullseye on the Saloon dartboard with a Ballistic Knife and the piano behind you starts playing itself. Leave the maze through the back of the mansion and you’ll find a ghost at the piano — tip him 10 points and he hands you a free perk. (You can also just play the Saloon piano yourself by tapping Use.)

The Haunted MansionShe takes 2000 a swipe

Inside the mansion a ghostly woman spawns relentlessly and steals 2000 points per hit — fast, but fragile (Galvaknuckles one-shot her). Fight all the way through the mansion and kill every ghost on the run and a random perk bottle drops at the end.

The LSAT catwalkOne jump, one chance

An LSAT sits on a catwalk above the starting area — a precisely timed jump gets you up for the 2000-point buy, and the catwalk collapses right after, so it’s once per game for one player. Pack-a-Punch it into the FSIRT and hold it for the “FSIRT Against the Wall” achievement.

Chalk wall-buysDraw your own guns — get paid for it

Chalk outlines in the gunsmith shop can be carried to blank wall spots around the map and drawn there, creating a wall-buy of that gun for everyone — and paying YOU 1000 points per chalk (2000 for the final one, doubled during Double Points). Feed Leroy candy near the chalk and he’ll draw three of them at random spots himself.

Bank & Weapon LockerShared across all three Victis maps

The bank (deposit/withdraw points) and the weapon locker (store a gun) work across TranZit, Die Rise and Buried — whatever you stashed there is waiting here. If a stored gun doesn’t exist on this map, you get its closest equivalent.

Time Bomb trick: place a bomb, withdraw your savings, spend everything opening the map — then detonate. Doors stay open, and the money is back in the bank.

ParalyzerMystery Box — infinite ammo, and it flies

Fires a beam that slows, freezes and then kills everything in a cone. Infinite ammo — the catch is an overheat gauge on the gun: hit 115 and it locks up until it cools back to 100 (about six seconds of continuous fire per burst).

It flies. Point it at the ground, fire, and press jump — you ride the beam upward as long as you hold the trigger. That’s how you reach rooftops and skip debris (including into the blocked church). Watch the landing: fall damage still applies — a quick blast at the ground just before touchdown breaks the fall.
  • You can lift a teammate: they jump into your beam. It cuts out if they cross your crosshair.
  • With Vulture Aid, ammo drops cool it faster.
  • Pack-a-Punched it becomes the Petrifier — roughly ten seconds of fire before overheating.
  • Required for Richtofen’s easter egg (the orbs) .
Ray Gun Mark IIMystery Box — the Ray Gun, evolved

Debuted on Buried (and available on every BO2 map). Fires three-round bursts with no splash damage — so no more blowing yourself up in a corner — and it punches straight through whole trains of zombies.

  • Pack-a-Punched: Porter’s Mark II Ray Gun — double magazine, more ammo, red rays.
  • You can’t hold it and the original Ray Gun at the same time — the box will never offer you the second one.
Time BombMystery Box — a save point in your pocket

Throw it down like C4 and a golden watch appears in your inventory. Detonate the watch and everyone rewinds to the moment and place the bomb was planted — same round, same weapons, perks and points. An icon above the round counter shows which round you’ll return to.

  • One use per bomb. A Max Ammo lets you place a fresh one.
  • It rewinds bank/locker deposits — but NOT opened doors, Leroy’s demolition, buildables, or easter egg progress. (That’s the free-money trick .)
  • Both easter egg sides need it — Richtofen’s Round ∞ and Maxis’s double wisp run .

Four buildables — and every single part spawns inside the General Store, so one building stocks them all. Build each at any workbench. The lazy way: feed Leroy candy next to a bench and he gathers the parts and builds it himself — .

Pack-a-Punch

5000 points per upgrade. The machine lives past the haunted mansion, through the hedge maze behind it — the same maze as the easter egg levers, with the fountain courtyard at its heart.

  1. Get into the mansion — it’s the big house beyond the church side of town. The ghost woman spawns nonstop inside and steals 2000 points per swipe; run through or one-shot her with Galvaknuckles.
  2. Out the back and into the maze. The hedge walls shift between games; keep pushing inward.
  3. The machine is at the maze’s center, by the fountain courtyard.
Shortcut: with the Paralyzer you can point it down, fire, and simply fly over debris and fences on the way — the fastest PaP commute on the map. Clearing every ghost on a full mansion run also pays out a random perk bottle.
