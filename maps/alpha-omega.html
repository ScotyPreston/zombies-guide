Alpha Omega

Black Ops 4 · Nuketown, overrun

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4. Normal difficulty, no modifiers.
  • Power on and Pack-a-Punch open.
  • The Telepads built.
  • A Brain Rot gun (double Pack-a-Punch) for step 9; Galvaknuckles (5,000, bought during step 2).

"Electromagnetic Awakening Party" — do everything Rushmore tells you, until he gives you the Elemental Shard. Talk to Rushmore between every step — his monitors are in Operations.

The full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Report to Rushmore. With Pack-a-Punch open, finish a round — someone starts yelling at you to report to Operations. Hold use on the big green monitors.
  2. The static TV and the Galvaknuckles. One of four TVs in the bunker shows a white static screen (don't confuse it with the Ray Gun TVs — only one spawns, the others don't matter). Then buy the door on the truck outside the Yellow House and the Galvaknuckles for 5,000 inside. Bring a zombie to the static TV and kill it with a Galvaknuckle punch in front of the screen — the TV reads out five letter + number codes. Turn subtitles on and record a clip; you need all five.
  3. Set the clocks, in the order you heard them. Each code is a house letter and a time ("D, 0745" = Transfusion Facility, 7:45). Go to each house in the order read out, hold use on its clock and turn the hands to that time. Rushmore says "security clearance granted" after each correct one.
  4. The sixth clock and the keypad. One house letter was never read out: its clock spins and stops on a random time. Turn that time into four digits (12:00 → 1200) and enter it on Rushmore's keypad (hold use per digit, then Enter). That unlocks every house trap and opens free gun lockers in Operations and Prisoner Holding — take them. Rushmore still "denies access" — that's correct.
  5. Walk the red crawler home. Talk to Rushmore first or the step won't trigger, and do it as early as you can — it only gets harder. A red-glowing Nova crawler spawns in the bunker (Rushmore comments when it's near). Don't shoot it: walk it to the Transfusion Facility, staying close or it loses interest and heads back. It hops through the window through the archway on the left wall inside the front door. Killed it or lost it = next round.
  6. Four authorisation codes — wake Sergeant A.D.A.M. Rushmore wants a missing canister back, which means activating A.D.A.M. Find four papers (note the name at the top of each):
    • Operation Toy Soldier — desk left of Rushmore. Always 7626.
    • Sawyer — APD Interrogation, one of three desks.
    • McCain — APD Control (the bunker under Interrogation): shoot the stack of papers on the desk.
    • Pernell — take the key from the key box in Solitary (right of the mannequin's front desk), then open the Yellow House upstairs desk drawer.
    Enter 7626 first, then the codes in the order Rushmore names them ("McCain authorization code required…"). "Sergeant A.D.A.M. has been activated."
  7. Marlton's door and the canister. Green House backyard, down the first flight of bunker stairs: behind barrels, left of the graffiti "Should have nuked this place when I had the chance", is a big metal door. Melee it — Marlton talks. Go to Storage, find the Sgt. A.D.A.M. poster and buy the Sergeant for free at the door right of it. Back at the metal door, wait for A.D.A.M. to arrive; Marlton panics and throws a canister through. Put it on the shelf with the other canisters in the Transfusion Facility.
  8. The server run. Place one telepad in the Power House left of the switch and the other by the Brew perk. Left of the Power House telepad a fuse sits behind glass: lure a Jolting Jack into zapping the glass (dodge the shot) and it lifts. Grab the server and sprint to Operations through the telepads before it overheats; place it on the server wall by Rushmore. Failed = wait a few seconds, grab it again, same round.
  9. Brain Rot codes. Three pictures leak green mist. Shoot a zombie nearby with a Brain Rot gun — it knocks the picture down, revealing a code behind it. Collect all three, then enter them on Rushmore's keypad quickly, one after another.
  10. Restore the power. The lights go out. Set six bunker power boxes: Diner UP · Beds UP · Lounge DOWN · Generators UP · Solitary DOWN · Storage DOWN, then flip the switch in the Power House again.
  11. Three sparking mannequins. New mannequins sparking blue appear (backyards of the Green and Yellow houses, the Diner, the Lounge, behind the Transfusion Facility and Prisoner Holding). Interact → ~1 minute lockdown → it drops an arm or a head. Three mannequins = two arms and a head. Fit them to the mannequin in the chair in APD Interrogation; the round pauses for dialogue.
  12. Follow the blue orb. A blue orb is somewhere on the map, usually in a zombie spawn window. Check every window and door; once found, stay with it as it drifts to APD Interrogation (lose it = next round). It enters the mannequin — more story — then one last talk with Rushmore unlocks the fight.
  13. The Avogadro. Full shield, all perks, PaP'd guns, Monkey Bombs, and ideally a Porter's Mark II-V. Everyone holds use on the central console in APD Control.
    • Charge one corner canister of the APD pyramid by killing the A.D.A.M. units that now spawn near it. The pyramid opens: the Avogadro. A nuke seals the surface — you're in the bunker for good.
    • Four soul canisters — the four monitors in APD Control show the order, left to right: Lounge, Diner, Beds, Storage. Hold use on one, then kill A.D.A.M.s and dogs beside it. The Avogadro chases and shocks — keep the room between you; if he shocks a canister, wait for it to come back. Full = Rushmore speaks, Max Ammo + Carpenter drop — grab them and leave fast, he electrifies the area. Beds is always last.
    • After Beds, run through Generators back to APD Control and wait for him. Monkey Bomb, then melee — he kneels; shooting him knocks him in the direction you fire. Melee and push him to the foot of the APD stairs until he's sucked back in.
    • Use the console again to teleport him; the APD opens with the Elemental Shard inside. Grab it — cutscene.

Ray Gun Mark II variants

Four craftable Ray Guns, each from an alternate ammo type on a re-packed gun plus a canister charged with souls. Build the Assembly Kit and unlock the frames first; each variant Pack-a-Punches into its Porter's version.

The frames — do this first
Rushmore, four TVs, a four-digit code

Requirements

  • Pack-a-Punch open, then one more round.
  • The Assembly Kit built.
  1. Talk to Rushmore — the face on the big green monitors in Operations.
  2. Four TVs, one at a time. Whichever TV shows static, hold use: a Ray Gun image appears, and you feed it souls with nearby kills. Full = it shows a number. Write the numbers down in the order you get them; the static moves to the next TV at random.
  3. Enter the four digits on the keypad left of Rushmore (look at a key, hold use; then Enter). The cabinet behind you lifts — take a frame before starting any variant.
Ray Gun Mark II-V
Electric beam — Kill-O-Watt
  1. Get a gun with Kill-O-Watt (re-pack at Pack-a-Punch). In the Generators area one panel glows yellow — shoot it with the Kill-O-Watt gun and take the canister.
  2. Outside, one of five light poles sparks yellow: shoot the metal at the top so it holds a yellow orb. Do all five and a beam links them.
  3. Hold use on the generator in front of the Yellow House to seat the canister, then stay in the yellow circle and get kills until it stops taking souls. Take it to the Assembly Kit upstairs in Operations and craft the Mark II-V.
Ray Gun Mark II-X
Full-auto — Cryofreeze
  1. Get a Cryofreeze gun. One cabinet leaks blue mist: shoot it with the Cryofreeze gun and take the canister.
  2. In the bunker, zombies spawn with a blue glow: freeze one, melee it, and collect the blue goo it drops. Three times.
  3. Place the canister on the APD Control table by the big canisters; kills inside the blue circle until it's full, then craft the Mark II-X at the Assembly Kit.
Ray Gun Mark II-Y
Explosive charge — Brain Rot
  1. Get a Brain Rot gun. One dirt pile leaks green mist: shoot a zombie near it with Brain Rot and it digs up the canister.
  2. A golden orb floats over one of the telepad pads: place a telepad there and shoot the orb — it teleports. Find it again, do the same, and this time jump into the telepad after it. The orb flies to Storage.
  3. Seat the canister in Storage, kills in the orange circle until full, craft the Mark II-Y.
Ray Gun Mark II-Z
Shotgun — Fire Bomb
  1. Get a Fire Bomb gun. One kitchen cabinet leaks orange mist: shoot it, it burns away, take the canister.
  2. One house outside puffs purple smoke from its chimney: land a grenade on top of the chimney to snuff it and light the fireplace inside. (The reference shows the throw on video.)
  3. Kills in the purple circle inside that house until the canister is full, then craft the Mark II-Z.

Pack-a-Punch

Power, a bunker lockdown, then four gas vents to clear by hand.

  1. Power. The switch is in the Power House in the Cul-de-Sac — the house with the open garage behind the Nuketown sign.
  2. The lockdown. Behind the houses, down into the bunkers; doors either side of the Pack-a-Punch machine lead to Generators. Hold use on the console and survive the lockdown — gas crawlers and sprinters. It ends with "ventilation systems could not be cleared".
  3. Clear the four vents. Back up top, four of the six houses leak green gas. Each has a vent unit in its backyard or garage (Prisoner Holding's is by the back door); a unit that needs clearing glows neon green. Hold use on it.
    Several fast gas zombies spawn on you the moment you touch a valve — step off and deal with them rather than die at the vent.
  4. Pack-a-Punch is live in the Beds area of the bunker.

Perk machines

Four machines, fixed spots. They're labelled by slot — Brew, Cola, Soda, Tonic — and each sells whatever perk you put in that loadout slot before the game.

The reference's quest loadout: Quick Revive (Brew), Stamin-Up (Cola), Dying Wish (Soda), Deadshot Dealer (Tonic); Ragnarok specialist for everyone.
