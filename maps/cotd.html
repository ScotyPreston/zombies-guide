Call of the Dead

Black Ops 1 · Siberian shipwreck

The original crew is locked behind the vault door and you're breaking them out. There are two versions of the quest — solo skips four co-op-only steps. Pick yours:

Requirements

  • Players: 1 — this is the shortened solo version of the quest.
  • The V-R11 from the Mystery Box (needed near the end). Semtex makes the generator step much easier.

"Stand-in" — full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Turn on the power. The switch is on the bridge, on the top level of the ship.
  2. Fix the missing fuse. Knife the vault door in the room below PhD Flopper and listen to the crew — Richtofen explains a fuse is broken. Search the room above and this floor for the fuse, place it in the slot next to the door, then knife the door again.
  3. Knock out the 4 security generators. Nikolai trips the security system; find all four generators (look for the red beacon) and destroy them. Easiest way: buy Semtex and stick one on top of each.
  4. Set the ship's wheel and levers. On the bridge, turn the wheel until the brown peg points to the bottom right. Then use the 3 levers beside it: pull the left lever once and the right lever 3 times.
  5. The human sacrifice. Get the V-R11 from the Mystery Box and shoot a zombie on the bottom floor of the lighthouse. It turns into a human, runs into the green light in the spiral staircase and floats up screaming — kill the human before it reaches the top.
  6. Send the crew to Paradise. The Vril Device floats down to the bottom of the lighthouse staircase. Grab it, carry it to the vault door, and put it in the same tube the steps used earlier. After Richtofen's dialogue, knife the fuse box one last time — that completes "Stand-in".
    Reward: a Wunderwaffe DG-2 (lasts until the ammo is gone) — and from now on George drops a Wunderwaffe instead of a Death Machine when you kill him.

Requirements

  • Players: 2–4. The vodka step needs one player knifing and one catching.
  • The V-R11 from the Mystery Box (needed near the end). Semtex makes the generator step much easier.

"Ensemble Cast" — full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Turn on the power. The switch is on the bridge, on the top level of the ship.
  2. Fix the missing fuse. Knife the vault door in the room below PhD Flopper and listen to the crew — Richtofen explains a fuse is broken. Search the room above and this floor for the fuse, place it in the slot next to the door, then knife the door again.
  3. Knock out the 4 security generators. Nikolai trips the security system; find all four generators (look for the red beacon) and destroy them. Easiest way: buy Semtex and stick one on top of each.
  4. Get Nikolai his vodka. The bottle hangs frozen in the ice in 1 of 4 spots. One player knifes the ice while another stands underneath to catch the bottle. Then place it in the receptacle tube to the left of the vault door.
  5. Press the 4 radios in order. Hit them in exactly this order — done right, a light in the sky starts flashing Morse code.
  6. Set the ship's wheel and levers. On the bridge, turn the wheel until the brown peg points to the bottom right. Then use the 3 levers beside it: pull the left lever once and the right lever 3 times.
  7. Press the 4 foghorns in order. Done right, a green beam shines from the submarine to the top of the lighthouse.
  8. Set the lighthouse dials. Each of the lighthouse's 4 floors has a dial — top to bottom they are Yellow, Orange, Blue, Purple. Turning one dial also spins others, which is what makes this the hardest step. This order sorts it out:
    1. Turn Yellow to 2
    2. Turn Blue until Orange reads 7
    3. Turn Purple to 6
    4. Turn Orange until Blue reads 4
    5. Turn Yellow back to 2
  9. The human sacrifice. Get the V-R11 from the Mystery Box and shoot a zombie on the bottom floor of the lighthouse. It turns into a human, runs into the green light in the spiral staircase and floats up screaming — kill the human before it reaches the top.
  10. Send the crew to Paradise. The Vril Device floats down to the bottom of the lighthouse staircase. Grab it, carry it to the vault door, and put it in the same tube the steps used earlier. After Richtofen's dialogue, knife the fuse box one last time — that completes "Ensemble Cast".
    Reward: a Wunderwaffe DG-2 (lasts until the ammo is gone) — and from now on George drops a Wunderwaffe instead of a Death Machine when you kill him.

Wonder Weapons

Nothing to build on this map. The V-R11 and the Scavenger both come out of the Mystery Box for 950 — keep spinning until one drops. The Wunderwaffe DG-2 is the only one you can't buy: it's the main quest reward.

V-R11
Mystery Box · 950

Turns a zombie back into a human. The human runs off and pulls the horde after him, which makes this the best panic button on the map — and it's the weapon the main quest needs.

It does almost no damage on its own — carry it alongside a killing weapon, not instead of one.

Upgraded — V-R11 Lazarus

Bigger magazine and a wider blast. Shoot a zombie to turn it human, then shoot that human a second time and it explodes, taking out everything that followed it — that's the Stuntman achievement.

Scavenger
Mystery Box · 950

A bolt-action explosive sniper. Rounds stick where they land and blow up a moment later, so it clears whole trains at once and stays useful deep into high rounds.

One shot into a big crowd from across the map is the Shooting on Location achievement — 10 kills with a single shot from over 100 feet.

Upgraded — Hyena Infra-dead

Double the magazine and reserve ammo, and a much bigger explosion.

Wunderwaffe DG-2
Main quest reward — not in the box

Chains lightning between zombies and wipes an entire train in one shot. On this map you can't get it from the Mystery Box — there are only two ways:

  • Finish the main quest — every player is handed one.
  • After the quest is done, George Romero drops one every time you kill him (instead of the Death Machine).
The quest-reward one lasts only until the ammo runs out, so save it for a round you actually need it in.

Upgraded — Wunderwaffe DG-3 JZ

More ammo and a longer chain between targets.

Pack-a-Punch

To find the Pack-a-Punch, use the lighthouse — wherever its beam is pointing is where the machine is.
