Dead of the Night

Black Ops 4 · Alistair Rhodes' manor, 1912

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4.
  • Pack-a-Punch open.
  • Silver bullets crafted.
  • An Alistair's Annihilator.

Three quest lines, three stone slabs, one invisible Werewolf.

The full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Reactivate the crystals. Go back to the three crystals that opened Pack-a-Punch (Wine Cellar, Library area, Bedroom area) and interact again. Each shows a vision — a Knight armour, a Telescopic lens, or a Wooden effigy — unlocking that quest line. The three lines are independent, any order; all three must be finished. Each pays out a stone slab.
  2. LENS LINE — the antenna. Every player shoots the antenna on the Mausoleum roof at the same time, with silver bullets. It vanishes and a light beam fires at the Mansion.
  3. LENS LINE — the Atlas rings. Three panels in the North Atrium spin the rings over the Atlas statue. The community solution: middle wheel left until GREEN lights → left wheel left until BLUE → right wheel left until RED — repeat that cycle until it solves. The crystal lights all three colours and bursts.
  4. LENS LINE — zodiac and scratches. Find 3 zodiac symbols among 7 possible rooms (Billiards, Dining, Entrance Hall, Library, Main Hall, Trophy, Wine Cellar). A symbol in a room means that room also hides 3 scratch marks — count every scratch and add them up for that symbol's number.
    Enter the three symbols on the circular panel upstairs in the Greenhouse Lab, least scratches first — knife to confirm each. A wrong code reshuffles every symbol and scratch on the map.
  5. LENS LINE — the telescope. Shield in hand: crank the Greenhouse roof open and bash the crank to lock it (release too early and it closes). Run everyone through the electric trap downstairs with shields up, then bash the telescope together — it fires into the sky. A hatch opens below: pull the stone slab out, then everyone interacts — survive the lockdown and take slab #1 plus a Max Ammo.
  6. KNIGHT LINE — blue fire runs. Put an energy core in a fire trap (the Library doorframe is the usual pick), activate it, and shoot the flames with the Alistair's Annihilator — they turn blue. Shield-bash 4 fireplaces per run, in order, three runs total — each run's last fireplace gives a crystal:
    Run 1: Smoking room → Library (down the stairs) → Library (other side of the door) → Billiards.
    Run 2: Main Hall (by the Mozu) → East Gallery (by the Strife) → Main Hall (facing the Mozu fireplace) → West Gallery (by the Essex).
    Run 3: Trophy → Bedroom → Music room → Dining. Be quick from the first ignition.
  7. KNIGHT LINE — the three knights. Interact with the knight statues — Mansion (Main Hall–Dining hallway), Graveyard (foot of the Mausoleum stairs), Gardens (Greenhouse terrace corner). Each releases a crystal that follows you — walk each to its matching colour circle in the Forest. Then kill zombies near the three ghost knights to march them to Pack-a-Punch, where they form a triangle: kill a Werewolf inside it. Slab appears — everyone interacts, survive, take slab #2.
  8. EFFIGY LINE. In the Graveyard, 5 white trees drop leaves — shoot the lowest branch of each (aim at the trunk if the branch won't register) and collect them to build the effigy. Find the tomb dated 1912 — the player whose name is on it enters spirit mode. Fire-shot the ground before the effigy with the Annihilator, and the spirit player interacts with the burning effigy → afterlife (invisible to players, not to zombies). Find the ghost lady hidden in the Mansion (windows and perk machines count), escort her back to the effigy, and she reveals slab #3 — everyone interacts, survive, collect.
  9. The Werewolf boss.

    Before the gate

    • Ammo, perks, shield, silver bullets — there is no coming back.
    All players hold interact on the stone gate in the Forest. The arena holds an invisible Werewolf:
    1. Stage 1: one floor tile glows green. Rotate the corner statues so their light beams hit the green tile, lure the boss onto it — he turns visible and vulnerable. Unload.
    2. Stage 2: he leaves; survive everything the map owns until he returns.
    3. Stage 3: same trap — but the green tile is invisible until a statue beam sweeps it. Trap him, finish him.

Side Easter Eggs

Hidden room 1 — the Billiards closet
Sink the balls 1 to 9

Push every ball into a pocket in number order, 1 through 9 (colours are always the same — 1 yellow, 2 blue, 3 pink...). The secret closet opens with free points, an elixir, or a weapon.

Hidden room 2 — the Wine Cellar
Knife the leaks, fastest first

6 leaking barrels drip at different speeds. Knife them from fastest leak to slowest — right order opens a secret room (sometimes free silver bullets); wrong order locks it for the game.

Wonder Weapons & secret gear

Alistair's Folly
The acid pistol — box, or the symbol code

From the Mystery Box — or free from its case in the Library by entering a 4-symbol code. Each colour has 4 possible spots and 4 possible shapes (A, D, W or Z-shaped); symbols change every game and repeats are possible.

Chaos Theory
Upgrade 1 — charged acid shots
  1. Kill a Zombie Werewolf with silver bullets — it drops the Chaos material.
  2. Run it through Alistair's machine in the Greenhouse centre; take the Prima Materia from the side.
  3. Shield-punch the odd bookshelf on the Library's left side for part two.
  4. Craft at the back-right Greenhouse workbench. Charged shots (3 ammo) make a stunning acid pool or a horde-clearing tornado.
Alistair's Annihilator
Upgrade 2 — required for the main quest
  1. The bat. On the fence between Mausoleum and Graveyard, one lamp glows orange — shoot it with the Chaos Theory; the orange moves to another lamp; keep shooting until a bat appears — shoot it mid-flight for the Bat Chaos Materia. Missed? It retries in about a minute.
  2. The Nosferatu. Kill vampires with the Chaos Theory for 4 shots of bile, then interact with the large tomb at the Graveyard's left entrance — kill the Nosferatu that answers for his Materia.
  3. The digger. In the Forest, blue mushrooms mark small dirt piles. Green-shot a ZOMBIE standing on one (shoot the zombie, not the ground) — it digs for you. One of three mounds holds the part.
  4. Machine both Materias, then craft at the back-left Greenhouse workbench. Adds a scope, a shrinking green portal shot, and a fireball.
Stake Knife
Secret melee — one-shots to round 26
  1. Read the tree code. In the Forest (left path below PaP, by the Koshka), the lantern tree plus three background trees show 4 alchemical symbols — read left to right. Changes every game.
  2. Shoot the Garden tiles in that order. One wrong tile = no Stake Knife this game.
  3. Bowie-knife the four symbols on the second tree (slightly downhill), same order — then shield-bash all four in any order. A branch falls; take it.
  4. Feed it at the Mausoleum altar — place the branch facing the perk machine and kill 10–15 Nosferatus until it glows red, then craft at the workbench by the left Cemetery entrance.
Savage Impaler
Secret twin crossbows
  1. Blow out 6 candles by knifing the flame tips: East Gallery table · Music room table · East hallway desk · Alistair's bedside · Basement bench · Study table. Audio cue per candle.
  2. The red tombstone. Right of PaP in the Forest, a clouded tombstone puts you in an all-zombies-chase-you state (once per round each). While in it, find the ghost lady outside the Mansion windows and escort her to the Mausoleum — may take several activations.
  3. Kill her possessed zombies the right way, in order: regular weapon → fire → specialist weapon → ballistic shield. Pick up each dropped item.
  4. Place the 4 items in the Mausoleum circles, kill ~8 Nosferatus inside the big circle until it turns blue, then sprint down the right stairs and open the tomb on your right — the crypt opens.
  5. Take the Impaler — and survive the Nosferatu lock-in. One free pickup per game; everyone else gets it in the box.

Traps & fire cores
The fire traps need feeding

The electric trap in the Greenhouse works from the start. The fire traps on the doorframes each need a Fire Core before they'll take your points — cores lie all over the map (benches, couches, the Mausoleum stairs, the piano bench, the Gardens...).

The Knight quest line eats one core — keep a spare in mind.

Pack-a-Punch

  1. Reach the Sentinel Artifact — open the two doors from spawn to the main hall balcony. It acts as this map's power.
  2. Find and feed the three crystals. Each hides in a jar (knife jars with glowing particles), then feed it a few zombie kills nearby:
  3. Do each crystal's mini-quest for a Tuning Fork. Interact with the fed crystal for a vision, then:
    • Grandfather clock: find the interactive one (Main Hall / Dining / Billiards), stand in its circle ~1 minute, take the fork.
    • Doll: Entrance room by Danu's machine — follow the ghost.
    • Painting: find the painting from the vision (East Gallery / Music / Lounge / Smoking), follow the ghost.
    • Perk machine: approach the one from the vision, kill the 5 vampires in the mist.
    • Rope: the Wine Cellar chair — follow the ghost.
    • Alistair's testament: the container above the Library — follow the ghost.
  4. Open the Forest. With all 3 forks, interact with the Dark Matter mass on the Forest terrace (door below spawn). Pack-a-Punch waits at the Forest's far end — and a Zombie Werewolf comes with it.
