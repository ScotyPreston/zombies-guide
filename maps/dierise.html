Die Rise

Black Ops 2 · a collapsed skyscraper — don’t look down

“High Maintenance” has two versions — you help Richtofen or Maxis, one per game, and your side should match TranZit and Buried. The first six steps are identical on both sides; they split at step 7. Either way the reward is all perks for the rest of the game and one more light on the Victis tracker.

Requirements

  • 4 players on vanilla — on PC (Plutonium) the solo mod trims the group steps down to one player.
  • Galvaknuckles (below the elevator shaft toward the Buddha Room) for the finale.
  • A sniper rifle — the SVU wall-buy in the department store works.

Richtofen’s steps

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Build the Trample Steam. All four parts are around the spawn lobby and the hallway past the first door; the bench is at the bottom of the escalator outside spawn. Part spawns pictured in the . In co-op, every player should build and carry one — you’ll need four placed later.
  2. Turn on the power. Work across to the dragon-statue building and head downstairs to the big room full of sewing machines — the switch is in there (follow the power icons on the walls). This wakes the elevators up, which the whole quest depends on.
  3. Build the Nav Card Table. The bench is on the dragon rooftop, under the pylon — and all four parts are on that same rooftop:
    • 115 Meteorite — in the trash pile in front of a window, by the AC units.
    • Wooden Table — directly behind the Nav Card Table bench, under the giant dragon statue.
    • Radio — in the corner by the destroyed water tower.
    • Power Box — in dead man’s corner, right of the zombie barrier.
  4. Stand on the four elevator symbols — together. Four golden symbols sit on top of four elevators. All four players stand on one each at the same time (solo mod: just one). They glow while you’re on them.
  5. Stand on the floor symbols in the right order. Four symbols appear on the ground. Trial and error: stand on one — if it glows, it’s first in the sequence. Keep hunting for the second, third, fourth; a wrong symbol resets the lit ones. Done when all four stay lit.
  6. Snipe the orbs out of the dragons’ mouths. With a scoped sniper, aim at the two dragon statues above the Nav-table rooftop — each has an orb in its teeth. Shoot both; fireworks confirm it.
  7. Build the Sliquifier. Bench is at the bottom of the stairs below the power switch; all four parts live in that building. Full part guide: .
  8. Sliquify the lobby statues. The two statues flanking the spawn-lobby door each take 20 Sliquifier shots (40 total). An orb starts spinning above each statue when it’s done.
  9. Trample Steams on the four zombie symbols — then 15 kills. Four floor symbols with a zombie mark the spots. Each player places a Trample Steam on one, facing the pylon — placed right, it doesn’t fling you. Then feed them: 15 zombies flung toward the pylon. The pylon glows blue when it’s had enough. (Solo mod: one Trample Steam.)
  10. Find your 8 Mahjong tiles. Eight tiles spawn across 11 possible spots. Four show a direction (N/E/S/W), four show a number (1–4). Match direction to number by tile color — if North and 3 are both green, North is third in your sequence. The chart below reads the symbols for you; note down all four pairs.

    The 11 spawn spots: the pylon railing · the lobby reception desk · bottom-left of the lobby escalator · the destroyed stairs past the lobby hallway · the plastic chair in dead man’s corner · the kitchen counter on the dragon building’s top floor · the table at the back-right of the power room · a shelf at the very bottom of the power building · a couch cushion in the upside-down hallways · a ledge in the elevator shaft toward the Buddha Room · the Buddha Room’s top floor, right corner. A few pictured:

  11. Punch the pylon in your tile order. Buy the Galvaknuckles (below the elevator shaft toward the Buddha Room), head to the pylon, and melee its corners in the direction order your tiles gave — the diagram shows which corner is which direction.

    Done right, the pylon glows blue — “High Maintenance” complete, all perks for the rest of the game, and the lobby’s Die Rise icon burns blue.

Maxis’s steps

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Build the Trample Steam. All four parts are around the spawn lobby and the hallway past the first door; the bench is at the bottom of the escalator outside spawn. Part spawns pictured in the . Every player carries one.
  2. Turn on the power. Cross to the dragon-statue building and head down to the sewing-machine room — the switch is inside.
  3. Build the Nav Card Table on the dragon rooftop under the pylon — all four parts are up there with it:
    • 115 Meteorite — the trash pile between the windows and the AC units.
    • Wooden Table — directly behind the bench, under the dragon statue.
    • Radio — the corner by the crashed water tower.
    • Power Box — dead man’s corner, right of the zombie barrier.
  4. Stand on the four elevator symbols — together. All four players on a symbol each, at the same time (solo mod: one). The Maxis-side reference points them out as: above the Quick Revive elevator outside spawn · left of the Trample Steam bench · right of the power switch · by the MP5 wall-buy.
  5. Stand on the floor symbols in the right order. Trial and error, same as Richtofen’s side: first one to glow is first in sequence, wrong steps reset progress, done when all four stay lit. Spots: in front of the Quick Revive elevator outside spawn · the middle of the cafeteria · the roof ledge by the showers · the pylon rooftop edge.
  6. Snipe the orbs out of the dragons’ mouths. Scoped sniper at the two dragon statues over the Nav-table roof; shoot the orb in each mouth — fireworks mean success.
  7. Kill 15 zombies in the Buddha Room. Head there and rack up 15 kills inside — Maxis pipes up when it’s enough.
  8. Fire a Pack-a-Punched Ballistic Knife into the Buddha Room floor. Box until the Ballistic Knife shows up, upgrade it at Pack-a-Punch, then back in the Buddha Room shoot a blade into the floor (wall or zombie works too) until Maxis speaks again.
  9. Trample Steams on the four lion symbols — then the orbs. Four floor symbols with a lion. Each player plants a Trample Steam on one, facing the pylon (it stays flat when placed right). Then go to the spawn lobby, collect an orb from each lion statue by the door, and place the orbs on the Trample Steams — they fling the orbs back and forth between all four and the pylon lights orange. (Solo mod: two Trample Steams on opposite sides, one orb each.)
  10. Find your 8 Mahjong tiles. Same as Richtofen’s side: 8 tiles across 11 spots, four directions + four numbers, matched by color into a direction order. Chart and spawn list:

    Spawns: pylon railing · lobby reception desk · bottom-left of the escalator · destroyed stairs in the lobby hallway · the plastic chair in dead man’s corner · the top-floor kitchen counter · the power room table · a shelf at the power building’s bottom · a couch cushion in the upside-down hallways · a ledge in the Buddha Room elevator shaft · the Buddha Room top floor, right corner.

  11. Punch the pylon in your tile order. Galvaknuckles on, up to the pylon: each glowing beam marks a compass corner — melee them in your tiles’ direction order.

    Done right, the pylon glows orange — all perks for the rest of the game, and the lobby’s Die Rise icon burns orange.

The Victis endgameTranZit + Die Rise + Buried, one side

“High Maintenance” is the middle chapter of the Victis trilogy. Finish all three easter eggs on the same side with the same four players, place the right Nav Cards, and the story finale unlocks at the Easter Egg Tracker in Buried’s courthouse — endings, rewards and the tracker itself are on the Buried page’s Side Easter Eggs tab.

Nav CardGrab it early

Like the other Victis maps, Die Rise has its own Nav Card pickup near spawn, and the card that fits one map’s table comes from another. Grab it every game — the endgame needs all the tables carded correctly.

Die Rise also has a musical easter egg and other secrets — send a reference and I’ll build the cards.

One buildable wonder weapon — and it debuts the “slippery floor” kill chain.

SliquifierFree — built from 4 parts

Fires purple goo that liquefies zombies — kills chain through whole crowds, and the slick floor it leaves drops zombies (and you) on their backsides. Required for Richtofen’s easter egg . Once built and taken, it also joins the Mystery Box (one copy in play at a time).

The bench

Down the elevator shaft at spawn, jump the gap, follow right and down the stairs.

The four parts — all in the power-switch building

  • Mannequin Foot — one spawn: facing the power-room double doors, take a right and keep right to the down-stairs; look left — it’s on the floor by the TV.
  • Handbrake — on a sewing-machine table right of the power switch, OR downstairs on the kitchen counter by the pot and fridge.
  • Gas Canister — on the table directly in front of the power switch, OR in the cage right of the workbench.
  • Wires & Discs — on the barrel by the stairs opposite the workbench, OR in the shower room by the sink and boiler.

The secret upgrade — “unpatch” it

From spawn, head right until you’re standing at the bridge you’d slide down to the next area. Don’t slide — look left and shoot the top symbol 10 times with the Sliquifier. That reverts it to its stronger launch-day version.

Trample SteamSpringboard trap — and quest hardware

A spring pad that flings zombies off the roof — or flings you across gaps. Both easter egg sides need four placed at once, so in co-op everyone builds their own. The bench is at the bottom of the escalator outside spawn; all parts are in the lobby and the hallway past the first door:

  • Chicken Wire — left of the left lobby statue, OR by the left wall after the first door.
  • Motor — on the lobby reception desk, OR right of the elevator in the hallway past the first door.
  • Bellows — on the left lobby staircase, OR under the painting past the door between the statues.
  • Flag — on a chair by the Olympia wall-buy, OR on the railing before the escalator.

Pack-a-Punch

5000 points per upgrade — and on this map the machine rides an elevator. Nothing here sits still.

  1. Find the PaP elevator. The machine lives inside one of the elevators in the spawn building, riding between floors. Chase it via the elevator shafts, or wait at a floor it stops on.
  2. Use it fast — you only have until the doors close and it moves on. An elevator key (found on top of the elevators) can be used on an elevator’s control panel to hold it in place while you upgrade.
Maxis’s easter egg needs a Pack-a-Punched Ballistic Knife — plan a visit before step 8. The reference guides don’t cover the PaP route in detail; send one (or your own notes) and I’ll flesh this tab out with shots.
