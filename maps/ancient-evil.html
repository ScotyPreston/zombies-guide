Ancient Evil

Black Ops 4 · Delphi — the Chaos finale

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4. Normal difficulty, no modifiers.
  • Sentinel Artifact and Pack-a-Punch done.
  • The Eternal Flame reignited.
  • Apollo's Will (the shield) and the Pegasus Strike.
  • All four Redeemed God Hands — used in the order Charon → Gaia → Hemera → Ouranos. Exalted not needed.

"Greek Tragedy" — wake the gods, put on a show, and kill Perseus. Several steps are video-only in the reference; they're marked.

The full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Light the three oil spills. With the Eternal Flame burning blue, shield out, melee the flame so the spear tip catches it, then melee an oil spill. Back to the bowl for a fresh flame before each one. Your screen shakes when all three are lit.
  2. Death vision — four statues. At the Spartan Monument, stand where Pegasus first picks you up. Fire a Redeemed Charon charged shot at the floor and stand in it for "death vision". Look along both rows of statues for one with glowing blue eyes and hit it with a normal Charon shot — it crumbles. Four statues; re-shoot the floor if the vision fades. Screen shake = done.
  3. The gears. Shoot open three walls with a normal gun; each hides a big fixed gear and a small moving one. Shield out, and throw a spear into the hole the moment the small gear touches the big one — it sticks there.
  4. Stop the rotating statues. Upstairs in the Stoa of the Athenians: three turning statues round a crystal. Throw a spear at the gear in the back building when all three face the gem — time it for when the statue nearest you faces right, toward the Intersection. Screen shake = done.
  5. Gaia's roots. With the Redeemed Hand of Gaia in the Offering of the Attalids, three root sets need uncharged shots in quick succession — move until green flashes appear in front of you, then shoot; you're on a timer. (Video-only in the reference.) A Gegenees spawns — don't kill him yet.
  6. The ankh and the spear. Lead the Gegenees to the Intersection of Treasuries, where an ankh sits in a crystal. Make him shield-blast the crystal (a shot or two in his weak spot provokes it), grab the ankh, then kill him and take the spear he drops.
  7. The sundial. Back in the Offering of the Attalids, hold use on the golden sundial to plant the spear. Lure an electric catalyst onto the dial and kill it there: the dial starts, a slot opens, and a faint line appears. Count the gold slots from the open slot to the line, in the direction the symbols turn. Wait for a blue symbol to appear in the slot and pass out of view, count that many rotations, and hold use — the blue symbol stops on the line. Three rings, each faster; hold a touch early on the last. The spear sinks when all three line up.
  8. Defend the Ra statue. Go in with the Redeemed Hand of Hemera, shield, all perks, a PaP'd gun, the Pegasus Strike and a full specialist. In the Gymnasium Bathhouse, give the Ra statue the ankh, drop the Pegasus Strike at the entrance from the Intersection, then give the statue the Hand of Hemera. A beam fires into the opposite wall: skeletons try to block it at both ends — keep both clear (co-op: one player per end), don't step in the beam, and survive the zombies. Failed = the wall doesn't melt; re-take the Hand from the Hemera altar and try again. Success = the wall melts, revealing a scepter: give it to the statue and a mirror rises from the pool.
  9. Put on a show — three times. Everyone takes a Hand and goes to the Amphitheater. Stand in the circle matching your Hand's colour (red Charon, yellow Hemera, blue Ouranos, green Gaia) — white flash, the show starts. Run into the spotlight of your colour; glowing zombies spawn: in the stands = uncharged shots, on the ground = your charged attack. Screen turns grey = sprint back to the entrance. Five fails per round allowed; about five spotlight sets per show, three shows. A Max Ammo and points drop at the end.
  10. The secret door. Dark side, right-hand path before the perk: a door with symbols. Shoot six symbols in this order with a Pack-a-Punched gun. Then whoever plays Shaw and Bruno interacts with it (skip if nobody is) — cutscene.
  11. Raise and fire the crossbow. On the River of Sorrow stairs a small blue lightning symbol appears: place the Pegasus Strike on it and Pegasus raises a giant crossbow from the river. Redeemed Ouranos charged shots turn it — about four, until it stops. Now light the spear at the Eternal Flame, come back with the shield out, pay 1,000 at the green bowl where the Center of the World meets Python Pass to start the venom trap, run through it so the flame turns green, and melee the top of the crossbow. It fires at Pack-a-Punch.
  12. Pegasus, then Perseus. Full perks, PaP'd guns, your favourite Hand, shield, full specialist. The Pegasus Strike doesn't work in here — bring Homunculi from the box. Everyone holds use on the gold portal up the ramp behind Pack-a-Punch.
    • Pegasus: three floating islands joined by jump pads. Shoot him while he flies (Hands, or a launcher); when he glows lava-orange he falls to an island — jump over and specialist him. When he starts to rise, jump off — he leaves fire and electricity, and a damaged island becomes unreachable. Perseus throws flaming spears the whole time: they hurt but refill your specialist, so pace your exposure.
    • Pegasus eventually flies up to Perseus and is killed. Be on "Mount Olympus: Columns" — it's the only island left, anywhere else is instant death.
    • Perseus: survive waves on that island; when he hops down, hurt him until he kneels glowing, then specialist him. Repeat until he dies and drops the Oracle Key. Grab it — cutscene.

Redeemed Hand of Charon

Shoot the charged shot into the ground and walk ~20 zombies over it — 15 kills from one shot. Gegenees don't count.

Ouranos: fling 20 in one channel attack
Redeemed Hand of Ouranos

15–20 zombies in front of you, hold the channel attack and keep holding as the next ones spawn in.

Round 20 inside the Temple of Apollo
Never buy a door

You may step outside the temple as long as no doors are bought. Spawn with a MOG 12 with Dragon's Breath, buy the Strife with the Stiletto off the wall, use wraith fires and the Scepter of Ra for hordes and Blightfathers, save the specialist for emergencies. Unlocks when round 21 starts.

Kill a Gegenees with only Apollo's Will
Spears until he drops

Shield out, keep throwing spears at the four-armed Gegenees until he dies; out of spears = buy more at the shield table.

The God Hands

Four wonder weapons, each upgraded twice: Fallen → Redeemed → Exalted. You can only carry one Hand at a time — hold use at its altar to deposit it and go get another.

Dormant Hands — do this first
Find a hand in a vase, take it to an altar
  1. Find a dormant hand. They hide in vases — 20 possible spots — marked by a purple glow. The Oracle calls out clues ("seek the primordial weapon…"); the reference's video maps each phrase to a spot. (Video-only.)
  2. Place it on an altar and survive the lockdown: kill 10 glowing zombies inside the circle (specialist recommended). Pick up the Hand.
Each altar gives a different Hand: Monument of Craterus = Hemera · Spartan Monument = Gaia · Python Pass = Charon · Center of the World = Ouranos.
Hand of Hemera
Light — altar at the Monument of Craterus

Redeemed

  1. Three mirrors: shoot each with a normal gun to rotate it as close to vertical as you can, then hit it with the Hand of Hemera to light the bowl beside it.
  2. Melee the bowl to take the light and run straight back to the altar and melee it to deposit. Three runs: Temple Terrace, Upper Road, Gymnasium Bathhouse.
  3. Start the ritual at the altar, enter the portal, clear the arena, leave by the portal. Charged shot = a held incineration beam.
Hand of Gaia
Earth — altar at the Spartan Monument

Redeemed

  1. Three plants carry red crystals: shoot every crystal off with the Hand of Gaia, a new plant sprouts — carry it to the altar and plant it. Three times.
  2. Portal, arena, portal out. Charged shot = a line of spiked earth.
Hand of Charon
Death — altar in Python Pass (dark side)

Redeemed

  1. Stand in the river in front of the Odin perk and kill zombies there with the Hand until the water turns red, then drink it.
  2. Find three coins (13 possible spots — video-only in the reference). You can't regenerate health until they're all returned, so be careful. Place them at the Charon altar.
  3. Portal, arena, portal out. Charged shot = a pit on the ground that devours anything that walks over it (not Gegenees).
Hand of Ouranos
Sky — altar at the Center of the World (dark side)

Redeemed

  1. Blast zombies into three giant arrowheads with the Hand; a feather pops out. Shoot the floating feather a few times to steer it to the altar (miss it and hit the arrow with another zombie). The reference has a video of the technique.
  2. Portal, arena, portal out. Channel attack = a Thundergun-style gust that flings everything in front of you.

Tributes & the Eternal Flame

IX-style challenges. Pay at a bowl to start one, bank rewards at the podiums, and every turn-in feeds the Eternal Flame — which the main quest and the Exalted Hands both need.

  1. Start a challenge at a bowl — Temple of Apollo (spawn) or the Cliff Side Ruins on the dark side. Not available until the Sentinel Artifact is active.
  2. Challenge types: hold a position · kills in a location · kills with Apollo's Will · headshots · take damage and recover · special-weapon kills · God Hand kills · take no damage · equipment kills. Keep completing them to rank the reward up before you cash in.
  3. Cash in at the podiums round the big bowl in the Temple of Apollo. Rewards are random within the tier:
    TierPossible rewards
    CommonDouble Points, Self Revive, Insta-Kill, Bowie Knife
    RareFire Sale, Max Ammo, PaP'd Strife, PaP'd Escargot, PaP'd RK 7 Garrison
    LegendaryMax Ammo, Full Power, Bullet Boost, PaP'd Rampage, PaP'd Switchblade X9, PaP'd Grav
    EpicFree extra perk, Self Revive, Pegasus Strike, PaP'd Swat RFT, PaP'd Daemon 3XB, PaP'd SDM
  4. Reignite the Eternal Flame. Each turn-in adds tribute points; the flame is done when the big bowl burns fully blue. Needed: 9 points solo, 18 for two, 27 for three, 36 for four. Fastest = everyone banks an Epic and a Legendary.

Pack-a-Punch

The Sentinel Artifact opens the map; the golden bridle and Pegasus open the dark side, where Pack-a-Punch sits encased in crystal.

  1. Wake the Sentinel Artifact. Spawn → Amphitheater, hold use on it: a lockdown survival wave until Pegasus swoops in and ends it. Every door on the main side opens.
  2. Take the golden bridle from one of two pedestals. Grabbing it spawns a Gegenees — be ready.
  3. Ride Pegasus. At the Spartan Monument stand in the lightning-bolt circle and hold use — he carries you to the dark side.
  4. Free the two eagles. Head down behind the perk to the Center of the World; the two approaches each hold a caged eagle. Python Pass: shoot the cage down first, then open the gate with your specialist weapon's attack. Cliff Ruins: specialist straight on the gate.
  5. Defend the eagles. Back at the Center of the World another survival wave starts: kill the skeletons while the eagles break the crystal. Pack-a-Punch opens, and a portal behind it takes you back to the main map.

Perk altars

Chaos-style: four god statues, fixed spots, each selling the perk you put in that loadout slot. Two are on the dark side.
