Origins

Black Ops 2 · Der Riese excavation site, 1918

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4 — soloable, but it's long. In co-op you can split the staff work.
  • All four staffs built AND upgraded — by far the biggest chunk.
  • You'll also need the Thunder Fists and the G-Strike along the way — both are steps below.

"Little Lost Girl" — full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Turn on the power by activating all 6 generators.
  2. Build and upgrade all four staffs. Do this before anything else — it's most of the quest. Full build + upgrade for each staff — see Staffs tab
  3. Fill the 4 Soul Collection Boxes — roughly 20 kills at each. You'll know a box is done when its lid closes and it sinks into the ground. Below the Excavation Site · Generator 5 · Generator 4 · outside the Church
    Watch the Giant Robot footprints near the boxes — getting stepped on can undo your progress. Easier from round 8 onward when zombies spawn faster.
  4. Take the Thunder Fists from the Reward Box. Where the reward chest is and how to claim it — see Side Easter Eggs tab, One Inch Punch card
  5. Cleanse the tablet at the Church. In the Generator 2 bunker, take one of the stone tablets off the table. Carry it to the Church, place it in the bath, then get 20 melee kills standing near the bath.
  6. Carry it back without touching mud. Pick the tablet up and return it to the Generator 2 bunker table — if you step in wet mud you have to cleanse it again. Put it back, get another 20 melee kills, and you're rewarded with the G-Strike. Full walkthrough with the safe route — see Side Easter Eggs tab, G-Strike card
  7. Build the Maxis Drone. All part locations — see Buildables tab, Maxis Drone card
  8. Place each staff in its pedestal: Fire → Excavation · Ice → Church robot · Wind → Middle robot · Lightning → Spawn robot
  9. The robot button and the Panzer wave. Climb into a robot, hit the red button, then get out fast. G-Strike the hole in the ground near Generator 5; once it opens, deploy the Maxis Drone. Done right, 10 Panzer Soldats spawn — kill all ten.
  10. Shoot down the plane and hunt the pilot. Grab a Zombie Blood power-up, then look to the sky above the Excavation Site for a glowing yellow airplane and shoot it. A zombie pilot ejects and lands somewhere on the map — find and kill it before your Zombie Blood runs out. It drops the Upgraded Maxis Drone.
  11. Upgrade the Thunder Fists. Head to the bottom of the Excavation Site and melee 20 zombies that have white smoke coming off them using the Thunder Fists. A tablet drops — pick it up for the Upgraded Thunder Fists.
  12. The finale in Agartha. Place all four staffs into their pedestals in Agartha and get 100 kills there. A portal opens in the ceiling — deploy the Upgraded Maxis Drone into it to trigger the ending cutscene.

Golden Shovel & Red Dig Piles16 red sites · up to 8 permanent perk slots ›

Requirements

  • Players: any (works solo).
  • You need a shovel, then the Golden Shovel, then Zombie Blood — in that order.
  1. Get a shovel. Four possible spots: Two in the spawn area · one in the church · one in the wind tunnel
  2. Dig about 30 piles anywhere on the map until the game upgrades you to the Golden Shovel. Ordinary dirt/bone piles all count.
  3. Get Zombie Blood. It can come from dig piles, zombies, the Panzer, challenge rewards, or the wagon.
  4. While Zombie Blood is active, the red dig piles become visible. Dig them with the Golden Shovel — they can hand you a golden perk bottle, which is a permanent perk. Keep at it and you build up to 8 permanent perk slots.
    Red piles only show up during Zombie Blood, so plan a route before you pop it — the timer is short.
  5. Bonus — the Golden Helmet. Once you have the Golden Shovel, digging piles can also hand you the Golden Helmet. It's random, so it may take several digs. Wearing it makes you immune to the Giant Robots' footsteps — you can stand under a foot and live (you'll still get dazed).
    Handy for the Wind Staff, whose parts all sit under the robots' feet.
Free Magna ColliderMaxis Drone + 4 medallions → free Pack-a-Punched MG08 ›

Requirements

  • Players: any (works solo).
  • The Maxis Drone built — it's the same drone the main quest needs.

The reward is a free, already Pack-a-Punched MG08 — the Magna Collider.

  1. Build the Maxis Drone. Three parts; the body and rotor each have three possible spots. Brain — at spawn Body — ice tunnel · between church and Gen 4 · between church and Gen 5 Rotor — excavation · below excavation · by Mule Kick
  2. Collect the 4 Maxis Medallions. They're up high, so look above you: 1. Front entrance church roof · 2. Generator 5 roof · 3. Pack-a-Punch entrance scaffolding (the AK wall-buy area) · 4. On the right outside spawn, heading toward Generator 2
  3. Take the Magna Collider from the Pack-a-Punch machine — it appears there for free, already upgraded.
G-Strike GrenadesStone tablet ritual · required for the main quest ›

Requirements

  • Players: any (works solo).
  • Required for the main Easter egg — and it needs melee kills, so get it early before zombies get tanky.

G-Strikes are Origins' upgraded Monkey Bombs, and they replace your tactical slot.

  1. Take a stone tablet. Inside the Generator 2 bunker there's a wooden table holding four stone tablets marked with a fist icon — pick one up.
  2. Carry it to the Church (near Generator 6, past No Man's Land) and interact with the basin to place the tablet inside.
  3. Get 20 melee kills near the tablet to cleanse it. You'll know it worked because you can pick the tablet back up.
  4. Carry it back to the Generator 2 bunker — without stepping in wet mud. Stick to wooden boards and dried mud. If it gets muddy, you have to redo the 20-kill cleanse.
  5. Put it back on the table and get another 20 melee kills. The G-Strikes rise out of the table — pick them up.
Secret songsArchangel · Shepard of Fire · Aether ›

Three separate music easter eggs, each triggered by activating three hidden objects.

  1. "Archangel" — activate 3 green Element 115 rocks: 1. Spawn room, right of the ancient chest on the far side of the bunk bed 2. Second floor of the work station, opposite the stairway, under a bed 3. On the scaffolding left of the Excavation Site entrance facing spawn, next to a crate
  2. "Shepard of Fire" — activate 3 red radios: 1. On the scaffolding inside the Excavation Site, above one of the Maxis Drone part spots 2. Inside the Giant Robot "Freya", opposite the audio log 3. On the left edge of the fire area in the Crazy Place
  3. "Aether" — go prone and activate 3 generator floor panels in this order: 1. The "1" panel at Generator 1 → 2. the second "1" panel at Generator 1 → 3. the "5" panel at Generator 5
Samantha's LullabyZombies Chronicles only · "Remember Forever" ›

Requirements

  • Zombies Chronicles version (the BO3 remaster) — this one isn't in the original BO2 release.
  • All four staffs built.
  1. Inside the Excavation Site, above where you build the staffs, there's a ring of rock with four squares — one on each side. Shoot each square with a different staff (each side has its own element).
  2. Go to the Crazy Place and interact with the Samantha doll on the inner blue 115 circle.
  3. More Samantha dolls spawn in the middle of the blue circle — shoot them all with a bullet weapon.
  4. One doll flies up toward Agartha — do not shoot that one. Let it go. A Max Ammo drops and "Remember Forever" starts playing.

One Inch PunchThunder Fists · 4 Soul Boxes → reward chest ›

Requirements

  • Players: any (works solo). No prerequisites — you can start this any time.
  • Required for the main Easter egg, and it one-hit-kills zombies up to around round 19.

Also called the Iron Fists or Thunder Fists. You earn it by filling the four Soul Boxes, then claiming it from the Ritual of the Ancients chest.

  1. Fill all four Soul Boxes. Kill zombies right next to each box until its lid closes and it sinks away — roughly 20–30 kills each. Do them one at a time. 1. By Generator 5 and Stamin-Up · 2. Left of the mound (Pack-a-Punch) on the way to Generator 4 · 3. By the wall-buy at Generator 4 and Jugger-Nog · 4. Outside the Church
    The boxes sit near Giant Robot footprints — getting stomped can reset your progress on that box. Round 8+ is easier since zombies come faster.
  2. Claim it from the Ritual of the Ancients chest at Generator 6 or Generator 1. Choose the bottom icon — the one with four circles — to open the chest and take the One Inch Punch.
  3. It gets upgraded during the main quest — melee 20 white-smoke zombies at the bottom of the Excavation Site.

Tap a staff to open its guide — each card covers building it and upgrading it. Read the shared setup first; all four follow the same shape.

Shared setup — read this firstGramophone · discs · Crazy Place · the statue chamber ›

All four staffs follow the same pattern. Do this once and the individual staff cards make a lot more sense.

  1. Grab a gramophone. It sits around the mound (the Excavation Site) — you need it to open every elemental chamber.
  2. Find that staff's coloured disc (record). Each has 3 possible spawns, listed in its own card: blue = Ice, red = Fire, purple = Lightning, yellow = Wind.
  3. Open the elemental chamber. Take the disc and gramophone to that element's tunnel and place them on the table — a glowing portal opens into the Crazy Place.
  4. Take the crystal. Inside the Crazy Place, grab the crystal off the matching coloured pillar/podium. Remember this podium — you come back to it to upgrade.
  5. Collect that staff's 3 parts (see its card), then build it at the statue chamber below Pack-a-Punch at the Excavation Site. Each staff has its own statue: Left = Wind · Middle-left = Ice · Middle-right = Lightning · Right = Fire
  6. Upgrading always follows the same four beats: solve that element's puzzle in its Crazy Place chamber → do a map-wide step → back at the statues, work the scaffolding levers until all four dials show your colour and shoot the orb → put the staff in your Crazy Place podium and get ~20 kills to feed it souls.
Ice StaffBlue · needs the snow event · upgrades to Ull's Arrow ›

Requirements

  • Wait for the snow. The three Ice parts only appear while it's snowing — that weather event starts at random.
  • A shovel for digging, plus the gramophone and the blue disc.
  1. Blue disc — all three spawns are in the Generator 2 Tank Station: 1. On the first table, with the 115 rocks · 2. On the shelf right of the Mystery Box · 3. On the shelf opposite the G-Strike tablets
  2. The 3 staff parts — only while it's snowing. Dig the small dirt mounds marked with bones: 1. The trenches between Generator 2 and Generator 3 · 2. No Man's Land, around Generators 5 and 4 · 3. Outside the Church / by Generator 6
  3. Crystal and build. Open the Ice Tunnel chamber with the disc + gramophone, take the blue crystal, then build at the middle-left statue.
  4. Upgrade 1 — the ice chamber puzzle. In the Crazy Place ice section, read the symbols on the wall, translate them with the chart, then shoot the matching symbols on the ceiling with the Ice Staff until they're all gone.
  5. Upgrade 2 — freeze three headstones. Shoot each one with the Ice Staff to destroy it. When all three are down, a beam of light rises from the Excavation Site. 1. Facing the Excavation Site from Generator 4 · 2. Right of the wall-buy at Generator 4 · 3. Left of the destroyed Giant Robot's hand at Generator 2
  6. Upgrade 3 — dials and orb. Back at the statues, work the scaffolding levers until all four dials show a blue symbol, then shoot the blue orb to send it to the Crazy Place.
  7. Upgrade 4 — feed it souls. Put the staff in the blue podium in the Crazy Place and get 20 kills around it, then take back Ull's Arrow.
Fire StaffRed · plane, Gen 6 chest, Panzersoldat · upgrades to Kagutsuchi's Blood ›

Requirements

  • Round 8+ — one part is dropped by the Panzersoldat, who first shows up around then.
  • Generator 6 powered (it gates a second part), plus the gramophone and the red disc.
  1. Red disc — three possible spawns around the Church: 1. Between the tank and the stairs beneath the Church · 2. On the wooden bench inside the Church, left of the water basin · 3. On the crate left of Generator 6 in the Church
  2. Part 1 — shoot down the plane. Watch the sky above the Excavation Site for a glowing plane, shoot it down, and the part drops to the ground near the tank and stairs under the Church.
  3. Part 2 — Generator 6. Activate Generator 6 (reached through the Church); the part sits in the challenge chest to the left.
  4. Part 3 — the Panzersoldat. Kill the Panzersoldat (round 8+) and pick the part up off its corpse.
  5. Crystal and build. The Fire Tunnel is out in the trenches, outside Generator 1 toward Generator 3. Open the chamber, take the red crystal, then build at the right statue (red base).
  6. Upgrade 1 — light the four bonfires. In the Crazy Place, stand on the grates near the red platform with the four unlit braziers and get roughly 30 kills with the Fire Staff to light them all.
  7. Upgrade 2 — the Church torch puzzle. Upstairs in the Church, the back wall shows four glowing symbols; each translates to a number.
    Watch out: the "4" is actually a bloodstain, not a carved symbol — it's easy to miss.
    Then head down to the tank area: the walls have unlit torches with numbers under them. Shoot the torches matching your four numbers in quick succession. Get it right and a beam of light shoots to Pack-a-Punch and the torches stay lit.
  8. Upgrade 3 — dials and orb. At the statues below Pack-a-Punch, turn each of the four levers until every dial symbol is red, then shoot the red orb at the bottom of the dials.
  9. Upgrade 4 — feed it souls. Place the staff in the red podium where you took the crystal and get 20 kills in the Crazy Place, then collect Kagutsuchi's Blood.
Lightning StaffPurple · three tank jumps · upgrades to Kimat's Bite ›

Requirements

  • The tanks running — two of the three parts are only reachable by riding a tank and jumping off at the right moment.
  • The gramophone and the purple disc.
  1. Purple disc — three possible spawns around Generator 4: 1. Inside the wagon by the tree outside Generator 4 · 2. On the table by Der Wunderfizz · 3. By the lantern at the bottom of the Wind Tunnel
  2. Part 1 — the Church trenches. Ride the tank from the Church and jump onto the scaffolding on the right just before it passes the trenches.
  3. Part 2 — Generator 2. Take the tank from the Generator 2 Tank Station; scaffolding appears on the left near the MP-40 wall-buy, leading toward the Excavation Site.
  4. Part 3 — back of the Church. Jump off to the right just before the tank enters the Church; a dirt path leads round to the Church's back entrance where the last part sits.
  5. Crystal and build. Place the gramophone and purple disc on the table to open the chamber, shoot the purple pillar with the staff to free the crystal, then build at the middle-right statue.
  6. Upgrade 1 — the arrow symbols. In the Crazy Place, shoot the arrow symbols with the Lightning Staff in three groups. Ignore the top row entirely. The red X's in these shots are the ones to hit: Group 1: symbols 1, 3, 6  ·  Group 2: symbols 3, 5, 7  ·  Group 3: symbols 2, 4, 6
  7. Upgrade 2 — the seven electrical dials. Turn each panel so its needle points the right way. Get all seven right and a beam fires into the Excavation Site. 1. Spawn / Laboratory — turn 2×, needle LEFT 2. Generator 2 Tank Station — turn 3×, needle DOWN 3. Generator 4 — turn 2×, needle UP 4. Excavation Site wall — turn 3×, needle UP 5. Generator 5 — turn 3×, needle DOWN 6. Church downstairs — turn 2×, needle RIGHT 7. Church upstairs — turn 1×, needle UP
  8. Upgrade 3 — dials and orb. Jump the scaffolding in the statue room to hit the levers until all the dials show purple, then shoot the purple orb underneath them.
  9. Upgrade 4 — feed it souls. Put the staff back in the purple podium and get 20 kills around it to collect Kimat's Bite.
Wind StaffYellow · under the robots' feet · upgrades to Boreas' Fury ›

Requirements

  • The gramophone and the yellow disc.
  • All three parts are under the Giant Robots' feet — read the warning below before you try it.
  1. Yellow disc — three possible spawns around Generator 5: 1. On the crate opposite the Lightning Tunnel entrance · 2. On the broken wall right of Stamin-Up · 3. On the workbench to the left as you enter the Lightning Tunnel
  2. All 3 parts — shoot up under the Giant Robots' feet as they step over you. One part per robot: Thor — on the path to Generator 2 · Odin — No Man's Land, between Generators 5 and 4 · Freya — the Church area
    This one can kill you. Only stand under the foot that has yellow lights glowing underneath it — any other foot will crush you.
  3. Crystal and build. Enter the Wind Tunnel at Generator 4, place the yellow disc and gramophone on the table, take the yellow crystal off the rising pillar, then build at the left statue.
  4. Upgrade 1 — the ceiling rings. In the Crazy Place, find the corner with the yellow teleporter pad and shoot the four rotatable ceiling rings with the Wind Staff until they match this pattern. It's the same every game.
    The four symbols spell F I L L going outward — that's the trick for remembering them.
  5. Upgrade 2 — turn three smokestacks. Point all three so they blow toward the mound; when they line up a beam shoots into the sky. 1. Right of Generator 4 · 2. On the tank path from the Church to Generator 5, right side by the wooden sign · 3. At Generator 5, behind Stamin-Up by the tank
  6. Upgrade 3 — dials and orb. Back in the statue chamber, hit the four scaffolding levers until every dial symbol is yellow, then shoot the yellow orb to send it to the Crazy Place.
  7. Upgrade 4 — feed it souls. Put the staff on the yellow podium where you took the crystal and get around 20 kills in the Crazy Place to collect Boreas' Fury.

Three parts. The brain is always in the same place; the body and rotor each have three possible spots.

Zombie Shield3 parts · 3 possible spawns each · back armour + melee ›

Requirements

  • Players: any (works solo).
  • Three areas of the map need to be open — the parts are spread across Generators 1, 2, 3 and No Man's Land.

Origins' shield is steel plate and bulletproof glass. It rides on your back and soaks hits from behind until it breaks. Three parts — the Window, the Base and the Handle — and each has three possible spawns, but always within the same area.

  1. Window — always between Generator 1 and Generator 3. It's the door window in a metal frame: 1. Near Generator 3 — take the ramp by Speed Cola; it's beside a crate at the bottom of the ramp 2. In the Fire Mine — at the bottom, on the table to the right, beside the equipment 3. In the tunnel — leaving Generator 1 stay right; it's in a wheelbarrow next to a table
  2. Base — the big metal slab, always somewhere in No Man's Land. It sits out in the open: 1. In the corner of a large puddle near the flaming cart, over by the Excavation Site 2. In another large puddle close to Generator 4 — the area with the MP5 on the wall 3. Between the Excavation Site and Generator 4, leaning against a dirt bank
  3. Handle — two metal triangles with a grip, always between Generator 1 and Generator 2: 1. In the enclosed part of the path outside Generator 1, on the ground by the wheelbarrow 2. Past Generator 2 and the Workshop entrance — at the dead end, in a wheelbarrow 3. On top of the Tank Station roof, lying against the edge
  4. Build it at a crafting bench once you have all three parts.
  5. Using it: it protects your back by default. Press up on the d-pad to hold it in front of you as a melee weapon — that leaves your back open, and since it takes both hands you'll holster your gun. You can also drop it on the ground as a barrier.
    You can carry the Zombie Shield and the Maxis Drone at the same time — they don't share a slot.

Opening Pack-a-Punch

  1. Turn on all six generators around the map. That's it — once all six are running, Pack-a-Punch is open at the Excavation Site in the middle of the map.

What each machine looks like

Der Wunderfizz — everything else

  1. Every other perk comes from the Divine Wunderfizz machine — Mule Kick, Electric Cherry, Deadshot Daiquiri, Double Tap II and the rest. It gives a random perk for 1500 points a pull.
  2. It moves. The machine spawns at one of several spots around the map — look for the white beam of light in the sky to find where it is this game.

Double Tap II

There's no Double Tap machine to buy. Aside from a lucky Wunderfizz pull, you get it from the Challenge Box / Reward Chest next to Generator 1 — take it as your reward for finishing the "spend 30,000 points" challenge.
