Voyage of Despair

Black Ops 4 · The Titanic, maiden voyage

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4.
  • Pack-a-Punch open and the water drained.
  • The Kraken, upgraded to the poison version, Jörmungandr's Fang.

"Abandon Ship" — the Chaos crew earns the Odin Sentinel Artefact on a sinking Titanic. Pen and paper for the clocks.

The full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Find the four clocks and their symbols. Six clocks are on the ship; four of them show a time other than 11:40. For each one, write down the time and the triangle symbol chalked nearby. The symbols are four triangles: up, up with a line, down, down with a line.
    Symbol translation: the chart shows each triangle and which dial it belongs to later. Note them as "TIME – UP/LINE", "TIME – DOWN/NO LINE" and so on.
    • Mail Room — clock right of the doorway to the Cargo Hold; symbol under the metal stairs by the Vapr wall-buy.
    • Bridge — clock right above the steering wheel in the room behind the four dials; symbol under the desk behind the wheel, to the left.
    • Upper Grand Staircase — clock engraved into the wall at the top of the stairs; symbol above a closed door to the left when you turn around.
    • 1st Class Lounge — mantelpiece clock above the fireplace; symbol on the wall under a three-piece candle holder, left of the Mystery Box spawn.
    • Galley — clock on the wall opposite the hanging corpse; symbol on the side of the cabinet to the clock's left.
    • 3rd Class Berths — clock at the foot of the stairs from the Poop Deck; symbol under those stairs, behind the crates and luggage.
  2. Bridge dials = the minutes. At the front of the Bridge, by the steering wheel, are four dials, each engraved with one of the symbols. Turn each arrow to the minute from that symbol's clock — it moves in 5-minute notches (12:10 = two notches right). All four right and they lock in place.
  3. Poop Deck dials = the hours (line symbols). At the far end of the Poop Deck, where you first touched the Sentinel Artefact, are two unmarked dials by the wheel: left = UP + LINE, right = DOWN + LINE. Enter each one's hour. They start at 12: turning left goes 11, 10, 9…; turning right goes 1, 2, 3…
  4. Engine Room dials = the hours (plain symbols). Down on the lower walkways (dry, if you drained the water) four dials sit on the sides of a pole. Ignore the bottom-right and top-left — they're visibly busted. Bottom-left = UP, top-right = DOWN. Same hour rules as the Poop Deck. A ding and chorus means all eight dials are right.
  5. Find the four sparking outlets. Six outlet spots; four will be sparking red, green, blue or yellow:
    • 3rd Class Berths — down the first stairs from the Poop Deck, take a left; on the right-hand wall.
    • Aft Decks — up the stairs from the Poop Deck, through a central zombie window.
    • 1st Class Lounge — next to the Zeus perk statue.
    • Upper Grand Staircase — near the symbol spot, right of the clock along the wall.
    • Dining Hall — facing the Ra perk statue, turn around and walk right; behind a white wooden panel.
    • State Rooms — from the Forecastle, ground-floor left entrance, first room on the left; on the wall by two paintings.
  6. Kill the matching elemental at each outlet. Lead the elemental zombie of that colour to its outlet and kill it right next to it — a ritual symbol appears on the floor. Four outlets, four symbols.
  7. The four rituals — Poison → Water → Electric → Fire. Everyone stands on the Poison symbol and interacts: you're teleported into a survival fight — kill everything that comes. Pick up the white (fake) Sentinel Artefact afterwards or you can't teleport to the next one. Repeat in order; wrong order = start over. After Fire you receive the real Sentinel Artefact.
    It's finicky: a second symbol sometimes won't appear while another is already on the floor. Easiest is one at a time — kill the Poison elemental, run its ritual, then the Water one, and so on.
  8. Plug the nine leaks. With Jörmungandr's Fang (or the base Decayed Kraken), go to the Turbine Room and shoot every steaming burst pipe along the walls — a kill hitmarker confirms each. Nine in total. The water starts rising again when you're done.
  9. Pack-a-Punch the Sentinel Artefact. The machine must be in the Turbine Room: if it is, use it now; if not, finish the round and it teleports back there. It's free. Then head back out to the Forecastle.
  10. Find the nine planet symbols. Each chalk mark is a planet:
    • Sun — Forecastle, at the back on the side of the curved funnel by the fast-travel portal.
    • Mercury — Mail Room, bottom of the second flight of stairs, on your left.
    • Venus — Millionaire Suites (from the Lower Grand Staircase), under the bedside table.
    • Moon — Lower Grand Staircase, on a closed white door right of the stairs.
    • Mars — Boiler Room: face the fast-travel portal, turn around, go prone — below the floor on the wall that sticks out by a red pipe.
    • Saturn — Bridge: past the metal-beam-floor room (a shield part spawns there), next room on the right, on the side of a cabinet by the zombie window.
    • Jupiter — Engine Room: face the Odin perk statue, turn around — on the floor by the stairs down.
    • Uranus — State Rooms, in the bathroom opposite the MOG-12 wall-buy, behind the potted plant in the corner.
    • Neptune — Aft Decks, inside the "RMS Titanic – Liverpool" ring buoy near the top of the hanging lifeboat.
  11. The solar system model. In the Cargo Hold, interact with the model of the solar system — the planets flash in a random order. Write the order down.
    Warning: starting this spawns endless zombies — the round won't end until you finish or fail the step. Stock up first.
  12. Shoot the planets in that order. Outside, the planets, Sun and a huge Moon hang in the sky — Neptune is in the water, floating around and surfacing now and then. Shoot a planet, then run to its chalk symbol and interact with the orb before the timer runs out; miss it and you wait a full round for another go. The Sun is always last — make sure you have ammo and Pack-a-Punched guns before you shoot it.
  13. Shoot the Sun and run. From the Forecastle, shoot the Sun; its orb rests by the Sun symbol. All players interact — white flash, timer starts. Race to the Poop Deck: take the right-hand staircase and smash the ice blocks as fast as you can. At the far end a red symbol appears on the floor — the boss portal. Don't touch it until everyone is ready.
    Take at least one Pack-a-Punched Hellion Salvo in — its damage and splash carry the boss fight.
  14. The Eye of Odin. You land in the water — swim to the nearby tree, use the Sentinel Artefact there, and you're back on the boat with the Eye.
    • Forecastle: the Eye can't be hurt. Kill every Stoker, elemental and Blightfather that spawns → teleport.
    • Engine Room: same again, Eye still invulnerable → teleport.
    • State Rooms: normal zombies and Stokers only. The Eye fires a blue beam that freezes you — melee to break out. While it fires, shoot it in the pupil (Hellion Salvo) → teleport.
    • Promenade Starboard Deck: the Eye flies around the ship in the water — keep hitting it with the Hellion Salvo.
    • Poop Deck: someone needs the Kraken. Keep shooting; when the Eye retreats to the very back and starts charging, shoot it with the Kraken to stop it (the ice version works best). If it finishes the charge, everyone without Dying Wish goes down. Stop it three times and it dies — cutscene.

Wonder Weapons

The Kraken can drop from the Mystery Box — or take the guaranteed route. Then upgrade it: Pack-a-Punch makes the Cirein-Cròin Crasher, and the Distillation Kit adds one of four elements.

Kraken
The guaranteed route — no box luck needed
  1. Kill a Stoker for its key. Stokers are the big fiery ones flinging hot coals with a shovel — they tend to show up around round 10. Shoot its glowing weak spots to kill it and it drops the Stoker's Key.
  2. Find the treasure chest — one of five spots:
    • Mail Room — below the Forecastle, right of the staircase.
    • Turbine Room — right of the Pack-a-Punch pedestal at the end of the path.
    • Provisions — in the slaughter room / freezer.
    • Galley — back-left corner.
    • State Rooms — very back of the ground floor, by the door to the Millionaire Suites.
  3. Feed it, three times. Use the key on the chest, then kill zombies next to it — it drinks their souls, vanishes and reappears at one of the other spots. Find it and feed it again; after the third, an item appears inside.
  4. Take the item to its crate. Which item you got decides where the Kraken lands:
    ItemCrate
    BinocularsForecastle — wooden box at the very back of spawn, right-hand side
    CompassBoat Deck — top of the Grand Staircase, left; crate on the left
    GlobeMid Deck — crate on the right-hand side
    ToolAft Decks — facing the Poop Deck, left-hand side crate
    TelescopePoop Deck — crate at the very back
    Walk up to the right crate and a tentacle bursts out of the water and drops the Kraken on it.
Cirein-Cròin Crasher
Pack-a-Punched Kraken

Put the Kraken through Pack-a-Punch: the magazine doubles to 12 shots. The element upgrades below work on the Crasher too — and that's the version you want going into the finale.

Element upgrades — do this first
Distillation Kit + a plasma

Requirements

  • The Distillation Kit built on the Engine Room table.
  1. Get a plasma. Kill elemental (Catalyst) zombies of the type you want — the mutated ones have a chance to drop their concentrated plasma: fire, ice, poison or electric.
  2. Upgrade at the kit. Interact with the Distillation Kit with the Kraken in hand — 6,000 points for the Crasher (3,000 if the Kraken isn't Pack-a-Punched). It also refills your ammo. You can swap elements as often as you like, as long as you bring a new plasma each time.
The main quest needs the poison one, Jörmungandr's Fang, for the leaking pipes.
Breath of Leviathan
Fire — Concentrated Radiance

Fires two linked projectiles like chain shot, straight ahead; they burst after about a second or when they hit the scenery. The shot itself barely hurts zombies and doesn't explode on them — aim at the floor or wall behind the pack.

Grip of Akkorokamui
Ice — Concentrated Purity

A single shot that leaves a freezing patch on impact, slowing everything in it. The best version for stopping the Eye of Odin's charge in the boss fight.

Inkanyamba's Roar
Electric — Concentrated Plasma

A shotgun spread of small bolts with no explosion. Anything it doesn't kill gets knocked flat — easy to finish, but on high rounds the bodies still block your path.

Jörmungandr's Fang
Poison — Concentrated Decay · needed for the quest

A single shot that passes straight through zombies and only bursts on the scenery (a smaller blast than the others). Survivors are left disoriented. This is the one that plugs the nine Turbine Room leaks in the main quest.

Buildables

Ballistic Shield
3 parts — blocks hits and carries an automatic pistol

Not needed for the main quest, but it blocks zombie hits and shoots — well worth building.

  1. Part 1 — State Rooms (ground floor, from the Forecastle):
    • Keep right: on a rocking chair seen through the gaping holes in the walls.
    • Cross to the left into the laundry room: on a shelf next to the towels.
    • The next room ahead with another hole in the wall: on an armchair by a single bed.
  2. Part 2 — Bridge (up the right-hand stairs from the Forecastle):
    • Turn around at the top: on the left by the smashed window looking in at the four dials and the wheel.
    • Right-hand door at the back of the Bridge: just inside the second door, on a wooden crate on metal beams.
    • Further in: sitting on a handrail in the middle — impossible to miss.
  3. Part 3 — Provisions / 3rd Class Berths:
    • From the freezer, head for the Engine Room door but look left: behind a stack of boxes by the door.
    • Left-hand side, end of the hallway: right at the back in front of the burning area you can't enter.
    • 3rd Class Berths above Provisions: up the stairs, on a handrail through a big hole in the wall.
  4. Build it on the Poop Deck — the bench is just down from the Pack-a-Punch pedestal.
Distillation Kit
3 parts + a plasma — the Kraken's element upgrade station
  1. Part 1 — Cargo Hold: at the Pack-a-Punch pedestal turn around — on the crates to the left, or towards the middle of the room on other luggage.
  2. Part 2 — Galley: at the back, on the table blocking off the fire, or down the corridor at the far end on the left-most shelf next to rolled-up papers.
  3. Part 3 — 1st Class Lounge: from the A Deck landing of the Grand Staircase go through the door on the right (by the Mystery Box) and up the plank ramp — on the first table to your right, or round the corner to the left on another table.
  4. Part 4 is a plasma from an elemental zombie, then build at the table on the side of the Engine Room. Upgrading and the four elements are covered in the weapons tab.

Pack-a-Punch

No power switch — the Sentinel Artefact opens the ship. Four pedestals; the last one you touch is where the machine spawns, and it moves between them afterwards.

  1. Reach the Poop Deck and wake the Sentinel Artefact. Open the bottom door in the Forecastle, go through the State Rooms and keep opening the doors ahead until you come out on the Poop Deck. The blue light on the raised platform is the artefact — interact. An explosion goes off in the distance and a red beam hits the pedestal below.
  2. Touch all four pedestals. Fast-travel from the Poop Deck back to the Forecastle and take them in this order so the machine ends up on the Poop Deck:
    • Cargo Hold — Mail Room below the Forecastle, open the Cargo Hold door, swim to the very back.
    • Lower Grand Staircase — go down the big staircase until you can't go any further; the pedestal is in the dark straight ahead.
    • Turbine Room — doorway under the Poop Deck into Provisions, through the door past the slaughter room into the Engine Room, swim down to the back; pedestal underwater by the red turbine.
    • Poop Deck — last. The machine spawns here, the handiest spot of the four.
  3. Drain the water while you're down there. At the Cargo Hold pedestal look right: a red handle on the pipes — turn it and the room slowly drains. In the Turbine Room, swim past the pedestal into the Boiler Room: an identical handle under a light on the right, by the blue pipes. Both done = no more swimming.
The machine rotates between the four pedestals. Walk up to a pedestal it isn't at and it tells you where it is.

BO4 changed the system: there are no fixed perk machines. You pick four perks in your loadout before the game, and they map to the four altar slots you buy in order at 2500 / 3000 / 3500 / 4000.
