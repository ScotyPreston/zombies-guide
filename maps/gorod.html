Gorod Krovi

Black Ops 3 · Stalingrad, dragons overhead

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4.
  • Power + S.O.P.H.I.A. activated — flip the switch in Dragon Command, then press the green button in front of S.O.P.H.I.A. (easy to miss, required for the quest).
  • The Gauntlet of Siegfried and a Dragon Strike controller.
  • Recommended: the Fafnir shield, the upgraded Dragon Strike, and a PaP'd NX ShadowClaw for instant revives.

"Love and War" — the crew has to free (and then fight) Nikolai 1.0, stuck in a powerless mech.

The full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Collect the 6 trophies. They can be done in any order, any time.
  2. Start the generator. In the Pack-a-Punch area by the egg incubator: get a Valkyrie drone destroyed right next to it (or lure it into shooting the generator), then interact.
    It runs for 5 minutes. You can restart it with one press — but only once per round. Read the valve step before starting it.
  3. Solve the valves. Six valves around the map, each settable to 1, 2 or 3. One has a green light (the start), one holds a code cylinder (the finish). Set the other valves so the air flows from green to cylinder.
    Don't work it out by hand — the gives you the exact settings for your game.
  4. KRONOS. Put the cylinder into S.O.P.H.I.A.'s machine. Six lettered cylinders sit on its left — shooting one turns it to the next letter. Spell K R O N O S, then interact with S.O.P.H.I.A.
  5. Place the trophies — then beat the challenges. The six trophies go on the desk under the green screen in front of S.O.P.H.I.A. Using the console starts one random challenge at a time from the list below — timer running, zombies spawning endlessly. Read them before you start:
    1. Bomb defusal — defuse 6 bombs (3 solo) in 2 minutes. The order flashes ONCE on the control screen — slowly, after the fast random flashes. Split the memorising between players.
    2. Gersh's soul — a yellow orb wanders the map. Shoot it with a Pack-a-Punched gun until he speaks, follow, repeat, all the way to Dragon Command — then walk up to him.
    3. Friendly Mangler — a green-eyed Mangler follows one player. Lead him to the corner right of the trophies in Dragon Command. Shooting his arm off speeds him up — just don't kill him.
    4. Friendly Valkyrie — same, but the drone only moves while a player is near it.
    5. Groph pod — a pod lands outside the map at a dragon platform. Defend it, then send the Gauntlet's dragon to fetch the part, and give it to S.O.P.H.I.A.
  6. The data card. Always the last challenge: take the card from the console, carry it to the red computer in the Pack-a-Punch area, and survive the 2-minute Mangler lockdown while it downloads. Bring the card back to S.O.P.H.I.A. and she hands over the power core.
  7. Deliver the core. Back at Belinski Square, send the Gauntlet's dragon to Nikolai 1.0, stuck in his mech nearby.
  8. The dragon fight.

    No turning back from here

    • PaP'd guns, full perks, fresh shields, gums.
    • A PaP'd NX ShadowClaw revives downed players instantly with one shot — bring one.
    • The GKZ-45 Mk3's Ray Gun Mk3 does massive boss damage.
    Everyone stands on the grid S.O.P.H.I.A. sat on — you're flung into a new arena. Press the middle button to start. Phase 1: you fight a dragon alongside Nikolai. Survive the zombie flood until Nikolai's mech shoots the dragon — a weak spot glows orange (wing, belly, neck). Dump everything into it. The arena edges stay clear of dragon fire — hug them.
  9. Nikolai vs Nikolai. After the cutscene, the mech turns on you: chain gun, electric harpoon, RAPS drones. Shoot the glowing yellow lights on the robot — the last one is under the pilot seat.
    The chain gun will shred you in seconds — never stand in its line.

The speed run reward
Beat the quest in under 1h30

Finishing "Love and War" gives all 9 perks. Finish it in under 1 hour 30 and those perks become permanent — you keep them through downs. Solo you lose only Quick Revive, and your next Wunderfizz spin is guaranteed to return it.

Wonder Weapons

The GKZ-45 Mk3 comes from the Mystery Box — its Ray Gun Mk3 mode is the best boss-killer on the map. These two are the ones you earn:

Gauntlet of Siegfried
Raise a baby dragon — needed for the quest

A dragon whelp on your arm: flamethrower up close, or send him out to hunt — and several quest steps need him as your fetch-dragon. Track progress on your grave near spawn (green torch = yours).

  1. Get the egg. In the Pack-a-Punch area by the sewer exit, look up: a big green egg. Shoot it down, grab it.
  2. Warm it. Put it on a nest (infirmary, armory, in front of the power building, or by the spawn graves) and get it torched — wait for a dragon strafing run, or hit it with a Dragon Strike.
    Then stay away a full round so it cools — hang around and the dragon may roast it again.
  3. Kill ~15 napalm zombies — burning zombies in any dragon-fire zone. No fanfare when it's done; check the ring around the egg icon (Tab).
  4. Collateral kills — kill multiple zombies with single bullets a few times. A Drakon or SVG works; an Insta-Kill counts too.
  5. ~15 melee kills — shield, Bowie or plain knife.
  6. Incubate. Back at Pack-a-Punch, place the egg in the incubator underground — lockdown, kill zombies to charge it, finish the round, take the egg, and claim the Gauntlet at your grave.
Dragon Strike controller
Call in dragon fire — needed for the quest

An airstrike that attracts every zombie to the target before the fire lands — Origins' napalm strike with wings.

  1. Everyone to the console. In the Pack-a-Punch house, all players interact with the upstairs console within 10 seconds.
  2. Survive 4 lockdown waves of zombies, Manglers and Valkyries. First three waves open one window at a time; the last opens everything.
    With a Ray Gun Mk3, camp upstairs in the corner or by the Gobblegum machine — it trivialises this.
  3. Take the controller from the console.

Upgrading it

More damage and 2 charges (Max Ammo / new round refills one).

  1. 40 Dragon Strike kills — a loud roar confirms.
  2. Hit the flags outside the map with strikes — as many flags as there are players (1 solo).
  3. Run the lockdown again — but this time use a Dragon Strike during every wave or you start over. Then collect the upgraded controller.

Power and Pack-a-Punch

  1. Turn on the power. The switch is in the Dragon Command room — it also wakes S.O.P.H.I.A.
  2. Collect code cylinders. Zombies drop them (like seeds on Zetsubou) in blue, yellow and red. Each colour has its own decoder: blue = Dragon Command, yellow = Supply Depot, red = Tank Factory.
  3. Chase the Groph pods. Each decoded cylinder launches a pod — find it under the green beam in the sky, kill ~20 zombies around it, and take the network circuit inside. You need three.
  4. Install the circuits in the bunker underground, then pay 500 to ride a dragon from any of the three platforms — it flies you to the Pack-a-Punch area.
    Getting back: jump down the sewers below the Pack-a-Punch house.
