Shaolin Shuffle

Infinite Warfare · 1970s New York

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4.
  • A Chi at level 2 (Shurikens) — round 5 onward.
  • Pack-a-Punch open, and ideally the upgraded Kendall 44.
  • Talking to Pam in the Dojo gates almost every step — when in doubt, talk to Pam.

The full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Setup. Reach round 5, get a Chi from Pam in the Dojo (any style) and level it to 2 for the Shurikens.
  2. The rat chase. Speak to Pam to spawn rat cages around the map. Shuriken a cage to free the rat, follow it cage to cage, Shuriken each one. The final cage spawns a big yellow circle — kill zombies inside it, then kill the Shaolin zombies that follow. A key drops: take it to the lockers in the subway near Bang Bangs — inside, symbols spelling "Soul Key".
  3. Shoot the four symbols in order:
  4. First Rat King fight — the Eye. Interact with the Rat King icon directly outside the Dojo. He's a bullet sponge — dump damage until he burrows, and take the eye he leaves.
  5. The eye hunt. TALK TO PAM FIRST. Then find 6 orange Rat King symbols — press your tactical grenade button to trigger the eye's vision and see them. One spawns at a time, from 13 possible spots:
  6. The morse code phone. A non-red phone appears in one of the subways — it beeps 15 beeps = 3 digits (5 beeps each; short = dot, long = dash). Use the chart:
    Your number matches a Nightmare Summer poster — check the number in its bottom-left corner before touching it. Grabbing the wrong poster sends you back to the eye hunt. Poster spots: left of the Dojo side door · the left wall at the bottom of spawn · the right wall at the bottom of the Bang Bangs station · opposite Bombstoppers · the Disco roof opposite Quickies · the rooftops near the Dojo, right of the Magic Wheel.
  7. The spotlight and the X. Take your poster to the Disco Inferno roof and place it on the spotlight — it projects an X on the window across the roof. Blow the X up (upgraded Kendall or a grenade), then kill the Shaolin zombies that swarm the roof.
  8. The word game. Near the roof's Chi gourd: a red letter on the wall and 6 Wylard symbols around the rooftop. It's hangman — your letter plus the six symbols spell one of the known words. Interact with the symbols to spell it.
    The cheat sheet: translate each symbol with the alphabet below, then eliminate against the word list. Start from your red letter — it rules out most of the list at once.

    Confirmed words include: ACTORS, AFTERLIFE, ARCADE, BLACKCAT, DISCO, DRAGON, INFERNO, KATANA, KUNGFU, NEWYORK, PAMGRIER, PINKCAT, RATKING, ROLLERSKATES, SHAOLIN, SHUFFLE, SPACELAND, SUBWAY, TIGER, SNAKE, CRANE and more — the full list is on the chart above. The gong sound means you got it.

  9. Second Rat King fight — the Brain. The symbol is on the rooftops up the stairs from Racin' Stripes. Same fight; he drops the brain. Pick it up and SPEAK TO PAM.
    A film-burn mark flashes top-left: after 3 rounds the film "breaks" and skips you 3 rounds forward, dropping you at the Dojo. Use the wait to set up — and when it happens, kill everything and grab the golden turnstile before leaving the area.
  10. Soul rings. After placing the turnstile, climb the scaffolding in the Mule Munchies alley and shoot the Rat King symbol in the window — a golden soul ring spawns. Fill it with kills; it moves after a few — chase the noise and glow around the map until the gong.
  11. Disco hot potato. On the Disco Inferno's second floor, use the turntables: a zombie spawns with a disco ball over its head. Kill it only while another zombie stands on the checkered dance floor. Repeat until the gong.
  12. Third Rat King fight — the Heart. The symbol is outside the Disco, in front of the Pink Cat. He drops his heart — it doubles as lethal equipment.
  13. The Rat King.

    Set up FULLY before talking to Pam

    • After you talk to her, entering the Rat King Lair starts the boss fight — no backing out.
    Three phases in any order, a damage phase between each. Your three trophies (eye, brain, heart) appear around the arena — place one on the big Rat King symbol in the middle to start its phase; each completed phase pays a Max Ammo.

    Then the final damage phase: unload until he dies, watch the cutscene, and pick up the Soul Key. Done.

Wonder Weapons & special melee

Shaolin's specials are martial: the four Chi styles, and two permanent melee unlocks.

Chi fighting styles
Round 5+ — talk to Pam

Reach round 5 and talk to Pam Grier in the Black Cat Dojo — the four Chi gourds activate. Drink one to take that style; swap any time (one player per style). All four level up the same way, differing in animations and Chi power:

  1. Drink from the gourd in the Dojo
  2. 15 melee kills in that style
  3. 25 kills with the Shuriken (unlocked at level 2)
  4. 50 kills with the Chi power
The Chi melee also breaks the special doors that open fast travel — needed for Double Pack-a-Punch.
Katana
Permanent reward — 10,000 on the Dojo wall

Finish the main quest once and the Katana becomes buyable on the Black Cat Dojo wall for 10,000 — in every game after, forever.

Nunchucks
The TV quest

Requirements

  • Not as Pam Grier — one of the four main characters only. (Director's Cut already has them in the Wheel.)
  • Map fully open incl. all Chi doors and fast travels; Racin' Stripes and the upgraded Kendall 44 recommended.
  1. The alarm clocks. Three clocks around the map: activating one blurs your screen and starts a timer — melee the two hidden items it reveals before time runs out. (The guide covers the exact clock spots in a short video — the items are always near their clock.)
  2. Your character's TV. Each character has a fixed TV: activate it repeatedly until your character's picture appears, then kill the Shaolin zombies. Flip the round and do the TV step a second time.
  3. Punch all three clocks in quick succession — a Fire Sale drops, and the Magic Wheel during it guarantees the Nunchucks. They stay in the Wheel forever after.

Double Pack-a-Punch

  1. Open the two Chi doors (round 5+, any Chi's melee power):
  2. Grab the fuses — it's timed. In the Pack-a-Punch projector room, press the button on the left, then sprint to the fast-travel door by Blue Bolts, pop out the other side, U-turn and look left — the fuses sit there.
  3. Charge them on the tracks. In the subway by the Pink Cat, place the fuses on the sparking rails (Bang Bangs side is easiest) and let the train run them over — don't be on the tracks when it does.
  4. Slot the charged fuses into the machine — Double Pack unlocked.
