Der Eisendrache

Black Ops 3 · Griffin Castle, Austria

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4. Fully soloable, but the four soul circles are much easier with two bows.
  • An upgraded bow.
  • A Ragnarok DG-4 each for the boss fight.
  • Set aside 2–2½ hours. This is a long one.

The goal: kill the original Dempsey, who's frozen in a rocket pointed at the Moon base.

Read a couple of steps ahead before you start. Several steps have to be set up before the one you're on.

The full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Charge the teleporter. Take the basic bow — if you already upgraded yours, stand at its altar and interact to drop it, then pick the plain Wrath of the Ancients back up where you originally got it. In the teleporter room, hit each of the 6 blue glowing balls on the teleporter with a charged arrow. They turn gold when it works.
  2. Shoot 4 electrified objects. You'll hear a roar — that means an object somewhere on the map is electrified. Find it and shoot it with an upgraded bow. Another roar follows, and so on: 4 objects, drawn from these 8 possible spots.
    You redo this every time the round flips, right up until about step 10. It's what lets the teleporter send you back in time, which you need in steps 3 and 9.
  3. Travel back in time. The teleporter pad now glows purple. Every player has to stand on it. You arrive in the same room in the past — no zombies, no weapons. Do three things before it sends you back automatically:
    1. Grab the soul canister on your left
    2. Grab the two fuses on your right, on a box
    3. Watch Dr. Groph type the safe code and remember it
    The code is 3 symbols long, drawn from four possible symbols — arrow, circle, card, lightning — and symbols can repeat.
  4. Set the Death Ray to Protect. Go up to the castle roof and put one of your fuses into the central machine, then go round the other side and set the display to "Protect".
  5. Enter the code. Hellhounds show up and a console near the clock tower switches on. Enter the code you memorised, top to bottom.
    If the console is dead, you haven't fired the Death Ray at least once this game — do that first. Get the code wrong and you have to time travel again to watch it.
  6. Empty the safe and load the coils. Back down in the teleporter room the safe is now open — take the two fuses and the card inside. Carry the fuses up to the roof and put one in each of the two Tesla coils: look at the sides, each has three fuse slots with one empty. Then set the Death Ray back to "Destroy".
  7. Two memory games. Each console shows the four symbols — arrow, circle, card, lightning — then goes dark and flashes one symbol on the main screen. You have a couple of seconds to hit the screen that showed it.
    Hellhounds spawn without limit from the moment you start a game until you finish it, and failing the second one means redoing the first. Keep a crawler alive, and repeat the four symbols' order in your head as soon as they appear.
  8. Shoot down the rocket. Back to the roof: set the Death Ray to "Protect" again, then hit the green button behind the machine — and get well clear of the second courtyard. The coils bring the rocket down there, cracking open Dempsey's cryo cell. Pick up the Vril generator by the wreck.
  9. Time travel again. Use the teleporter a second time. If it isn't glowing, flip the round and redo step 2. In the past, use the book on your right — it opens a crate by the exit. Take the ornamented stone from it.
  10. Follow the ghost — four soul circles. Go underground to where you picked up the bow and put the rod into the grave. A ghost appears and moves around the map, stopping at 4 spots. Stand in the circle at each stop and kill zombies — your screen glows the colour of the bow you have to use, and a soul leaves the body when it's the right one.
    Infinite hellhounds the whole time, and Panzers turn up too. Spam charged shots until the ghost moves. With two players you can cover all four circles with two bows — take the lightning bow if you can, its charged shot is by far the strongest.

    When the circles are done, put the stone in the slot near Double Tap, then follow the ghost underground and place the blue soul canister by the pyramid.

  11. The boss fight.

    Gear up before this step

    • Jugger-Nog and Quick Revive, a full shield, a good gun and your bow.
    • Everyone needs a Ragnarok DG-4.
    Every player drops their Ragnarok on the glowing blue tiles (the anti-gravity ones) to get teleported into a dark red circular room. Skeletons spawn — ignore them and keep moving. Now and then the boss teleports to the middle and charges a lethal electric attack that kills everyone in the room. Two ways to survive it: hide behind a pillar, or drop a Ragnarok on the glowing ball that appears in the little hole in the centre. Doing that weakens him and the symbol on his chest lights up — that's when you damage him.
    Use uncharged arrows, not charged ones — they do more damage here. Each time you take him down far enough he vanishes and sends Panzers at you; kill them and he returns. Repeat until he goes out in a white flash.
  12. Finish it. Put the summoning key in the pyramid, then pick it straight back up. Take it up the stairs by the clock tower and place it in the console where you entered the code. That rains hell down on the Moon base — sit back for the cutscene.

Side Easter Eggs

Eleven of them, songs included — sorted quickest first.

Secret song — "Dead Again"
3 teddy bears

Kevin Sherwood / Elena Siegman. Interact with all three teddies.

Secret song — the requiem
3 gramophones

A piece of an Anton Reicha requiem. Interact with the three gramophones.

Samantha's Lullaby
1 music box

A reworked Samantha's Lullaby plays from the music box on the table next to her bed.

Disco ball
Shoot the moon, then the rocket

In the church's command room there's a globe on the ceiling with a moon and a rocket orbiting it. Shoot the moon to stop it, then shoot the rocket when it's right beside the moon. Disco lights, disco music.

Free Death Machine
One wall-run

In the pyramid room, wall-run the blue square facing the Pack-a-Punch machine. A Death Machine power-up appears above the crafting table in the same room.

Free Mega Gobblegum
A plant and a time trip
  1. Take the plant off the shelf in the teleporter room (through the pyramid underground).
  2. Travel to the past — either main quest steps 1–2, or the Plunger method below — and put the plant back on the same shelf there.
  3. Back in the present, the plant has grown: interact with it for a random Mega Gobblegum.
Panzer Soldat helmet
3 claw kills

Wear a Panzer's helmet and take less damage from Panzers. Kill three of them with the hidden ceiling claws: shoot a claw down, then shoot it again as a Panzer walks in front — it launches forward and kills him.

The Plunger
Melee weapon — and a Panzer one-shot

Requirements

  • The fire-quest wall in the clock tower already broken open.
  • A Ragnarok DG-4.
  1. Place the Ragnarok in the broken wall opening in the clock tower.
  2. Stop the clock at 9:35. Downstairs, the little lever by the Wundersphere-landing door starts the clock ticking; interacting stops or restarts it. Stop it so it lands on 9:35 — done right it reads 9:36.
    Solo trick: stop it around 9:00–9:15 first, restart, then count the seconds in your head and stop as you're saying "9:34". A crawler or a slow-zombies gum makes this far less stressful.
  3. Wall-run to the past. In the pyramid room a glowing ball now floats above the pyramid. Wall-run the symbol on your right coming down the stairs — after a few runs you're teleported. The plunger is on the table left of the teleporter.
Upgrade: kill a Panzer — with anything — and the plunger catches fire for 4 minutes, during which it one-shots Panzers.
Second gondola supplies
Grenades in, 5 fuses later

Putting a fuse in the spawn panel calls the cable car with a small supply drop. There's a better one: at the very start of the game, throw all your grenades into the gondola before it leaves. Keep feeding fuses as normal — the fifth fuse summons a second gondola with much better supplies.

Skeleton zombies
In Plain Sight + 3 hidden skulls

Requirements

  • The Wrath of the Ancients (base bow).
  • The “In Plain Sight” Gobblegum — the skulls are invisible without it.

Pop the gum and shoot the three floating skulls with the bow — from then on every zombie spawns as a skeleton with new sounds. Completely useless, very funny.

Turning it off: the three skulls sit on a chair in the church in front of the dragon — interact with them to get normal zombies back.
Unlock the BRM wall-buy
Wall-run every blue square

During anti-gravity in the underground, wall-run across every blue square on the walls — touching the ground between them is fine here. The BRM light machine gun then becomes a 1800-point wall-buy next to the crafting table in the same room.

Wonder Weapons — the bows

One base bow, four elemental upgrades. Build the base bow first, then pick an element — each player can carry a different one, and the main quest needs at least one upgraded bow in the game.

Which to build? The Storm bow is the strongest and the fastest to build. The Wolf bow makes crawlers on demand, which is handy for holding rounds. Fire stuns crowds; Void is strong but takes the longest.
Wrath of the Ancients
The base bow — feed 3 dragons

Explosive arrows, chargeable for more damage. Easy to get — doable before round 6 — and it replaces one of your weapons. You need it to build any of the upgrades, and an upgraded bow is required for the main quest.

  1. Feed the three dragons — 8 zombies each. Kill zombies close to a dragon and it snatches the bodies.
    Wait for the eating animation to finish before killing the next zombie — kills during the animation don't count.
  2. Collect the bow. Head up toward the church, into the room with the armoured grave. The bow is waiting on it.
Fire Bow — Rune Prison
Charged shots stun in an area
  1. Break the wall at the clock tower. Upstairs in the clock tower, shoot a charged arrow at the marked wall — it crumbles and drops the quest item.
  2. Catch the fireball at the rocket test. Go down to the landing pad (underground, past the pyramid). When a rocket test starts, the doors seal — wait inside the teleporter area or the thrusters kill you. The moment the test ends, run out while the doors open, look right, and shoot the glowing ball just above the concrete building. You have about 10 seconds.
    Miss the shot? It comes back at the next rocket test.
  3. Shoot 3 red circles — from the air. Launch yourself with the Wunderspheres and shoot each circle with a charged arrow mid-flight; they only glow while you're airborne. A hit stays lit and plays a sound.
    Which launch for which circle: the courtyard sphere for the Death Ray circle · the room-above-Double-Tap sphere for the clock tower circle · the Death Ray or landing pad sphere for the last one.
  4. Stand in each circle and kill. Kills count anywhere as long as you're standing in the circle — a ray of fire reaches up to the sun when a circle is done. Do all three.
  5. The clock and the fireplaces. Use the clock in the clock tower — one of three ground circles lights up. From that circle, shoot a charged arrow at the ground to spawn a blazing rock, then stand on the rock and shoot again — 3 shots total to walk it into its fireplace. Shoot as far as you can each time.
    Which circle feeds which fireplace: the Double Tap circle → Samantha's room · the top-of-castle circle → the church fireplace (second dragon) · the clock tower circle → the fireplace upstairs by the first dragon, near the Wunderfizz.
  6. Pop the sun. Back on top of the castle, the sun now floats at your height — interact with it. It rises, explodes, and leaves the reforged arrow.
  7. Charge it at the altar. Take the arrow to the altar room under the pyramid and place it on the matching altar. Kill zombies right next to it until the souls stop flowing in and a sound plays, then interact with the arrow, let the animation finish, and pick up your upgraded bow.
Wolf Bow — Wolf Howl
Uncharged shots turn a zombie into a crawler
  1. Activate the 4 paintings in story order. Interact with a painting: right one, the green symbol stays and your character speaks; wrong one, it fades. A wrong pick resets the chain — you must redo the ones before it. The order is always the story of the Wolf King:
    Where the paintings hang: past Samantha's room near the 1500 door · on the church balcony · in the clock tower by the stairs · in the room beneath the clock tower, near Mule Kick.
  2. Collect the item underground. The wall on the left after the underground stairs is now broken open — the item is on the floor.
  3. Shoot the flag, take the skull. From the landing pad, look back above the entrance: a flag stands on the mountain. Charge-shoot its base, then pick the glowing skull up off the ground. Place it on the headless wolf skeleton by the spirit altar.
  4. Follow the spectral wolf. He stops at three bone piles — kill zombies around each pile until he scratches it open, and pick up the piece before moving on.
  5. The wall in the basement. He finally sits against a basement wall. Above him is a hole with a wolf symbol that only appears while you're wall-running on the anti-gravity — shoot the symbol while it's lit. A platform rises: stand on it, interact with the hole, then interact again for the arrow.
  6. Charge it at the altar. Take the arrow to the altar room under the pyramid and place it on the matching altar. Kill zombies right next to it until the souls stop flowing in and a sound plays, then interact with the arrow, let the animation finish, and pick up your upgraded bow.
Storm Bow
The strongest bow — and the easiest to build
  1. Shoot the weathervane on top of the castle, then collect the broken arrow it drops.
  2. Ignite 3 woodsheds with charged arrows.
  3. Wall-run the 5 blue symbols. The underground cavern walls hold five blue symbols. Wait for an anti-gravity sequence, then run across all five without touching the ground. Done right, they stay lit when gravity returns.
  4. Kill 6 zombies at each of 3 electric jars.
  5. Electrify the burning sheds. Aim at a charged jar until your arrow crackles, then — without lowering the bow — shoot a burning woodshed. Each jar works once, so it's one jar per shed.
  6. Take the arrow from the storm. The weathervane is now spinning in its own thunderstorm — press action on the blue cloud at the bottom of that tower.
  7. Charge it at the altar. Take the arrow to the altar room under the pyramid and place it on the matching altar. Kill zombies right next to it until the souls stop flowing in and a sound plays, then interact with the arrow, let the animation finish, and pick up your upgraded bow.
Void Bow — Demon Gate
Powerful area damage — the longest build
  1. Start it: charge-shoot the void symbol on the tunnel roof near Double Tap, just above the 1000-point gate.
  2. The purple square. In the trophy room beneath the clock tower, melee-kill a zombie on the glowing purple square, then interact with the broken tile to release a floating jar.
  3. Touch the 6 skulls around the map:
  4. Feed 6 crawlers to the circle. Lead six crawlers into the circle in the trophy room — the skulls eat them. Then charge-shoot the flying jar. A demonic voice says three words — write them down in order. They come from: door, stag, horn, heart, crown, griffin.
  5. Farm the 6 purple symbols. Zombies anywhere randomly drop purple symbols — but a dropped symbol vanishes after 5 seconds, so grab them fast. The circle shows how many you have.
  6. The armour corridor. Between the power lever and Samantha's quarters stand 6 suits of armour, each with a sigil. Using the chart below, interact with the three armours matching your three words, in the order the voice said them. Each shows a purple symbol — remember those too, in order.
  7. Enter the name. Back at the circle, shoot the glowing symbols in that same order. Wrong code and the skulls attack — leave the room and try again. Right code, and the voice says it is his name. Interact with the tile for the arrow.
  8. Charge it at the altar. Take the arrow to the altar room under the pyramid and place it on the matching altar. Kill zombies right next to it until the souls stop flowing in and a sound plays, then interact with the arrow, let the animation finish, and pick up your upgraded bow.

Buildables

Two builds, both worth it: the shield for staying alive, the Ragnarok because the main quest requires one per player.

Rocket Shield
3 parts · 3 spawns each

Blocks from behind like every shield — plus a rocket boost that blasts zombies out of your way. Three parts, each with three possible spots.

  1. The double pipe — around the first courtyard:
  2. The griffin plate — the second courtyard, in front of the church:
  3. The frame — underground, and you can only grab it during anti-gravity:
  4. Build it at a crafting table — underground near the pyramid, or above Double Tap.
Ragnarok DG-4
3 parts — needed for the main quest

The map's specialist weapon: slams the ground with gravity, and planted on the ground it projects a force field — perfect for safe revives. Just remember to pick it back up. Everyone needs one for the boss fight.

  1. Part 1 — kill the first Panzer. He arrives around round 12 and drops the part instead of a power-up.
  2. Part 2 — fire the Death Ray, then look up: the part is floating in the air. Launch yourself at it with a Wundersphere — the one above Double Tap if it floats over spawn, the second-courtyard one if it floats over the Death Ray. It despawns if you're slow.
  3. Part 3 — the rocket test. At the launch site, when the voice announces a test, pull the lever by the left staircase, then shelter in the safe area. After the test, watch the box with 3 lights by the teleporter: they go red to green. When all three are green, pull its lever — the part is waiting inside the teleporter.
  4. Build it at a crafting table and pick your Ragnaroks up.

Pack-a-Punch

  1. Turn on the power — the switch is in the Armory.
  2. Stand on all three teleporter pads, in any order. Each one beams a piece of the machine away:
    • Opposite the power switch, just outside in the Bastion
    • Up on the catwalks of the rocket pad
    • In the Undercroft — the underground pyramid room
  3. The machine assembles at the Undercroft pad. Normal 5000 to upgrade.
The Undercroft pad is also where the Rocket Shield's third part can float above during anti-gravity — two birds, one visit.
