Rave in the Redwoods

Infinite Warfare · Camp Redwood, 1990s

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4 — soloable. Co-op only changes how many lights and skulls you have to handle.
  • Power on and Pack-a-Punch open —
  • An upgraded crossbow is a huge help for the boss —
You lose a perk every time you fail a ritual. That stings badly in Director's Cut, where a lost Bang Bangs can't be re-bought (it's bugged).

Loadout advice from the guide: bring an accurate AR like the Volk — avoid the FHR, it'll make rituals 1 and 3 miserable. Grab the Balloon Trap (2 blue + 1 orange in a totem) or Gas Grenades before ritual 2.

"Rave in the Redwoods" — full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Talk to Kevin Smith on Turtle Island to spawn the first ritual picture.
  2. Ritual 1 — shoot the ARMS off. Grab the picture in the Recreation Area behind the big Rave statue, next to a wall-buy. Place it at the bonfire just outside spawn (use the door on the Up 'N Atoms side). You drop into Rave mode with zombies pouring in — shoot their arms off, quickly, it's timed. Each gib sends a soul to the picture.
    The slasher that spawns is invulnerable until Rave mode fully ends — then killing it is also timed. It drops a Max Ammo. Talk to Kevin again after picking the picture up.
  3. Ritual 2 — shoot the FEET off. The picture is upstairs in spawn where Tuff 'Nuff is, on the floor immediately right as you walk in. Place it in the Recreation Area directly in front of Quickies. Same drill — gib feet, kill the slasher, talk to Kevin.
    This is the ritual where the Balloon Trap or Gas Grenades really earn their keep.
  4. Ritual 3 — shoot the HEADS off. The skull is in the Power room, on the floor left of the Magic Wheel. Place it on the beach in front of Slappy Taffy. Gib heads, kill the slasher, pick the skull back up.
  5. Trigger the boss fight. Head to the power room — there are interactable lights, one per player (solo = 1, four players = 4). Everyone hits a light at the same time; you'll hear an alarm and a portal open. Then all players board the boat to Turtle Island, where Kevin is waiting.
    Once you hit the lights you can no longer Pack-a-Punch. Be completely set up before you do this.
  6. Boss — Phase I: raise the skulls. Kill zombies next to each skull to raise it (2 skulls solo, more in co-op). Raise them close together in time — a skull left up too long while others lag will sink again. When they're all up, a blue symbol appears somewhere on the island: lure the Super Slasher into it, he freaks out and a purple symbol appears on his body — shoot it.
  7. Phase II: break the symbols. He keeps chasing; keep shooting the purple symbol on his body. Each one you break spawns another. Eventually he stops, beats his chest, spawns two saw blades and leaps onto the roof.
  8. Phase III: firestorm. With him on the roof, green circles appear on the island — get inside one before the firestorm hits, they're the only protection and it does heavy damage fast. Skeleton zombies spawn while you're in there; your crossbow shreds them.
  9. The loop, and the damage phase. Those three phases repeat three times, with blue walls closing the island in tighter each loop. On the third loop the firestorm phase is replaced by the damage phase — just unload into him (a fast SMG like the FHR or the Mauler is ideal here). He sinks into the ground and drops the Soul Key.
  10. Collect the Soul Key — easter egg complete.

Music Easter Egg — "Rave On"10 glowing spots around spawn, in Rave mode ›

Requirements

  • Power on, and a Rave pouch to enter Rave mode.

Enter Rave mode at the spawn fireplace (left of the door leading to the Mess Hall), then find 10 glowing spots around the spawn building. The spots never move, and you can spread it over multiple pouches — but it's doable in one if you're quick.

  1. Ceiling directly above the fireplace where you entered Rave mode
  2. Up the stairs in spawn, immediately right before you go through the doorway
  3. Under the wooden table left of the pool table
  4. Behind the bar to the right of the pool table
  5. Through the door to the balcony left of the pool table, right above the doorway
  6. Left of the door to the Adventure Course, on a roof outside the playable area
  7. Under the desk with the computer in spawn
  8. Walking into the Tuff 'Nuff room, immediately left behind some boxes
  9. Left of where the boat piece spawns, under the shelf in the wardrobe
  10. Left of the Tuff 'Nuff machine, between the cabinets

Hit the last one and the XP pops with the achievement. The song also unlocks as lobby music.

Kill the Slasher — "Tables Turned"Post-quest · the Smiley · round 10+ ›

Requirements

  • The main Easter egg must be finished first — same game or a later one.
  • Round 10 or higher, and a Rave pouch.
  1. Grab the Smiley. In the power room, left of the table where you throw knives at the zombie on the wheel, it hangs from a bar — it's free.
  2. On round 10+, throw a pouch into the fire to enter Rave mode — the slasher only shows up from round 10.
  3. Hold ADS (right mouse / left trigger) to wind the Smiley up and get the blade spinning, then walk into the slasher with it.
    He does a lot of damage very fast — don't linger.
  4. Enough damage and he vanishes, popping the "Tables Turned" achievement.

Shared setup — do this first9 statue spawns · speakers · big statue · 15-20 Vlad kills ›

All four upgraded crossbows use the exact same three steps — only the statue changes. Read this once and each weapon card is just "which statue, and where's its big version".

Requirements

  • The Vlad — you need it in hand to fill a statue and to pick the upgrade up.
  • On Director's Cut you can skip all of this and pull upgraded crossbows straight from the Magic Wheel.
You can work more than one statue at a time. Every statue must be filled using the Vlad, and the finished upgrade can only be collected while the Vlad is in your inventory.
  1. Step 1 — collect a statue. Any of the four animals can spawn at any of these nine spots:
  2. Step 2 — feed it souls on the speakers. Take the statue to the Recreation Area in front of the big Rave statue (left of Quickies) and place it on the speakers. Damage zombies just enough that they burst into dust — a low-powered weapon is far more efficient for this.
  3. Step 3 — take it to its big version. When the pick-up prompt returns, carry the statue to its matching large statue and place it in front. Then get 15–20 kills with the Vlad close in, and interact with the statue to claim the crossbow.
The VladBase crossbow · sausage + 3 deer-head symbols ›

Requirements

  • A sausage from the bucket on Turtle Island near the projector.
  • A Rave mode pouch — these drop from zombies as you play.

This unlocks the container in spawn holding the crossbows. It's the prerequisite for every upgrade below.

  1. Enter Rave mode, then at each location throw a sausage at the deer head. It pops off the wall — shoot it and a symbol drops. Pick the symbol up.
  2. Three symbol locations: 1. Bear Lodge (spawn) — right of where you pick up the crossbows 2. Mess Hall — above the fireplace 3. Camper Cabins — round the back, inside one of the cabins
  3. Try for one Rave mode. Use the "listen to the fairies" prompts to extend it and all three are doable on a single pouch.

Double Pack-a-Punch

If you've completed the Zombies in Spaceland main quest, Double Pack is already unlocked — skip this entirely. Open the Spaceland guide
  1. Repair the projector first (above).
  2. Turn on the computer. Back at spawn there's an old 90s computer on the front desk — interact with it and you'll hear dial-up noises.
  3. Wait two full rounds. The Alien Fuses then spawn to the right of the computer — pick them up and install them in the Pack-a-Punch machine.
