The Beast from Beyond

Infinite Warfare · A military base on a distant ice moon

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4. The shortest main quest in Infinite Warfare.
  • Power on and the Pack-a-Punch bridge repaired.
  • The Entangler — step 1 gets it.

The full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Get the Entangler. It's the first two steps of this map's Ghosts 'n Skulls egg. Explode a box (direct hit) at one of three spots — the first skull pops out; shoot it. Then everyone stands on a red X in the Cargo Bay: the X turns to a green circle, zombies flood in, and nobody steps outside their circle (one foot out = wait a round and retry). A skull rises in the middle, teleports somewhere in the room — sprint to it and jump through it. The Entangler spawns centre-room.
  2. Restore Neil — the four floppy disks. Each disk carries a symbol; the order they go into Neil depends on those symbols.
    1. Disk 1: Entangler-grab the disk under the Hallway stairs and shoot it into the vent — it pops out of another vent in the Hallway or spawn. Check them all.
    2. Disk 2: Entangler-grab the space helmet in the Hallway, carry it to the Medbay and throw it at the monitor — the force field drops; the disk is on the counter.
    3. Disk 3: drops from your first Phantom kill (Phantoms start spawning after your first Pack-a-Punch trip). Kill it near Neil in spawn to save yourself the walk.
    4. Disk 4: just lying by a pole behind the Pack-a-Punch portal.
    The order: match your four symbols to the chart — it tells you exactly which disk goes in first through fourth.
  3. Hack Neil. Entangler-grab the red button from the hallway behind the Brute (Theater side) and shoot it into the Beast from Beyond poster in the Arcade. Then press the button that appears under the Medbay table (where disk 2 was) — the hacking puzzle lights up in the other room.
    Solving it: note every bar lying horizontal, then flip exactly those positions. Repeat if needed — solved means all bars face the same way.
  4. Escort Neil. Carry him from his terminal to the projector room and set him in the computer. Open the Pack-a-Punch door before picking him up — carrying him locks you out of door-buying.
    Interacting with Neil in the computer starts the boss fight. Gear up first.
  5. The Cryptid gauntlet.

    Boss loadout

    • Double-PaP'd Kendall 44, plus a heavy hitter for the Mammoths — the EBR-800 "Bomber" variant + Ego Trip fortune card melts them; an FHR or Mauler works too.
    1. Rhinos: the laser cracks open 4 containers — kill all four Rhinos.
    2. Survive waves of Scouts, Phantoms and the odd Rhino until 3 monitors light up around the arena.
    3. Hit all 3 monitors fast. Done right, the portals die, spawns stop, and the laser starts firing around the room.
    4. The 99-second countdown: the laser blows open a centre container revealing a timer monitor — survive to zero, then the monitor kills everything in the room...
    5. ...and spawns two Mammoths. Giant bullet sponges that leave blue fire as they take damage — keep training, keep chipping. With the Bomber: Ego Trip, aim at the head, dump a clip or two each.

Wonder Weapons

The Entangler comes from main quest step 1 — and the Venom line is this map's grind:

Venom-X
Buttons, a trapped zombie, and mazes

Requirements

  • Power on, PaP bridge repaired — but don't enter PaP yet (it makes step 1 easier).
  • The author leans on a community tool for the buttons and mazes; it's offline, but archived: web.archive.org → sneekee.github.io/DLC4-Tools. The steps below work without it, just slower.
  1. Run a Phantom through the electric trap in Water Treatment — it doesn't have to die there. (First PaP visit spawns Phantoms.)
  2. The button box. An electric panel opens in the Ops Center below Bang Bangs, showing coloured buttons (random each game). One is correct — the archived tool computes it from the count and colours; without it, expect trial and error.
  3. Trap a zombie in the 666 chamber. Lure one close and hit the console — it holds the zombie 5 minutes while you work.
  4. The mazes. Above Bombstoppers: three consoles. Activate the centre one ("Bypass SuperUser Access") and the side screens show mazes — steer the red square to the blue diamond with the Souvenir / Kung-Fu ability keys. A wrong move kills your jarred zombie and spawns a Phantom — retrap and retry next round. Solve 5–6 mazes.
    Keyboard tip: bind "Use Souvenir" to Left Arrow and "Use Kung-Fu Ability" to Right Arrow first — it turns the mazes from misery into a minigame.
  5. Claim it. The door left of the button box opens — the Venom-X is inside.
Venom-Y
Morse code and a kill quota

Requirements

  • The Venom-X in hand.
  1. The purple consoles by Bang Bangs: the top one beeps your key — first beep short = ARCHER, long = CROSS. The bottom one beeps a morse cipher containing your number.
  2. Decode your number with the matching chart:
  3. Enter it on the two-button console in the maze room: left = short beep, right = long beep. The part-pickup sound confirms.
  4. Kill that many Cryptids with the Venom-X. Track the percentage next to Venom-Y in the Tab menu — overkilling is fine.
  5. PaP the Venom-X at 100%.
Venom-Z
The final form

Requirements

  • The Venom-Y, and Double Pack-a-Punch active.
  1. Same consoles, new cipher. Your key is the opposite of last time (had Archer? now Cross). Decode the new number with the same charts and punch it in.
  2. Special kills: that many kills on any special enemy (Cryptids, Shaolins, clowns) with the Venom — brings the Tab meter to 50%.
  3. The hidden figures. On the Exterior console, cycle the year to 195-something (the last digit's blurry — that's the one). Lime-eyed figures appear around the map: stand in front of the PaP portal and shoot them from there — it only counts from that spot. Your cipher number of hits = 100%.
  4. PaP once more.

Power, Pack-a-Punch and Double Pack

Power — Neil's head

  1. In the Ops Center, jump down the big hole in the middle of the room.
  2. Neil's head lies at the end of the walkway. Take it.
  3. Set it on the console in spawn — power's back, and Cryptids stop spawning from the next round.

Pack-a-Punch — fix the bridge

  1. Piece 1 — Medical, right of Blue Bolts.
  2. Piece 2 — Cargo Bay, wedged between the stairs and boxes.
  3. Piece 3 — in the Theater: fire the Cargo Bay laser at the portal across the room to blow it open, go through, and the piece sits on a burning car on Main Street.
  4. Repair the bridge in the Exterior and cross to the PaP portal.

Double Pack-a-Punch

  1. Melee the fuse box in the hallway between spawn and Cargo — the Alien Fuses are inside. (Needs the Entangler quest done first? No — but you'll want the Entangler soon anyway.)
  2. The film reel from the Theater concessions counter goes on the projector in the PaP room (squeeze into the reel corner).
  3. Fish the Brute head out of the film. Back in the Theater, the screen now plays Spaceland. Turn on the Wormhole Trap and fire the Entangler into the wormhole — out comes a Brute's head. Drop it, pick it up.
  4. The laser charging rig: head on the headless Brute statue (Theater–Lobby hallway), fuses on the Sasquatch across from it — the Brute lasers the fuses. Collect and slot into the machine.
