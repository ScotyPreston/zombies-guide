Shadows of Evil

Black Ops 3 · Morg City, 1940s

Requirements

  • Players: 1–3 for everything up to the ending — the final step needs 4. You can bring people in just for that last part.
  • The Apothicon Sword.
  • Strongly recommended: the Apothicon Servant and the Propulsion Shield.
How a ritual works — all four are the same shape. turn into the Beast with a curse torch, power something up, collect that character's personal item, then place it on their altar and activate it. You survive in the room while Corrupted Keepers spawn — don't shoot them, just keep moving. Shooting them makes them respawn, often right on top of you. Each finished ritual leaves a portal worm to pick up.

The full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Nero's ritual — the Junction (spawn) Grab a curse torch to turn into the Beast, then hit the power box on the crane in the middle of the Junction, by the candy machine. That drops a crate which bursts open — inside is Nero's item, a feather pen. Still as the Beast, grapple up by the starting alley, cross the roofs and hit the second switch to drop the stairs so you can get back up as a human. Put the pen on the table in the ritual room and activate it.
  2. Jackie's ritual — the Canals As the Beast, drop into the canal and run to the far end — there's a wall you can pass through with a power switch behind it. Turn it on, then backtrack: the grate over a crate is now open. Break it and grab Jackie's police badge as a human. Open the door to the train station, transform, grapple the hook on the cabaret roof and power the stairs. Then place the badge inside the Rubby Rabbit.
  3. Jessica's ritual — Footlight Find a torch, grapple the hook and power the stairs. Near the perk machine there's a crate on the neighbouring ledge — jump up and shove it off with your tentacles to drop Jessica's wig. Open the door onto the road full of cars, transform again and grapple the Black Lace Burlesque tower; the switch up there opens the club. Place the wig inside.
  4. Floyd's ritual — Waterfront Head down the stairs and look up for the grapple point. Push the crate to drop Floyd's champion belt. Open the door to the train station, go down to the harbour, and use Beast form to rip open the gym doors. Place the belt inside.
  5. Open the portal. Each district has a rift portal behind a door only the Beast can break. Go down and walk at the wall of glowing symbols — with all four rituals done it vanishes. Inside, place the four worms on the four altars, wall-running across the gaps to reach them, then use the last altar to run one more ritual. This one gives you plenty of room to move, so it's easier than it looks — just don't fall off a wall-run. When it ends the Shadow Man shows his true colours and Pack-a-Punch appears.
  6. Get the Apothicon Sword — and upgrade it. You need the sword for what comes next. The whole build (train symbols → symbol wall → egg → four statues) and the upgrade live on their own tab.
  7. Free the four ghosts. In Nero's room a book is now lying on the floor to the left of the door — interact with it and it flies away. That spawns a flag in the underground.
    The moment you pick the flag up, all the zombies vanish and Insanity Elementals — fast exploding balls — take over until the step is done.
    Carry the flag to a spot marked by an electrical spark and defend it for about 30 seconds from the balls, the mosquitoes and the Shadow Man himself — he does serious damage to it. A ding means you can pick it up; do a second spark spot (you get a Max Ammo every two), then carry the flag to that district's ritual altar. A Margwa spawns; kill it and the ghost appears by its worm in the Pack-a-Punch room. Repeat for all four districts — only one per round, so you have to flip the round in between.
    If the flag gets destroyed, just pick it up in the underground and start that district again.
  8. Fight the Shadow Man. Gear up first — perks, shield, good guns — and read the final step before you start, because you can't leave the room. He sits behind an energy shield. Break it by activating the four Keepers: they're purple, turn red when activated, and only stay red a short while, so you have to sprint between them. With all four red they fire beams that drop his shield — then shoot him. He teleports around, edging closer to the table each time; when he's close enough, interact with the table to capture him. If the shield comes back, just do the Keepers again.
    Margwas keep spawning throughout. Be careful wall-running — enemies can knock you into the pit.
  9. The finale — crush the worm.

    This step needs 4 players

    • Everything before this can be done with fewer, but the ending cannot — you can bring people in for this part.
    Two things change: a pink poison cloud hits your screen every minute or two and will kill you fast — run through a white cloud to clear it — and Margwas replace the zombies. Torches are dead until you kill a Margwa next to one, which turns it blue and usable. You can revive downed teammates by calling the train.

    Then: one Beast at each of the three train stations, and the fourth player in human form calls the train. As it runs through the Junction it crushes the worm — while it's moving, the three Beasts power up the box near their station. Done right, the rails glow blue. Now all the Beast players sprint back to the Junction and hit the Keeper ghosts with the tentacle before the worm comes back.

Wonder Weapons

Two of them, and both are built rather than bought. The sword is required for the main quest; the Servant is optional but excellent.

Apothicon Sword
Required for the main quest

A melee wonder weapon, and the main quest can't be finished without one.

You can do this before or after opening the portal — it doesn't depend on the rituals.
  1. Ride the trains and memorise 3 symbols. There's a station in the Canals, Footlight and Waterfront. On each ride, watch the buildings for a glowing red symbol — one per line, marked "?" on the guide map.
    Trick: the wall you'll shoot has all 9 symbols in fixed positions, numbered 1 (bottom left) to 9 (bottom right). Learn those numbers first, then from the train you only have to remember three numbers instead of three shapes.
  2. Shoot your 3 symbols on the wall. Go underground, turn into the Beast and face the brick wall below the stairs. Hit your three symbols with the tentacle — order doesn't matter.
  3. Take an egg. The wall opens onto stones holding eggs and swords. Grab an egg.
  4. Fill it at all 4 statues. Four wooden crates around the map hide Cthulhu-like statues — smash them in Beast form, place the egg, then kill zombies next to it to feed it souls. A sound plays and the egg glows when that one's done. There's one statue in every district.
  5. Claim the sword. Take the full egg back underground and put it where you found it — a sword appears on the same altar.
Upgraded sword
Optional — 4 rituals, one per round

Upgrading is optional but it turns the sword into a monster. Which altar you use depends on who you're playing as:

CharacterTheir altar
Nero the MagicianHis apartment above the spawn area
Jackie the DetectiveThe Rubby Rabbit cabaret — Canals
Jessica the Femme FataleThe Black Lace Burlesque club — Footlight
Floyd the BoxerThe gym by the harbour — Waterfront
  1. Get the dark orb. A blue ghost is floating at your altar — interact with it.
  2. Do 4 circle rituals. With the orb you'll see glowing circles in front of each ritual room and in the middle of the Junction. Stand in one and start it — 1 to 3 Margwas spawn and chase only you. Kill them all to finish that circle.
    One ritual per round, per player — and only one can be running on the map at a time. You have to flip the round between each.
  3. Trade it in. With all four done, go back to the ghost and hand over your sword. It gives you back the upgraded blade.
Apothicon Servant
3 parts · craft at any table

The map's wonder weapon — it fires black holes that suck in everything nearby. Three parts, then build it at a crafting table.

  1. Part 1 — from the first Margwa. It drops when you kill the first one. If you miss it, later Margwas keep dropping it until you pick it up.
  2. Part 2 — a random drop. Killing Insanity Elementals or the mosquitos eventually drops a glowing green piece. This one is pure luck, so keep killing them.
  3. Part 3 — from a purple pod. The plant-like pods around the map ripen over time: green → red → purple. Only a purple one can give you the tentacle, and even then it's a roll of the dice — open purple pods until you get it.

Buildables

Everything here is assembled at a crafting table. Build the shield first — it's the one that keeps you alive.

Crafting tables
3 of them — where you build everything

Everything on this tab gets assembled at one of three tables, one per district. You'll only ever need two, so you get to pick where things live — but once something is built there you can't move it, so choose a table near where you like to train.

Propulsion Shield
3 parts · one per district

Blocks everything behind you and has a boost that launches zombies away when you melee with it. On Black Ops 3 this is the single best survival item on the map — build it early.

  1. Footlight part. By the perk machine and the box — on a bench, propped against the perk's fence, or just inside on the left before the stairs.
  2. Canals part. In the perk area — in the room itself, out on the bridge, or in the room past the stairs.
  3. Waterfront part. Near the Waterfront perk — in the mess past the stairs, by the window opposite the perk, or next to the Cthulhu statue.
  4. Build it at whichever table suits you.
Each part has a few possible spawns within its area, so check all of them — the shots show the general spot, not a fixed shelf.
Civil Protector
3 fuses · then 2000 to call him

A robot ally that drops out of the sky, kills zombies, revives you and your teammates, and flies around on jump boots. Zombies ignore him completely. He leaves on his own after a while.

  1. Find 3 fuses — blue glowing cylinders, one in each district. They move around within the area shown.
  2. Slot them into the fuse box in the underground.
  3. Call him for 2000 from a red box — there's one in each district plus the Junction.
    You can pool points. Put in whatever you have and a teammate tops up the rest — it doesn't have to be 2000 from one person. There's also an achievement for calling him from every box.

Pack-a-Punch

There is no power switch to flip on this map. Pack-a-Punch appears in the underground portal room the moment you finish the four rituals and the portal ritual.
