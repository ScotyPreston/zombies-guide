Zombies in Spaceland

Infinite Warfare · 1980s theme park

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4 — fully soloable. Solo or 2 players is easiest: the boss fight spawns one Alien per player.
  • You must build one of the following: Head Cutter, Shredder, Face Melter, or Dischord — you will need it for the final step.

"Sooooul Key" — full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Open Pack-a-Punch. Turn on all five power switches, ride all four district portals, then ride the central portal to the Projector Room. While you're in there, tap the mini-UFO table in the back-left corner — it gates the later quests. You will NOT get the Seti-Com pickup cue until you have been through the PaP portal. Full walkthrough with screenshots — see Pack-a-Punch tab
  2. Collect the 3 Seti-Com parts: the Umbrella, the Calculator, and the Boom Box. Each has 3 possible spawns. All 9 spawn locations with screenshots — see Buildables tab, Seti-Com card
  3. Get the built Seti-Com from the Hoff. With the parts collected, the PaP portal visited, and the next round started, the UFO on top of Polar Peak makes sounds and changes colors — that's the cue. Hand the parts to David Hasselhoff at his DJ booth and he gives back the assembled Seti-Com (it appears in your sticker pack). He must not be active as your summoned ally.
  4. Complete the 3 Seti-Com defenses — 60, then 90, then 120 seconds. Only one SUCCESSFUL defense counts per round. Walk the map until the screen shakes, place the Seti-Com on the marked spot, and keep every zombie off it. If you fail, a Brute spawns — kill it and you can attempt another defense the same round. 3 random sites per match from these 7 possible locations: 1. Journey Into Space — next to the Escape Velocity trap (look for the red blood stain) 2. Journey Into Space — outside Rover Rampage near the Magic Wheel 3. Underground — directly in front of the Racin' Stripes perk 4. Kepler — across from Bombstoppers, in the corner closest to the Space Croc Chomp 5. Polar Peak — directly in front of the entrance coming from Cosmic Way 6. Polar Peak — in the middle of the gift shop 7. Polar Peak — across from Bang Bangs in the Spaceship Splash area
  5. Wait two full rounds, then collect the Hoff's radios (he won't hand them over earlier — hard timer, not a bug). Return to the PaP portal and place the four radios: one on each of the 3 symbols around the portal's perimeter, plus one directly in front of it.
    Set up BEFORE activating the radios — activating them starts the boss fight.
  6. Simon Says. Activate one radio and they all light up, each showing a color (set by your placement order — note which is which). The UFO flashes a color sequence; repeat it on the radios, three times, each sequence longer. In co-op it takes multiple players to hit the radios inside the timer. Fail = the UFO turns white and a Brute spawns — kill it and try again. Use the in-app tracker —
  7. Clown wave. Pure survival — kill all the exploding clowns until the Alien spawns. Tip from the guide: hold the Polar Peak entrance so clowns and zombies only come from in front of you.
  8. Alien boss fight (one per player). Damage it with a Weapon of Rock — the collar on its neck turns red when it's close to a down. When it drops and puts up its yellow shield, sprint behind it and melee its backpack to break a fuse (you only get a few seconds; it can also spawn zombies while down). Break both fuses across two downs; the third down kills it. Each Alien drops an Auto-Nuke, Max Ammo and 1,000 XP.
  9. Upgrade your Weapon of Rock. The Alien Fuses appear in front of the PaP portal. Take them, insert them into the Pack-a-Punch machine, and upgrade your Weapon of Rock for 5,000 points.
  10. Shoot down the UFO. It now chases you, firing electricity balls. From Cosmic Way, look at the back of the big circular SPACELAND sign above the twin bridges — it has 5 blue glowing lights. Shoot all five when the UFO is directly above the PaP portal: the sign fires a vertical beam that destroys anything above it.
  11. Pick up the Soul Key piece — it spawns in front of the PaP portal. Collecting it completes the Easter egg.

Ghosts 'n Skulls quest6 skulls + minigame → all perks permanently ›

Requirements

  • Main Easter Egg completed first — skull 5 is impossible without it
  • For skull 5: a Shutter Shades drop + your Weapon of Rock
  1. Skull 1 — pop the balloons. Shoot every balloon in the spawn area; a completion sound plays when you got them all. Spawn plaza, directly across from Up N' Atoms
  2. Skull 2 — spell G-H-O-S-T. Five skulls hide behind letters on signs and must be collected in order. To collect one, aim down sights at it until it disappears. Each letter has two possible signs — completion sound plays after the T: G — the G on the Galaxy Journey sign above Mule Munchies, or the G on the Rover Rampage (bumper cars) sign
    H — the H on the Hyper Slopes sign at the top of the slides, or the H on the Chromosphere sign
    O — the O in the Journey Into Space sign, or the O on the Cosmic Tunes booth outside the Polar Peak gift shop
    S — the S on the Star Mission sign near Tuff 'Nuff, or the S on the Astrocade sign above the coin machine
    T — the T in "Hunter" on the Octonian Hunter sign, or the T on the Moonlight Cafe sign
  3. Skull 3 — dance floor code. The Ghosts 'n Skulls cabinet screen shows 1984 in colors — always green 1, blue 9, pink 8, gray 4. On the Astrocade dance floor, stand on a green square and get exactly 1 kill, then a blue square for exactly 9, then pink for 8, then gray for 4 — in that order. Check the cabinet after: if the 3rd skull lit up, it worked.
  4. Skull 4 — arcade games. Play all five arcade games; a skull appears in each and is collected differently: Rings of Saturn basketball — score while the skull is above the hoop · Cryptid Attack — hit the tooth the skull appears over · Black Hole — shoot the ball into the 50 slot · Zombie Zoom — reach the skull before the other zombies · Bowling for Planets (skee-ball) — score in the hole where the skull appears
  5. Skull 5 — beam the skull over the PaP portal. Put on the Shutter Shades: a small skull floats back and forth above the central portal (like the quest UFO, but smaller). Wait until it's over the center, then shoot the SPACELAND sign nodes with your Weapon of Rock to fire the beam — exactly like the main quest finale — so the beam zaps the skull.
  6. Skull 6 — Brute power. Bait a Brute into firing its laser at the Ghosts 'n Skulls cabinet — that collects the last skull and powers the machine on.
  7. Play the minigame. You're given the Entangler: fire to grab a flying ghost, release to fling it at the skulls. Prioritize skulls that turn red — if 3 skulls escape you're ejected and must redo skull 6 to get back in. Multiple waves, so it takes a while.
  8. Reward: every perk on the map permanently for the rest of the match (solo: Up N' Atoms excluded) + 1,000 XP.

Shared setup — do this firstGold Teeth · croc shard device · Arcane Core · UFO table ›

Each weapon needs 3 parts: a Battery, a Souvenir Toy, and an Elemental Shard. The batteries differ per weapon; toys and shards follow the same pattern.

  1. Open Pack-a-Punch first — the UFO table and shard steps need it.
  2. Buy the Gold Teeth for 300 tickets at the Astrocade prize counter.
  3. Bait a Brute into the Space Croc Chomp so the jaws slam down on him — this smashes the croc's teeth.
  4. Place the Gold Teeth on the croc — a device holding all four elemental shards is revealed in its throat.
  5. Buy an Arcane Core for 300 tickets at any prize cart — it attaches to your current gun (the guide recommends putting it on the Kendall 44).
  6. In the PaP room's back-left corner, interact with the UFO table to send the four mini-UFOs out to the traps.
  7. Shard loop (done per weapon): get 15 kills with that weapon's trap → its UFO changes course → get 25 kills near the UFO using ONLY the Arcane Core gun → the colored orb drops → grab it → shoot that weapon's slot on the croc device → take the shard.
  8. Souvenir coins for the Toy parts:

Opening Pack-a-Punch

Upgrades cost 5,000 points. Tip from the guide: do power and portals together — right after flipping a switch, ride the portal next to it.

  1. Flip all five power switches, one per district:
  2. Activate and ride each of the four district portals (each drops you back at the central junction):
  3. Check the light bar above the big central portal frame at Cosmic Way: yellow bulb = portal not ridden yet, blue = done. All four blue = the main portal opens.
  4. Ride the central portal through the alien tunnel to the Projector Room — the Pack-a-Punch projector is there. While inside, also tap the mini-UFO table when you're ready for the wonder weapon quests.
  5. Note: double Pack-a-Punch is locked until you either complete the main quest (Alien Fuses) or play Director's Cut.

Simon Says tracker

Main quest, after the speakers summon the UFO: its underside flashes a color sequence you must replay on the four speakers — 3 sequences, each longer, all random per match. Tap the pads below as the UFO flashes so you don't have to memorize.

Set your speaker colors first: the colors follow the order YOU placed the speakers — 1st placed = yellow, 2nd = blue, 3rd = green, 4th = red. If you get an input wrong, a Brute spawns and the UFO re-flashes a NEW sequence — hit Clear and tap in the new one.
