Black Ops 4 · "Five" remade — the Pentagon

Requirements

  • Players: 1–4. Normal difficulty, no mutations.
  • Regular frag grenades in your loadout before the game — without them the quest can't be finished.
  • Power on and Pack-a-Punch open, and a Pack-a-Punched gun for the portraits.

The Project Skadi quest — four codes, a keypad, and three rounds in Groom Lake for the Winter's Howl. The easiest quest in Black Ops 4.

The full quest

Tap a step to check it off — progress saves on this device.

  1. Wake the crawlers. Get into the Main Offices by round 1 or 2 and hold use on this door. Wait for the dialogue, hold use again, and keep going until a demonic girl yells at you to leave the man alone — a swarm of Nova crawlers spawns. Knife as many as you can (they count toward the no-explode achievement).
  2. The key. Down in the War Room, beside the Cola machine, a key hangs in a box on the wall. Take it.
  3. Code 1 — the drawer. Back upstairs, open this Main Offices drawer with the key. A picture with a number appears — write it down (it's different every game).
  4. Code 2 — the barricade. In the Labs hallway outside Weapons Testing, a big teleporter sits at the back of one of the barricades. Throw frags over the top of it; a second code picture appears just right of the window.
  5. Code 3 — the Panic Room TV. Hit the DEFCON switches in this order: War Room upper level, the one furthest from the stairs → Server Room, back-left → War Room upper level, nearest the stairs → War Room lower level, right of the elevator. Teleport to the Panic Room: one TV shows static — hold use and it slides back to reveal the code. Grab the teleporter piece while you're in there.
  6. Code 4 — the presidents. With a Pack-a-Punched gun, in the Deserted Hallway on the Offices floor, shoot the four portraits' golden nameplates in this order: Washington (1st from left) → Franklin (3rd) → Hamilton (2nd) → Lincoln (4th). Washington's nameplate slides aside — the last code.
  7. The keypad. War Room lower level, by the Server Room door, one terminal has a four-number keypad. Shoot each digit until it shows your number, confirm, and enter the four codes in this order: Portraits → Drawer → Barricade → Panic Room TV. "Project Skadi Prototype acquired."
  8. Three rounds in Groom Lake. Full ammo, shield repaired. Leave one zombie at the end of a round, teleport to Pack-a-Punch (Groom Lake) — it respawns with you; kill it and survive three full rounds without leaving. Keep moving; standing still gets you killed here. The boxes either side of the machine lift: a Bonfire Sale and the briefcase with the Winter's Howl. You give up a weapon to take it.

Side Easter Eggs & achievements

The reference is an achievement walkthrough — these are its map achievements. The round-150 egg, the "Shockwave" song and the Richtofen jump scare aren't covered; send a reference and I'll add them.

Round 20 with no power / no elevator
Do both in one game

Camp in the hallway elevator without pressing its button, and leave the door straight into that hallway closed — come round through Central Filing and the Main Offices instead. Hit the box early for a Hades or Zweihander; specialist for emergencies.

50 kills inside Nova 6 gas
Sit in the Labs

Crawlers spawn most in the Labs; Porcine Research (hanging pig, Tonic) is the only half-decent spot, watch the windows behind you. Kill crawlers with anything except a knife, Ray Gun or Winter's Howl so they burst into gas, then stand in it and kill. The gas fades, so keep popping crawlers.

Teleport while downed, then get revived
Much easier with a partner

Power on. Keep one zombie; down yourself in front of the War Room floor teleporter with a friend waiting on another floor. Crawl in when the symbol shows their floor, get revived.

Seven teleports in under a minute
Panic Room ping-pong

Power on, all doors open, DEFCON 5 set. Teleport into the Panic Room, wait a second, use the teleporter behind you to come back out, repeat.

115 electric-trap kills
Build both traps first

Traps live in the two long Offices hallways once built; each has a cooldown, so it takes several uses.

Five rounds in the War Room elevator
The Labs–War Room lift

Set up first: full shield, PaP'd guns, full specialist. Once you start you can't step out — no drops outside, no shield repairs, let the last dog into the lift for its Max Ammo. You can ride between floors, but kill whatever's already inside first. Zombies come through the doors and the lift's own window.

Shatter 115 / 50 crawlers without exploding
Both with the Winter's Fury

Pack-a-Punch the Winter's Howl into the Winter's Fury straight after the quest. Freeze zombies, then hit them with anything else to shatter — 115 of them. The Fury also kills crawlers without letting them explode; the knifed ones from the quest's first step count too.

Wonder Weapons

One wonder weapon, earned by the quest.

Winter's Howl
Project Skadi — the quest reward

Comes out of the briefcase after the three Groom Lake rounds.

Pack-a-Punched it's the Winter's Fury. Frozen zombies shatter when you damage them with anything else, and it kills Nova crawlers without setting off their gas.

Pack-a-Punch

Power, DEFCON 5, the Panic Room and the Signal Amplifier — then the War Room teleporter takes you to Groom Lake.

  1. Power — lower level, in the Labs, the room nearest the Soda perk.
  2. Build the Signal Amplifier.
  3. DEFCON 5. With power on, hold use on the four DEFCON switches: two on the War Room upper balcony ("Hold to adjust DEF-CON level"), one down the left stairs from them on the wall, one on the Server Room back-left wall. The floor teleporter turns into a head symbol and the announcer calls DEFCON 5.
  4. Place the Amplifier at that teleporter — walk up to it without entering — then hit all four switches again and jump in. Pack-a-Punch is in Groom Lake.

Getting around

Elevators work without power but only link neighbouring floors (Offices–War Room, War Room–Labs). Teleporters need power, go to any floor, and show where they'll send you:

SymbolDestination
Desk chairOffices (top floor, spawn)
Computer terminalsWar Room / Nerve Center (middle)
Test tubeLabs (basement)

Walk in while it glows blue; red means it's cooling down.

Perk machines
